IoT questions come in four kinds.

1. **Why is a location or device missing from the report, and what does the report mean?** [[zia.iot.discovery]].
2. **What does a type, category, classification, UUID or Unknown mean?** [[zia.iot.classification]].
3. **Can a rule target IoT devices, and what must a location enable first?** [[zia.iot.policy-use]].
4. **How do I pull the data programmatically?** [[zia.iot.api-surface]].

The predefined IoT Traffic Group is in [[zia.locations.groups]] and the Cloud App Control IoT predefined rules are in [[zia.cloud-app-control.rule-types]]; they are linked here, not repeated.
