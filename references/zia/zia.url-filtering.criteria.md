Within one rule, criteria combine by a fixed expression. {{operator-expression}} Mostly AND, with three OR groups: identity (configurable OR or AND), location, and device.

{{unset-ignored}} This is why a broad-looking rule can catch far more than intended: every criterion left empty widens it.

The second URL Categories field is the common surprise. {{url-categories2-and}} Operators who add a second field expecting "either list" get a rule that almost never matches.

Not every criterion is visible in the console. {{api-only-scoping}} {{user-risk-score-levels}}

Some criteria depend on what ZIA can see. {{payload-criteria-need-inspection}} See [[zia.ssl-inspection.pipeline]] for when the full request is visible.
