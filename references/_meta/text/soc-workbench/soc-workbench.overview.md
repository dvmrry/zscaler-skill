# SOC Workbench (Agentic SOC): alert unification, correlation and prioritization, and what can be automated (soc-workbench.overview)
Also called: soc workbench; agentic soc; zscaler agentic soc; soc workbench api; soc workbench report export; ai response agent; alert correlation zscaler

The help article previously at the SOC Workbench address (/soc-workbench/what-zscaler-soc-workbench) is titled What Is Zscaler Agentic SOC? and now lives under /agentic-soc with its sibling article, the old address redirecting to it; current help calls the product Agentic SOC. [soc-workbench.overview:renamed] Agentic SOC is a unified platform that uses a multi-agentic AI approach to prioritize and triage threats, with risk-based prioritization that weighs a threat's business context. [soc-workbench.overview:what-it-is]

**What it does with alerts.** It tracks Zscaler alerts and alerts from third-party applications (for example Gmail and CrowdStrike) in a single console, with visibility across assets, identities and exposures. [soc-workbench.overview:unify-alerts] It is powered by the Zscaler Data Fabric for Security, and its integration with the Zero Trust Exchange adds threat context and initiates inline controls to mitigate risk automatically. [soc-workbench.overview:data-fabric-and-zte] As part of the Agentic SecOps Platform portfolio, it connects exposure insights with threat prioritization, so vulnerable assets and identities are factored into weighted results. [soc-workbench.overview:exposure-context]

**Day to day.** It pulls first-party data from Zscaler products and integrates with third-party solutions, enriches each alert with identity, asset, location and threat-intelligence metadata at the fabric layer, groups alerts into incidents, and scores incidents by risk. [soc-workbench.overview:workflow] Its AI-driven threat analysis turns raw alerts into contextualized threats and uses historical attack patterns to identify the first 5 things to address. [soc-workbench.overview:first-five] It accelerates investigations by providing all threat details on a single page, along with log search from one unified location and the asset impact on the same page. [soc-workbench.overview:investigation] The AI Response Agent takes triaged incidents and provides targeted remediation recommendations. [soc-workbench.overview:response-agent]

**Console pages.** Incidents, their verdicts and statuses are in [Incidents in Agentic SOC: the Incidents page, detail tabs, verdicts, statuses, custom incidents and customization](kb:soc-workbench.incidents), the rules that group alerts into incidents and the dispatch workflows in [Incident rules that group alerts into incidents, and incident workflows, in Agentic SOC](kb:soc-workbench.incident-rules), alerts and severity scoring in [Alerts in Agentic SOC: the Alerts page, severity score composition, alert-score factors and severity categories](kb:soc-workbench.alerts), and assets in [Assets in Agentic SOC: merged assets, Risk Score and Risk Mass, columns, filters and charts](kb:soc-workbench.assets). The SOC Workbench Dashboard shows alert and incident information with trends for the previous 30 days, the mean time to respond for incidents, the top 5 devices affected by incidents, the top 5 high-risk incidents, the top 5 alerts generated by applications versus the number of actual incidents, and where Agentic SOC places the organization's alerts on the MITRE ATT&CK tactics; you set the date range for its data. [soc-workbench.overview:dashboard]

**Agentic SOC page.** The Agentic SOC page is the starting point in the Agentic SecOps Platform Admin Portal and shows which sources produce alerts, the total alerts, the incidents created from alerts and the triaged incidents; its data range is set by a date range. [soc-workbench.overview:landing-page] Agentic SOC uses multiple AI agents (for example enrichment, correlation and triage agents) to enrich alerts, correlate and aggregate them into incidents, and triage the incidents to assign a verdict and criticality. [soc-workbench.overview:ai-agent-roles] [divergence:soc-workbench.overview:triage-input] The page's alert total counts alerts from all sources enriched by the enrichment agents, its incident total counts incidents correlated by the correlation agents across factors such as users, assets, artifacts and Indicators of Compromise, and alert reduction is the alerts that became actual incidents. [soc-workbench.overview:page-totals] Incidents triaged by the Agentic SOC triage agents carry verdicts, reasoning, contradicting evidence and analysis of discrepancies, and the page's interactive Sankey chart shows how incidents affect the organization, with each area clickable for details. [soc-workbench.overview:triage-sankey] Defaults and values are open [gap:soc-workbench.overview:page-defaults]; incident grouping rules are in [Incident rules that group alerts into incidents, and incident workflows, in Agentic SOC](kb:soc-workbench.incident-rules).

**Agents.** The Agents page is a dashboard that tracks the activity and performance of the AI agents in Agentic SOC; its time range filter applies to the performance metrics and per-agent investigation counts and the default time range is 7 days. [soc-workbench.overview:agents-page] Agents are grouped into the categories Correlation, Data Processing, Email Security, Enrichment, Investigation, Remediation, Response, Security, Triage, User Behavioral Analysis and Utility. [soc-workbench.overview:agent-categories] The Agents page reports Alerts Investigated (alerts processed in the time range), FTE Multiplier (time saved divided by the number of days times 8 hours in the range), Time Saved (hours saved on manual work) and Sub-5 Min Resolution (the percentage of alerts resolved under 5 minutes). [soc-workbench.overview:agent-metrics] Clicking an agent card shows the agent's job description and a filtered list of all alerts it has impacted, and you can customize, download and share that Alerts table or open the list on the Alerts page. [soc-workbench.overview:agent-detail] Per-agent details are open [gap:soc-workbench.overview:agent-details].

**Sources.** Which third-party tools can feed it is not captured: [Which source connectors and outbound integrations the SecOps platform offers](kb:portfolio.secops-platform:connector-catalog).

## Automation

No vendored client covers SOC Workbench or Agentic SOC: the Go and Python SDKs, the Terraform providers, the Ansible collections, the MCP server, the OneAPI Postman collection and the captured Automate contract have no such service. [soc-workbench.overview:no-client-surface] Its reports are exported through the platform's Reporting API, [A saved report can be exported programmatically through the Reporting API, a GraphQL-based interface that exports the data to an AWS S3 bucket or returns a downloadable link; the client authenticates for an access token, submits a request to start the report and polls for its status.](kb:portfolio.secops-platform:reporting-api), whose contract is open: [The SecOps Reporting API's endpoint, token request, GraphQL schema and response shape](kb:portfolio.secops-platform:reporting-api-contract).

What to check:
1. Treat SOC Workbench and Agentic SOC as one product when matching help pages, console menus (SOC Workbench > My Reports) and questions.
2. For exporting incidents programmatically, use a saved report and the SecOps Reporting API; no client exposes SOC Workbench objects.
