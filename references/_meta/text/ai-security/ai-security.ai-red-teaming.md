# AI Red Teaming: adversarial testing of AI apps, the Overview page, and where each part is documented (ai-security.ai-red-teaming)
Also called: ai red teaming; red teaming overview; ai red teaming overview page; tested ai assets; untested ai assets; red teaming health score; airt

AI Red Teaming tests a conversational generative AI application, such as an internal chatbot or a public-facing customer engagement assistant, with AI-generated attack scenarios that a selected set of probes runs; connecting the asset is the first onboarding step. [ai-security.ai-red-teaming:what-it-tests] This subject covers the Overview page and routes to its children for each part.

**Order of work.** A first test run needs the AI app connected to AI Security and at least one probe selected and configured; a new test run's prerequisites are a connected app and configured probes. [ai-security.ai-red-teaming:sequence] Connecting and describing the app is in [Connecting an AI app for red teaming: network type, private access, and the application profile that shapes tests](kb:ai-security.ai-red-teaming.assets) and [Red Teaming connection types and the fields each needs (REST API, Proxy SDK, OpenAI REST API, Copilot Studio, Glean, Microsoft Teams, Agentforce, Amazon Bedrock AgentCore and Agents), and the connection test](kb:ai-security.ai-red-teaming.connections); a proxy for a non-standard app is in [The AI Red Teaming Onboarding Agent: generating a Proxy SDK proxy for a non-standard app, its spec gate, versions and local skill](kb:ai-security.ai-red-teaming.onboarding-agent); private apps are reached through [The AI Red Teaming broker: outbound WSS tunnel to private apps, provisioning, run manifest, allow-lists and troubleshooting codes](kb:ai-security.ai-red-teaming.brokers); probes are in [Red Teaming probes: categories, custom probes and datasets, risk priority, coverage and attack strategies](kb:ai-security.ai-red-teaming.probes) and runs in [Red Teaming test runs: units, new runs, scheduling and history](kb:ai-security.ai-red-teaming.test-runs).

**Overview page.** The AI Red Teaming Overview page (AI Infrastructure > AI Red Teaming > Overview) is the central place to configure, run and review adversarial testing; it filters by Business Unit and shows Usage Insights (test runs executed in the last seven days, and the ratio of red-teamed to onboarded AI assets), tested AI assets by health score (Critical, Low, Medium or High), and AI assets by availability (Customer-Facing or Internal). [ai-security.ai-red-teaming:overview-page] The Tested AI Assets tab lists each asset's name and business unit, asset type, latest test run timestamp, health score (a score of the asset's security posture from test results, where higher usually indicates better posture), environment, availability (internal or customer-facing), supported modes and whether its prompt is hardened; Untested AI Assets is a separate tab. [ai-security.ai-red-teaming:tested-assets-columns] What the Health Score measures against the Overall Risk Score that probe priorities raise is a recorded disagreement in [Red Teaming probes: categories, custom probes and datasets, risk priority, coverage and attack strategies](kb:ai-security.ai-red-teaming.probes). What Hardened Prompt means is open [gap:ai-security.ai-red-teaming:hardened-prompt].

**Roles and API.** The roles that run tests are open [gap:ai-security.ai-red-teaming:roles]. The Red Teaming API is unchecked beyond the family's operation count [gap:ai-security.ai-red-teaming:api-unchecked]; [The AI Security family: AI Access Security, AI Guard (AI Guardrails), AI Red Teaming and the AI asset inventory](kb:ai-security.family) holds that count.
