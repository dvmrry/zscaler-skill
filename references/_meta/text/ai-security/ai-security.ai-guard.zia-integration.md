# Sending ZIA users' AI traffic to AI Guard by proxy chaining: prerequisites, proxy, QUIC rule and forwarding (ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration)
Also called: ai guard zia integration; integrating zia with ai guard; proxy chain to ai guard; forward.prod.zsaiguard.net; ai guard proxy gateway; ai guard x-authenticated-user; ai guard quic; ai guard supported applications; ai guard for users; ai guard users not showing; AllUsersGroup; ai guard not catching anything; ai guard inline not detecting

AI Guard for Users inspects people's traffic to public AI apps by having ZIA forward that traffic to AI Guard as a chained proxy. This subject holds the proxy-chaining article's configuration and stated conditions, the AI Guard for Users architecture page, and the ChatGPT and Microsoft 365 Copilot quick starts.

**Before starting.** ZIA can be configured to invoke AI Guard for processing users' AI traffic. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:invoke-from-zia] Prerequisites are an AI Guard subscription, ZIA and AI Guard linked by Zscaler Support, Experience Center enabled for the tenant (the ZIA steps assume it), the CA certificate of the AI Guard endpoint, and a supported generative AI application. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:prerequisites] AI Guard can be mapped to only a single tenant and a single domain: a domain present in both a QA and a production tenant can be mapped to one of them, not both. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:single-tenant-domain] The supported-application table (last updated Sept. 02, 2026) gives, per application, the supported client types and the domains to forward; for example Google Gemini Workspaces supports Ask Gemini and Generate document in Docs, Slides and Sheets but not Help me write or Refine, at `appsgenaiserver-pa.clients6.google.com`. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:supported-apps-table] Windsurf is supported only on its desktop app in Proxy mode, and custom block messages are not supported for it. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:windsurf-proxy-only]

**Configuration in ZIA.**
1. The AI Guard CA certificate (a .pem file) is uploaded in ZIA as a root certificate of type Proxy Chaining. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:root-cert] The AI Guard proxy chain root certificate can be downloaded from AI Guard's Tenant Settings for upload into ZIA, which creates the trust between ZIA and AI Guard. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:cert-download]
2. The ZIA proxy points to `forward.prod.zsaiguard.net` on port 9443 with the AI Guard root certificate, Insert X-Authenticated-User enabled, and Base64 encoding of the X-Authenticated-User value disabled. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:proxy-settings]
3. The proxy gateway keeps Fail Close selected, uses the AI Guard proxy as its primary proxy and leaves the secondary proxy blank. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:gateway-fail-close]
4. A Firewall Filtering rule on the QUIC network service with action Block/ICMP drops QUIC; the proxy-chaining article creates it in the Experience Center under Zero Trust Cloud > Policy > Firewall Filtering, while the ChatGPT quick start gives the Zscaler Admin Console path Policies > Access Control > Firewall > Firewall Filtering Policy; for ZIA Tunnel 1.0 the article says to disable the QUIC protocol in the browser. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:quic-block]
5. A Wildcard FQDN destination group holds the required domains for the chosen AI providers, which the Prerequisites section's supported-applications table lists, and a Forwarding Control rule on that group uses Proxy Chaining to the AI Guard proxy gateway. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:forwarding-rule] Forwarding Control itself is in [ZIA Forwarding Control and Source IP Anchoring](kb:zia.forwarding-control).

**Conditions for it to work.** Forwarding works only for users signed in to ZIA, for example through Zscaler Client Connector, and authenticated by the organization's IdP. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:sign-in-required] Once ZIA is configured, users defined in Authentication Service appear in AI Guard automatically, but groups are not yet populated automatically; Policy Match policies can target users and groups, and the default group is `AllUsersGroup`. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:users-appear] Whether to enable user and group sync first is disputed [divergence:ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:sync-enable]. How users and groups sync in AI Guard is in [Policies by user or user group need ZIA synchronization of users, groups and domains, with the ZIA tenant already linked to AI Guard; sync is enabled by toggles under ZIA Information, Start Sync under Advanced Actions syncs immediately outside the scheduled batch window, and synced users and groups appear on the AI Users page.](kb:ai-security.ai-guard.tenant-settings:zia-sync).

**Users architecture.** AI Guard for Users provides run-time protection for GenAI applications on a per-user or user-group basis by enforcing enterprise policies, using intent-based detectors on both prompts and responses. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:users-purpose] In the AI Guard for Users architecture, Zscaler Client Connector authenticated users connect to ZIA, which forwards traffic to the public GenAI apps by proxy chaining to the AI Guard infrastructure within the Zscaler cloud, with a parenthetical that traditional Cloud App Controls, Web DLP and Threat Protection "still occur here". [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:users-flow-request] In the AI Guard for Users architecture, if an AI Guard policy blocks the prompt, the traffic does not go to the GenAI apps; a prompt that is not blocked is proxied by AI Guard to the GenAI apps, and the response is analyzed with policy response detectors if configured, the user receiving a blocked message from AI Guard if it is blocked. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:users-flow-block] In the AI Guard for Users architecture the response is sent back through ZIA, which returns it to the user. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:users-flow-return] What the page leaves out is open [gap:ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:users-architecture-order].

**Quick starts.** The ChatGPT and Microsoft 365 Copilot quick starts enable Store Prompts/Responses under Tenant Settings > Security (otherwise all Prompt/Response fields show No Data), enable ZIA User and Group Sync and ZIA Domain Sync under Tenant Settings > Integrations, and download the AI Guard Proxy Chain Certificate. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:quickstart-tenant-prep] In the quick starts and the proxy-chaining article, the Description of the third-party proxy being defined cannot exceed 256 characters, and the Description of the destination IPv4 group cannot exceed 10240 characters. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:quickstart-description-limits] The ChatGPT quick start's Wildcard FQDN destination IPv4 group holds .chatgpt.com and .chat.openai.com, and a Forwarding Control rule on that group uses the Proxy Chaining forwarding method to the proxy gateway. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:quickstart-chatgpt-domains] The Microsoft 365 Copilot quick start's Wildcard FQDN destination IPv4 group holds .substrate.office.com, .substrate.svc.cloud.microsoft, .copilot.microsoft.com and the .api.business, .api.enterprise and .api.individual githubcopilot.com domains, forwarded with the Proxy Chaining method. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:quickstart-m365-domains] The domain list for Microsoft 365 Copilot differs between the quick start and the supported-applications table [divergence:ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:m365-domain-list]. What the quick starts leave out is open [gap:ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:quickstart-domain-limits].

**Claude apps through Client Connector.** The Claude integration runbook says AI Guard by default supports all the Claude tools it lists when using Claude version 2.1.101 or later, and that a Claude Code CLI version earlier than 2.1.101 needs its Enabling Claude Code CLI and Code for VS Code section. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:claude-version] The Claude runbook's prerequisites are Zscaler Client Connector installed and logged in, the Zscaler Root CA installed in the Trusted Root Certification Authority store on the device, all Claude applications, tools, extensions, plugins or add-ons installed, and SSL Inspection configured in ZIA for the domains .api.anthropic.com and .claude.ai. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:claude-prereqs] The runbook's table marks Claude Code Desktop, Claude Cowork (in Claude Code Desktop), Claude Code CLI, the Claude Code Visual Studio (VS) Code Extension, and the Claude Extension for Chrome, Excel and Powerpoint with a green checkmark in each of the Works by Default, Windows and macOS columns; the runbook says AI Guard by default supports all the listed tools when using Claude version 2.1.101 or later, and that an app with the checkmark in Works by Default needs no additional steps so long as the prerequisites are followed. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:claude-tools] For a Claude version earlier than 2.1.101, the Claude runbook enables Claude Code CLI and Code for VS Code by exporting the Zscaler Root CA and setting the NODE_EXTRA_CA_CERTS environment variable to it: on Windows with the Base-64 encoded X.509 (.CER) format and setx; on macOS in PEM format, since Node.js-based tools such as Claude CLI need a PEM-formatted certificate, with an export line in ~/.zshrc (the default on modern macOS) or ~/.bash_profile. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:claude-node-ca] The Claude runbook's check for Claude Code CLI is to enter a prompt: if it is working you receive a response instead of an SSL certificate error, and the traffic appears in Zscaler. [ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:claude-verify]

**Not stated by the article.** Whether SSL inspection settings in ZIA matter on this path is open [gap:ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:ssl-inspection], as is what traffic escapes inspection without the QUIC rule or with the gateway set to fail open [gap:ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:quic-bypass]. Whether traffic from GRE or IPSec tunnel locations reaches AI Guard on this path is open [gap:ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:tunnel-locations].

What to check:
1. When AI Guard for Users seems to catch nothing, whether the traffic reaches AI Guard at all (a transaction for the user on the dashboard) before suspecting a detector: the ZIA side below decides that, and whether ZIA SSL inspection or bypass settings for the AI domains matter on this path is open.
2. That the user is signed in to ZIA (for example through Client Connector) and authenticated by the IdP; forwarding works only then.
3. That the application and client type are in the supported table and the destination domain is in the wildcard FQDN group that the forwarding rule sends to the AI Guard gateway.
4. That the ZIA proxy is forward.prod.zsaiguard.net on port 9443 with Insert X-Authenticated-User on and Base64 encoding off, and the gateway is fail-close.
5. That QUIC is blocked by a firewall rule, or disabled in the browser for ZIA Tunnel 1.0.

Sources disagree (name both sides):
- The Microsoft 365 Copilot quick start forwards six domains; the supported-applications table lists nine for the same application. [divergence:ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:m365-domain-list]
  - zscaler-help: The quick start's wildcard FQDN group holds six domains: .substrate.office.com, .substrate.svc.cloud.microsoft, .copilot.microsoft.com and the three githubcopilot.com api domains.
  - zscaler-help: The supported-applications table's Microsoft 365 Copilot row also lists augloop.office.com, www.copilot.com and .cloud.microsoft.
  Impact: The forwarding-rule step sends readers to the table for the required domains, so the quick start's six alone may leave some Microsoft 365 Copilot traffic unforwarded; the pages do not say which list is current.
- The quick starts have you enable ZIA User and Group Sync; the proxy-chaining article says users appear automatically and groups are not populated. [divergence:ai-security.ai-guard.zia-integration:sync-enable]
  - zscaler-help: The quick starts enable ZIA User and Group Sync and ZIA Domain Sync under Tenant Settings > Integrations before configuring ZIA.
  - zscaler-help: The proxy-chaining article says users defined in Authentication Service appear automatically after ZIA is configured, and groups are not yet populated automatically.
  Impact: Whether the toggles are needed for users to appear, or only for groups and domains, is not established; [[ai-security.ai-guard.tenant-settings:zia-sync]] says sync is needed for policies by user or group.
