# AI Guard's detectors: the capabilities Help lists and the detectors the policy editor configures (ai-security.ai-guard.detectors)
Also called: ai guard detectors; ai guard detector list; prompt injection detection; jailbreak detection; toxicity detector; refusal detection; competitor detection; finance advice detector; legal advice detector; url reachability; ai guard pii detector; how many detectors does ai guard have; how does zscaler protect against prompt injection; ai guard custom detectors
Former IDs: ai-security-03

Two sources describe AI Guard's detectors: the product's What Is page lists capabilities, and the policy editor names the detectors with their own settings. They are not the same list.

**The capability list.** The What Is AI Guard page lists fifteen capabilities, introduced as some of the options available: Visibility & Access Control; Prompt Injection & Jailbreak Protection; Toxicity; Sensitive Data Protection; Off-Topic Response Detection; Malicious URL Detection & Blocking; Language Detection & Enforcement; Code Injection & Execution Detection; Gibberish & Low-Quality Prompt/Response Filtering; Refusal Detection & Intervention; Finance Advice; Prompt Tagging-based Access Control; Competitor Discussion Detection; URL Reachability Detection; and Legal Advice. [ai-security.ai-guard.detectors:capability-list] Under Sensitive Data Protection, AI Guard enables inline inspection and classification to detect and block sensitive data before it is sent to AI APIs. [ai-security.ai-guard.detectors:sensitive-data]

**Notable detectors.** Refusal Detection flags and intervenes when the AI refuses valid queries, alerting the security team because the refusals might be a denial-of-service attack. [ai-security.ai-guard.detectors:refusal-dos] Under Finance Advice, AI Guard can block actionable financial guidance (investing, trading, tax, product choices) while allowing neutral finance facts, history and definitions; Legal Advice blocks prompts seeking legal advice, interpretation or compliance guidance while allowing neutral legal facts, definitions and non-legal work inquiries. [ai-security.ai-guard.detectors:advice-detectors] Competitor Discussion Detection identifies and blocks prompts that reference competitors, rival products, pricing comparisons or market strategies. [ai-security.ai-guard.detectors:competitor] URL Reachability Detection checks whether URLs in prompts are accessible, safe or broken, to prevent hallucinated links. [ai-security.ai-guard.detectors:url-reachability]

**What the policy editor configures.** The policy editor names these prompt detectors with extra settings: Code (programming languages), Text (named regular-expression patterns), Competition (competitor names), Language (languages), Secrets (secret types such as API keys or tokens), Off Topic (topics to treat as off topic), PII (types such as Person's name and Location), Topic (custom topics, each with a name and topic definition), Prompt Tags (categories such as News and Media), and Intellectual Property (a sensitive context to keep from leaking). Configuring response detectors follows similar steps. [ai-security.ai-guard.detectors:configurable-detectors] Not every prompt detector has a threshold setting: Text and Off Topic do not include it because they activate on specific keywords, phrases or topics, and Text takes regular-expression patterns. [ai-security.ai-guard.detectors:keyword-detectors] The Topic runbook disagrees about whether Off Topic has a threshold and whether it matches by keyword [divergence:ai-security.ai-guard.detectors:off-topic-threshold]. Settings shared by all detectors, and how a policy is applied, are in [AI Guard policies: detector settings, policy control rules, first-match order, applications and testing](kb:ai-security.ai-guard.policies).

**Per-detector runbooks.** The PII detector is in [The AI Guard PII detector: categories, what it catches and misses, masking, entities and threshold](kb:ai-security.ai-guard.detectors.pii), the Intellectual Property detector in [The AI Guard Intellectual Property detector: reproduction of a confidential context, what it ignores, sensitive context and threshold](kb:ai-security.ai-guard.detectors.intellectual-property), the Competition detector in [The AI Guard Competition detector: the evidence rule, competitor list, tickers, languages, threshold and the scoreByCompetitor log field](kb:ai-security.ai-guard.detectors.competition), and the Topic and Off Topic detectors in [The AI Guard Topic and Off Topic detectors: OR and AND triggering, topic definitions, limits, threshold and languages](kb:ai-security.ai-guard.detectors.topic).

**Languages.** The Multilingual Support page says AI Guard currently supports English, French, German, Japanese, Portuguese and Spanish, with more being added; as more prompt and response detector categories are added they may not support all languages, and a Multilingual badge on a detector's tile marks the categories that support all languages, with a hover that shows which languages that detector supports. [ai-security.ai-guard.detectors:languages] The detector runbooks give a different count [divergence:ai-security.ai-guard.detectors:supported-languages]. The runbook claims that state 28 languages are [The runbook says the detector ships with a built-in ticker map covering the largest tech and finance tickers and tries fuzzy matching for others, with less reliable matches; it answers yes that 28 languages are supported out of the box, says capitalization evidence is English-only (in other languages the detector relies on business context alone), and says prompts under about 10 characters do not give the model enough context to classify reliably.](kb:ai-security.ai-guard.detectors.competition:languages-tickers), [The runbook says to define the topic in English and that the detector handles 28 languages natively, listing Arabic, Chinese, French, German, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Thai, Turkish and Vietnamese, and more, out of the box; prompts under about 10 characters do not give the model enough context.](kb:ai-security.ai-guard.detectors.topic:languages) and [The IP runbook answers yes, 28 languages are supported, says the bi-encoder is multilingual, and says the model is retrained periodically as new false-positive patterns surface, with updates rolled out transparently and no customer action needed.](kb:ai-security.ai-guard.detectors.intellectual-property:model).

**Counts.** Zscaler's Claude Code skill example says AI Guard provides 19 prompt detectors and 21 response detectors, listing prompt detectors under direction IN and response detectors under direction OUT; its reference file calls itself the complete reference of the detectors available in AI Guard policies. [ai-security.ai-guard.detectors:integration-skill-list] Which list is complete, and the per-direction count, are still open [gap:ai-security.ai-guard.detectors:taxonomy-source]. Whether custom detectors can be written is open [gap:ai-security.ai-guard.detectors:custom-detectors]. What to check:
1. Name detector categories from the Help list or the policy editor rather than quoting a total; the Help list calls itself some of the options.
2. For a keyword or regex need, the Text detector (regex patterns); for Off Topic (listed topics), whether it has a threshold and matches by keyword is disputed, so read the divergence rather than assume either.
3. Before stating how many languages AI Guard supports, which list applies: the Multilingual Support page's six or the detector runbooks' 28.

Sources disagree (name both sides):
- How many languages AI Guard supports: the Multilingual Support page lists six, while the detector runbooks say 28. [divergence:ai-security.ai-guard.detectors:supported-languages]
  - zscaler-help: The Multilingual Support page lists six languages: English, French, German, Japanese, Portuguese and Spanish.
  - zscaler-help: The Competition runbook says 28 languages are supported out of the box.
  - zscaler-help: The Topic and Off Topic runbook says 28 languages are supported out of the box, including Arabic, Chinese, Hindi, Korean, Russian, Thai, Turkish and Vietnamese, which the Multilingual page does not list.
  - zscaler-help: The IP runbook says reproduction of confidential English content in French, German or any of 28 supported languages is detected.
  Impact: The Multilingual page allows per-detector differences, since newer detector categories may not support every language, but it does not reconcile six with 28; an answer on a language outside the six should name which page it rests on and that the other disagrees.
- Whether Off Topic has a threshold and how it matches: the policy-editor page says it has no threshold option and describes activation by specific keywords, phrases or topics, while the Topic runbook gives the pair a threshold and semantic matching rather than exact-substring keywords. [divergence:ai-security.ai-guard.detectors:off-topic-threshold]
  - zscaler-help: The policy-editor page says some prompt detectors such as Text and Off Topic do not include the threshold option because they activate with specific keywords, phrases or topics.
  - zscaler-help: The Topic and Off Topic runbook gives the pair a threshold with a default of 0.7, says both detectors use the same underlying model and topic definitions, and says the detector does not match keywords by exact substring but uses semantic understanding.
  Impact: The keyword-detectors claim states what the policy-editor page says and stands as a statement of that page; whether the console shows a threshold on the Off Topic tile is not settled, so do not tell an administrator Off Topic has no threshold or that it matches only literal keywords.
