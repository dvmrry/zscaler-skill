# Breach Predictor AI Assist dashboard and its AI agents (breach-predictor.ai-assist)
Also called: breach ai agents

The AI Assist dashboard is powered by multiple Breach Predictor AI agents and draws on log sources, peer best practices, current policy settings, third-party integrations and ThreatLabz threat data. [breach-predictor.ai-assist:inputs] The AI Assist feature's access is to be asked of the Zscaler Account team, and AI Assist can be opened from any screen in the portal. [breach-predictor.ai-assist:access] The June 30, 2025 release summary lists the AI Assist Dashboard as a Feature in Limited Availability. [breach-predictor.ai-assist:availability]

## Agents and prompts

The AI Assist dashboard lists five agents: Detection (detects threats), Context (provides additional details about specific threats), Triage (gives an initial verdict and confidence level for each threat), Response (responds to each threat and provides next steps) and Third-Party Integrations (monitors the integrated third-party applications). [breach-predictor.ai-assist:agents] Each AI Assist page offers suggested prompts that change dynamically with the current screen and the primary agent engaged, or a text box for own questions; thumbs up and thumbs down feedback is available. [breach-predictor.ai-assist:prompts] On the AI Assist dashboard, the Top Response Actions tile gives a prioritized list of recommended policy updates from the Response Agent. [breach-predictor.ai-assist:top-response]

## On the Findings attack path

On the Attack path tab, clicking AI Assist gives generative AI information about the threat; the threat summary has general ThreatLabz information and Breach Predictor's analysis of the threats at each attack stage, including the log source.
