# Dated end-of-sale and end-of-life notices for Analyzer, Shift, Office365Mon, CIEM, SecureAgent and ZAB hardware (portfolio.usage-terms.product-notices)
Also called: end of sale notice; eos eol notice; zscaler analyzer eol; zscaler shift eol; office365mon eol; ciem eol; secureagent eol; zab hardware eol

Each notice below is what it says on its announcement date; none states the product's current status [gap:portfolio.usage-terms.product-notices:current-status].

**Digital experience and routing.** The notice dated December 5, 2023 gives Zscaler Analyzer an EOS date of February 28, 2024 and an EOL date of February 28, 2025, and says to plan a migration to Digital Experience (ZDX). [portfolio.usage-terms.product-notices:analyzer] The notice dated August 1, 2022 gives Zscaler Shift (Zscaler Intelligent Routing for Guest Wi-Fi Protection) an EOS date of November 1, 2022 and an EOL date of November 1, 2024, and suggests a migration plan to ZIA. [portfolio.usage-terms.product-notices:shift]

**Microsoft 365 and cloud identity.** The notice dated July 20, 2022 gives Zscaler Office365Mon an EOS date of October 20, 2022 and an EOL date of October 20, 2023, and suggests migration to ZDX for Microsoft 365. [portfolio.usage-terms.product-notices:office365mon] The notice dated July 18, 2022 gives Zscaler Cloud Identity Entitlement Management (CIEM) an EOS date of October 18, 2022 and an EOL date of October 18, 2023, and says ZPC is available to CIEM customers at no extra charge for the remainder of the subscription period. [portfolio.usage-terms.product-notices:ciem] ZPC has its own EOS and EOL notice in [Zscaler Posture Control (ZPC) and Cloud Protection: what ZPC covered, and its end of life](kb:portfolio.cloud-protection).

**Mobile agents and hardware.** The notice dated July 20, 2017 gives Zscaler SecureAgent for iOS an EOS date of March 1, 2017 and an EOL date of November 30, 2017, and requires migration to Client Connector for iOS for deployments using IPSec tunnels. [portfolio.usage-terms.product-notices:secureagent-ios] The notice dated August 6, 2018 gives Zscaler SecureAgent for Android an EOS date of August 1, 2018 and an EOL date of March 1, 2019, and requires migration to Client Connector for Android by March 1, 2019. [portfolio.usage-terms.product-notices:secureagent-android] It is dated after its own EOS date [divergence:portfolio.usage-terms.product-notices:secureagent-android-notice-period]. The notice dated December 20, 2017 says Zscaler would no longer support Zscaler Authentication Bridge (ZAB) hardware as of March 1, 2019, with support permanently terminated after March 31, 2019, and that ZAB functionality is available as software. [portfolio.usage-terms.product-notices:zab-hardware]

Sources disagree (name both sides):
- The EOS and EOL Policy, effective August 1st, 2017, requires Zscaler notification at least 60 days before EOS for Software Products, while the SecureAgent for Android notice has announcement date August 6, 2018 and EOS date August 1, 2018. [divergence:portfolio.usage-terms.product-notices:secureagent-android-notice-period]
  - zscaler-help: Zscaler notifies at least 60 days before EOS for Software Products.
  - zscaler-help: The SecureAgent for Android notice was announced August 6, 2018 for an EOS date of August 1, 2018.
  Impact: Neither page says whether the policy applied to SecureAgent or whether earlier notice was given elsewhere, so the notice dates cannot be read as proof the 60-day period was met or waived.
