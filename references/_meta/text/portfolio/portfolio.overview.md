# Zscaler product portfolio: which product serves each customer need (portfolio.overview)
Also called: zscaler portfolio; zscaler products; product portfolio; portfolio map; zscaler platform; zscaler product list; what products does zscaler have

Each customer need below names the product that serves it and links that product's entry subject. Portfolio-level subjects follow the products.

## Access and forwarding

- Internet and SaaS access, URL filtering and SSL inspection: [ZIA URL Filtering](kb:zia.url-filtering) (ZIA).
- Private application access without a VPN: [ZPA application segments](kb:zpa.app-segments) (ZPA).
- The endpoint agent that forwards user traffic: [ZCC app profiles (web policies)](kb:zcc.app-profiles) (ZCC), with device posture in [ZCC device posture profiles](kb:zcc.device-posture).
- Cloud and branch workload traffic forwarding: [Cloud & Branch Connector: what Cloud Connector is and where each area is described](kb:cloud-connector.overview) (Cloud & Branch Connector).
- Isolation of risky browsing in a remote browser: [How Cloud Browser isolates a page: remote browser, traffic flow, containers and the idle timeout](kb:zbi.cloud-browser) (ZBI, Zero Trust Browser).
- Branch SD-WAN and agentless device segmentation: [Zero Trust Branch: what it is, how it forwards and segments, and how it is deployed](kb:zero-trust-branch.overview) (Zero Trust Branch).
- East-west segmentation of workloads: [Zscaler Microsegmentation (ZMS): agents, local enforcement, region and provisioning](kb:zms.overview) (ZMS).
- Cellular connectivity for IoT and OT devices: [Zscaler Cellular: Zscaler SIM, Cellular Edge and how SIM traffic reaches ZIA and ZPA policy](kb:zscaler-cellular.overview) (Zscaler Cellular).

## Identity, experience and administration

- Identity, OAuth API clients and admin access: [What ZIdentity (Authentication Service) is, and where each part of it is covered](kb:zidentity.overview) (ZIdentity).
- Identity threat detection on endpoints and directories: [Identity Protection (ITDR): how it reads identity stores and endpoints, what it needs, and what can be automated](kb:identity-protection.overview) (Identity Protection, ITDR).
- Digital experience monitoring: [ZDX at a glance: what it measures and which subject answers each question](kb:zdx.overview) (ZDX).
- A single admin console across products: [Zscaler Experience Center: the unified admin console, what it consolidates, and how it is automated](kb:unified.overview) (Experience Center).

## Data, AI and SaaS

- Runtime policy for AI and LLM applications, and the AI Security family: [The AI Security family: AI Access Security, AI Guard (AI Guardrails), AI Red Teaming and the AI asset inventory](kb:ai-security.family) (AI Guard, AI Red Teaming).
- Data at rest: discovery and posture of stored data: [Data Security Posture Management (DSPM): what it finds in stored data, how it differs from inline DLP, and what can be automated](kb:dspm.overview) (DSPM).
- Inline and out-of-band CASB, which is more than one product: [CASB: ZIA inline controls plus out-of-band SaaS security, not one product](kb:portfolio.casb).
- Shadow IT and SaaS application risk reporting: [Shadow IT Report (also SaaS Security Report, ZINS): shadow IT discovery, risk index and API surface](kb:portfolio.saas-security-report) (formerly ZINS).
- Microsoft Copilot oversharing and data controls: [Microsoft Copilot data protection: Zscaler Copilot Security for Microsoft](kb:portfolio.copilot-data-protection).
- DLP incident workflow and ticketing: [Workflow Automation incidents: where they come from, who gets them, statuses, search limits and closing or deleting](kb:zwa.incidents) (ZWA, Workflow Automation).
- SaaS usage, spend and workplace analytics: [Business Insights: SaaS usage and spend, workplace utilization, prerequisites and data sources](kb:business-insights.overview) (Business Insights).

## Exposure management and security operations

- Outside-in discovery of internet-facing assets: [EASM findings: the three drill-down levels, what the risk fields mean, statuses, ROI and the Admin Portal pages](kb:easm.findings) (EASM).
- Asset inventory and coverage gaps: [Asset Exposure Management (AEM): the asset inventory, coverage gaps and policies, and what can be automated](kb:aem.overview) (AEM).
- Vulnerability prioritization: [Unified Vulnerability Management (UVM): what it does with findings, and what can be automated](kb:uvm.overview) (UVM).
- Cyber risk quantification: [Risk360: what it measures, which data it uses, how long it keeps logs, and what can be automated](kb:risk360.overview) (Risk360).
- Active-defense decoys: [Zscaler Deception: decoys and lures, the Private Access integration, roles, and what can be automated](kb:deception.overview) (Zscaler Deception).
- Alert consolidation into incidents: [SOC Workbench (Agentic SOC): alert unification, correlation and prioritization, and what can be automated](kb:soc-workbench.overview) (SOC Workbench).
- Forecasting where a breach may move next: [Breach Predictor: what it predicts, which data it reads, how it maps attacks, and what it integrates with](kb:breach-predictor.overview) (Breach Predictor).
- The SecOps suite, Red Canary MDR, managed threat hunting and CTEM: [Security Operations suite: Exposure Management, Threat Management, Red Canary MDR, managed threat hunting and CTEM](kb:portfolio.security-operations).
- Cloud protection and posture (CNAPP), where the captures are thin: [Zscaler Posture Control (ZPC) and Cloud Protection: what ZPC covered, and its end of life](kb:portfolio.cloud-protection).

## Resilience, segments and government

- Government cloud editions and certifications: [Federal and government cloud editions: FedRAMP, DoD IL5 and other certifications as marketed](kb:portfolio.federal-clouds).
