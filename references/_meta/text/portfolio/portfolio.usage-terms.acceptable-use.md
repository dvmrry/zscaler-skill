# What the Zscaler Acceptable Use Policy and Product Output Disclaimer say about permitted use, AI Features and Outputs (portfolio.usage-terms.acceptable-use)
Also called: acceptable use policy; AUP; product output disclaimer; AI Features; High Risk Activity

The Acceptable Use Policy covers Zscaler's and its affiliates' products, platforms, professional, support and consulting services and any other services provided by or on behalf of Zscaler, including Outputs, deliverables and recommendations generated in connection with them; it is incorporated into, and subject to, the terms of the customer's Agreement, binds on accessing any Offering, and Zscaler may modify it at any time. [portfolio.usage-terms.acceptable-use:aup-scope] Zscaler reserves the right to monitor use of the Offerings and to suspend or terminate access for a violation of the AUP. [portfolio.usage-terms.acceptable-use:aup-monitor-terminate]

**Conduct and technical restrictions.** Section 1 of the AUP bars posting, transmitting or storing libelous, obscene, threatening, defamatory, infringing or unlawful content; reproducing rights-held material without permission; spam; harvesting email addresses or identifiers without consent; falsifying or removing message headers; and impersonation, forgery or phishing. [portfolio.usage-terms.acceptable-use:aup-conduct-restrictions] Section 2 of the AUP prohibits accessing systems or data without the owner's consent, circumventing security, using or distributing tools such as password guessing programs, keystroke loggers, packet sniffers or Trojan Horse programs, unauthorized port scanning, and copying, distributing, sublicensing or making unauthorized modifications to Zscaler Offerings. [portfolio.usage-terms.acceptable-use:aup-technical-restrictions] The AUP bars interfering with any entity's use of the Offerings, including by transmitting malicious code or generating disruptive traffic levels, and degrading the Offerings or Zscaler infrastructure through denial of service attacks, network flooding or service overloading. [portfolio.usage-terms.acceptable-use:aup-interference]

**AI Features.** AUP section 3 governs Zscaler functionality powered by AI or machine learning (AI Features), including chatbots, policy recommendations, generative outputs, AI-assisted security assessments and AI red teaming tools such as the SPLX platform, whether embedded in products or delivered through services; new or updated AI Features are subject to the Policy on release without separate notice or consent. [portfolio.usage-terms.acceptable-use:aup-ai-features-scope] The AUP says AI Features' outputs may be incomplete, inaccurate or inappropriate, are for general informational purposes only, must not be the sole basis for any decision including consequential decisions about employment, health or safety, and are the customer's sole responsibility to verify before acting. [portfolio.usage-terms.acceptable-use:aup-ai-outputs-verify] The AUP prohibits using AI Features to violate law, override safety or privacy filters, generate harmful, sexually explicit, infringing or deceptive content, exploit them for malicious purposes, create training data for other AI systems without Zscaler's permission, or reverse engineer them or extract model weights; they are also not to be treated as professional advice. [portfolio.usage-terms.acceptable-use:aup-ai-prohibited-uses] The AUP states AI Features are not designed or intended for purposes requiring fail-safe performance (High Risk Activity), such as stock trading, financial transaction processing, public transit, nuclear facilities, electrical grid management, aircraft navigation or communication, air traffic control, life support machines or weapons systems, and no Offering or AI Feature may be used as a safety component for one. [portfolio.usage-terms.acceptable-use:aup-high-risk-activity] The AUP says users should not share any personal or sensitive information, Zscaler does not need or require sensitive personal information to provide AI Features, users should not input it, the customer is solely responsible for its own policies on what may be submitted, and Zscaler is not liable for loss, disclosure or misuse of sensitive or confidential information submitted in violation of the customer's policies or applicable law. [portfolio.usage-terms.acceptable-use:aup-sensitive-information]

**Product Output Disclaimer.** The Product Output Disclaimer defines Outputs as information such as risk scores, vulnerability assessments, recommendations, chatbot responses, generated content, reports or deliverables presented by Zscaler Offerings or AI Features; Zscaler disclaims any warranty or representation for them, disclaims all liability for actions taken or not taken based on them, and provides them on an "as is" basis only. [portfolio.usage-terms.acceptable-use:output-disclaimer-as-is] The disclaimer states Outputs may expose restricted information such as employee or financial data, or reveal latent security vulnerabilities in the customer's IT environment that a malicious insider could exploit, and makes the customer solely responsible for access controls and for limiting access to authorized personnel. [portfolio.usage-terms.acceptable-use:output-disclaimer-access-control]
