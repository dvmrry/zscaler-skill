# Zscaler Hardware Usage Terms and RMA process (portfolio.usage-terms.hardware)
Also called: hardware usage terms; rma; return merchandise authorization; product-specific hardware terms

**Ownership and restrictions.** Under the Hardware Usage Terms all Hardware remains Zscaler's property during and after the Subscription Term, and the customer may not sell, lease, transfer or repurpose it or let a third party use it. [portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:ownership] The terms prohibit loading software other than that provided or authorized by Zscaler, physical alterations, self-repair except as instructed, reverse engineering, access by persons other than Zscaler or Zscaler-authorized agents without Zscaler's prior written consent, and relocation without prior written approval. [portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:restrictions]

**Customer obligations.** The customer must appoint at least one technical representative with physical access, and must, at its expense, maintain the Hardware in an environment meeting Zscaler's operating conditions and provide continuous power with surge protection. [portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:obligations] If Zscaler determines traffic or usage exceeds Hardware capacity, the customer must within thirty (30) days of notification accommodate additional or upgraded Hardware or take action to reduce utilization in accordance with Zscaler's guidance. [portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:capacity] The customer is responsible for removing all data from Hardware before returning or disposing of it; Zscaler is not responsible for the security or return of data remaining on Hardware returned to or disposed of by the customer. [portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:data-removal] Values the page leaves undefined are open [gap:portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:undefined-conditions].

**Replacement, return and evaluation.** If Hardware fails or becomes defective during the Subscription Term, Zscaler provides advance replacement Hardware subject to availability, shipped within one (1) to three (3) business days of confirming the failure; on receipt of the replacement the customer returns the defective Hardware within thirty (30) calendar days. [portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:replacement] The RMA page words the replacement decision differently [divergence:portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:replacement-obligation]. On termination or expiration of the Subscription Term all Hardware must be returned within twenty-one (21) calendar days; Zscaler bears return shipping cost and may invoice the replacement value if Hardware is not returned within twenty-one (21) calendar days; where return is not cost-effective, Zscaler may instruct disposal, with a Certificate of Destruction and disposal costs borne by the customer. [portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:termination-return] Hardware provided for evaluation, proof of concept, or trial purposes must be returned within twenty-one (21) calendar days after the evaluation period ends or converted to a paid production subscription before it ends. [portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:evaluation]

**SLA and precedence.** Failure to comply with any obligation under the Hardware Usage Terms, including the Product-Specific Hardware Terms, results in invalidation of related Service Level Agreements for the affected Zscaler service. [portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:sla] In the event of a conflict, the Product-Specific Hardware Terms control for the applicable Hardware. [portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:precedence]

**Product-specific terms.** For ZIA appliances the customer must keep at least two Hardware units per installation location, internet connectivity through at least two discrete ISPs, and staff available 24 hours per day, 7 days per week; Zscaler Cloud Operations manages configuration and operation. [portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:zia] For ZIA appliances (Section A.7) the repair and replacement process for the Hardware is managed by Zscaler Cloud Operations, and Zscaler coordinates replacement directly with the customer in accordance with the procedures described in the applicable support documentation. [portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:zia-repair] For Zero Trust Branch appliances (Section B) the customer must keep personnel with physical access available 24/7, give Zscaler remote connectivity access, and do the physical setup and rack-mounting (if applicable); the technical representative must be trained at the customer's expense. [portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:ztb] For ZPA appliances (Section C) the customer must keep personnel with physical access available 24/7, give Zscaler remote connectivity access, and do the physical setup and rack-mounting (if applicable); the technical representative must be trained at the customer's expense. [portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:zpa-appliance]

**RMA process.** The RMA page says its RMA Process and Policy applies to all Zscaler Hardware and starts with a support ticket in the Zscaler Support Portal; Hardware must not be returned until an RMA is issued. [portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:rma-start] Its scope for ZIA appliances differs from the Hardware Usage Terms [divergence:portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:rma-scope-zia]. Zscaler Support works with the customer to troubleshoot and diagnose issues remotely, and if the issue cannot be resolved remotely or is determined to be a Hardware fault, Zscaler might decide to replace the Hardware; to continue the RMA process Zscaler Support requests company name, Org ID, shipping address, contact details, and product model, serial and asset tag numbers, then creates an RMA number and ships a replacement device. [portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:rma-info] The customer must package and ship the defective device to Zscaler within 30 calendar days of receiving the replacement; a return label is included in most cases, and returned items must have a valid RMA number. [portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:rma-return] The RMA process is complete after the replacement device is installed and operational and Zscaler receives the defective device. [portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:rma-complete]

Sources disagree (name both sides):
- The Hardware Usage Terms say Zscaler shall provide advance replacement Hardware when Hardware fails during the Subscription Term, while the RMA page says that if the issue cannot be resolved remotely or is a Hardware fault, Zscaler might decide to replace the Hardware. [divergence:portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:replacement-obligation]
  - zscaler-help: Zscaler shall provide advance replacement Hardware, subject to availability.
  - zscaler-help: Zscaler might decide to replace the Hardware after remote troubleshooting.
  Impact: Whether replacement is an obligation after a confirmed failure or a Zscaler decision after troubleshooting is not stated; the RMA page is a process description and does not say it limits the terms.
- The RMA page says its process applies to all Zscaler Hardware and starts with a support ticket, while the Hardware Usage Terms say the repair and replacement process for ZIA appliances is managed by Zscaler Cloud Operations, and that Product-Specific Hardware Terms control on conflict. [divergence:portfolio.usage-terms.hardware:rma-scope-zia]
  - zscaler-help: The RMA Process and Policy applies to all Zscaler Hardware.
  - zscaler-help: For ZIA appliances, repair and replacement is managed by Zscaler Cloud Operations, and Product-Specific Hardware Terms control on conflict.
  Impact: For a ZIA appliance the pages do not say whether the support-ticket RMA steps or the Cloud Operations process governs a replacement.
