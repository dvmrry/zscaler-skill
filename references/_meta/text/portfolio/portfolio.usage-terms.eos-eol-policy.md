# Zscaler EOS and EOL Policy: SaaS, software and hardware-refresh milestones (portfolio.usage-terms.eos-eol-policy)
Also called: eos eol policy; end of sale; end of life; supported versions; hardware refresh date; hardware return date

The EOS and EOL Policy states it is effective as of August 1st, 2017 and covers Zscaler SaaS Products, Zscaler Software Products and Zscaler Hardware. [portfolio.usage-terms.eos-eol-policy:effective] See also [Dated end-of-sale and end-of-life notices for Analyzer, Shift, Office365Mon, CIEM, SecureAgent and ZAB hardware](kb:portfolio.usage-terms.product-notices), [Dated end-of-support notices for platforms, crypto, parsers and services](kb:portfolio.usage-terms.service-notices) and [Dated end-of-support notices for Client Connector operating systems and posture features](kb:portfolio.usage-terms.client-notices).

**SaaS Products.** For a SaaS Product, Zscaler notifies at least 90 days before End-of-Sale; after EOS the product is unavailable for new orders but may be renewed or extended with more users through its EOL date, with support until EOL. [portfolio.usage-terms.eos-eol-policy:saas-eos] A SaaS Product's EOL is at least 1 year after its EOS date; after EOL the SaaS Product is no longer available, but customers with contracts extending beyond it are offered an upgrade path or replacement, or Zscaler supports the current Product until the end of the then-current contract term without renewal. [portfolio.usage-terms.eos-eol-policy:saas-eol]

**Software Products.** In general, Zscaler supports the latest Software version and the two previous versions of each Software Product, although it may designate another version as supported and notes this at Supported Versions. [portfolio.usage-terms.eos-eol-policy:software-support] Zscaler Support will request an upgrade to a Supported Version before beginning troubleshooting, and Supported Versions are eligible for patch and maintenance upgrades until the EOL of the Product. [portfolio.usage-terms.eos-eol-policy:software-support-ticket] For Software Products, Zscaler notifies at least 60 days before EOS and EOL is at least 6 months after the EOS date; EOS products are no longer available for purchase or download, and after EOL they are no longer eligible for patch, maintenance upgrades or support and have no supported versions. [portfolio.usage-terms.eos-eol-policy:software-eos-eol] The Supported Versions page covers only some products [gap:portfolio.usage-terms.eos-eol-policy:supported-versions-list]; for Cloud Connector see [Cloud Connector OS upgrades: redeployment and the ZscalerOS 24 end of support](kb:cloud-connector.upgrades.os-images).

**Hardware.** Hardware Refresh applies to Zscaler-owned hardware; the Refresh Date is in most cases about 5 years from the subscription start date, though Zscaler may refresh hardware at any time, and Zscaler notifies at least 90 days before it. [portfolio.usage-terms.eos-eol-policy:hw-refresh-date] The Hardware Return Date is, unless otherwise agreed, a maximum of 90 days after the Refresh Shipment Date; SLAs are not available for Products running on hardware used after the Hardware Return Date, and, at Zscaler's option, hardware not returned within 90 days from the Hardware Return Date may be subject to additional asset recovery mitigation. [portfolio.usage-terms.eos-eol-policy:hw-return] Specific hardware may have an EOL date, the latest date it can be used in production, which unless there is an urgent reason to refresh sooner is 1 year after the Refresh Date; SLAs are not available to Products on hardware used after its EOL date. [portfolio.usage-terms.eos-eol-policy:hw-eol] The hardware terms themselves are in [Zscaler Hardware Usage Terms and RMA process](kb:portfolio.usage-terms.hardware).

**Disclaimer.** The policy's general disclaimer says that, subject to the details where use of the Products is permitted after an EOL date, use of Products past the EOL date, or the Hardware Return Date for hardware, results in invalidation of all Service Level Agreements and service credits for the Products. [portfolio.usage-terms.eos-eol-policy:disclaimer]
