# Business Continuity Cloud: components, constraints and what is open (portfolio.business-continuity-cloud)
Also called: business continuity cloud; bcc; zscaler business continuity cloud; business continuity mode; private policy cache; private pac servers; private pac server; bc cloud

The catastrophic-tier deployment is described in [The Resilience page says Zscaler business continuity solutions handle traffic during full cloud outages; the Business Continuity Cloud help page says that service mainly helps organizations stay connected and protected during critical failure scenarios, which it says include global Zscaler cloud outages.](kb:portfolio.resilience:bcc-relation). The ZIA behavior, including prerequisites and failover, is in [Private Service Edges in the Business Continuity Cloud](kb:zia.private-service-edge.business-continuity).

The Business Continuity Cloud is a fully isolated, dedicated private cloud hosted in a third-party data center that lets users reach the internet and private applications when the Internet & SaaS (ZIA) and Private Access (ZPA) clouds are unreachable, without on-premises business continuity infrastructure. [portfolio.business-continuity-cloud:bcc-isolated-cloud] The managed-service page says the Business Continuity Cloud has two components, a private data plane and a private control plane: the data plane uses Private Service Edges for Internet & SaaS and for Private Access, and the control plane uses Private Policy Cache, Private PAC Server and Private Cloud Controllers for Private Access. [portfolio.business-continuity-cloud:bcc-planes] The two help pages count its components differently [divergence:portfolio.business-continuity-cloud:component-count]. The Private Access Business Continuity Cloud uses Private Service Edges, App Connectors and the Private Cloud Controller to keep private applications reachable in business continuity mode. [portfolio.business-continuity-cloud:bcc-private-access] Private Cloud Controllers are in [Private Cloud Controllers and Private Clouds as the Business Continuity layer](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.private-cloud-controller).

The components page says the Business Continuity Cloud has two Zscaler-managed components, Private Policy Cache and Private PAC Servers, which work with the Private Service Edges for Internet & SaaS. [portfolio.business-continuity-cloud:bcc-components] The Private Policy Cache supports a seamless switch between public and private clouds in business continuity mode. [portfolio.business-continuity-cloud:bcc-seamless-switch] Their roles are in [During normal operations the Private Policy Cache fetches, stores and regularly syncs tenant-specific configurations from the cloud so they are available in business continuity mode, and serves as the primary control path for local Service Edge instances; during business continuity it operates independently of public clouds to maintain security policies.](kb:zia.private-service-edge.business-continuity:policy-cache-roles) and [Private PAC Servers receive and locally store PAC files from the public cloud for each Business Continuity Cloud site; during business continuity they host disaster recovery PAC files that redirect client requests to the appropriate Business Continuity Cloud, and their geo-awareness lets Zscaler Client Connector connect to the nearest Business Continuity Cloud based on its IP address or location.](kb:zia.private-service-edge.business-continuity:pac-servers), and the redundant pairs in [Private Policy Cache and Private PAC Servers are deployed in redundant pairs in each Business Continuity Cloud site; they are managed by Zscaler and subject to ongoing updates and enhancements at any time.](kb:zia.private-service-edge.business-continuity:redundant-pairs).

Only last-known-good policy applies in this mode: [Only the last known good policy configuration applies to outgoing traffic in business continuity mode: recent policy updates might not take effect immediately, and new user authentication or enrollment is not supported.](kb:zia.private-service-edge.business-continuity:last-known-good-policy). Z-Tunnel 2.0 is not supported: [Zscaler Tunnel 2.0 is not supported in business continuity mode.](kb:zia.private-service-edge.business-continuity:z-tunnel-2-unsupported). Which tunnel types the mode supports is a divergence between the two help pages: [zia.private-service-edge.business-continuity:supported-connectivity-methods](kb:zia.private-service-edge.business-continuity:supported-connectivity-methods).

Public cloud upgrades delay the business continuity upgrade on purpose: [During public cloud software upgrades the Business Continuity Cloud upgrade is delayed on purpose to prevent upgrade-related issues; this also delays the update of new policies or configurations, for fault isolation.](kb:zia.private-service-edge.business-continuity:upgrade-delay).

Customers must configure, enroll and maintain Private Cloud Controllers to provide application access during a global Private Access outage. [portfolio.business-continuity-cloud:bcc-customer-pcc] The customer side is therefore more than a Private Service Edge deployment [gap:portfolio.business-continuity-cloud:customer-side]. Whether ZPA's business continuity settings API is this service's configuration surface is open [gap:portfolio.business-continuity-cloud:sdk-surface].

What to check:
1. Before answering a tunnel question about business continuity mode, check the connectivity divergence and do not rely on one help page alone.
2. For a question about configuring the service, say that the configuration surface is open and do not infer one from the ZPA settings API.

Sources disagree (name both sides):
- Which two components make up the Business Continuity Cloud. [divergence:portfolio.business-continuity-cloud:component-count]
  - zscaler-help: Private Policy Cache and Private PAC Servers, alongside the Internet & SaaS Private Service Edges.
  - zscaler-help: A private data plane and a private control plane, spanning Internet & SaaS and Private Access (Private Cloud Controllers included).
  Impact: Reading only the components page suggests the service is ZIA-only; the managed-service page also covers private applications through Private Access Private Service Edges, App Connectors and Private Cloud Controllers.
