# Dated end-of-support notices for platforms, crypto, parsers and services (portfolio.usage-terms.service-notices)
Also called: end of support notice; zscaleros 10 end of support; esxi 5.5; centos 7 rhel 7; spidermonkey pac parser; 3des; flash cookies; sms otp end of support; cloud connector repository certificate; certificate downloads

Each notice below is what it says on its announcement date. The 3DES notices name their scope: IKE Phase 2 for IPSec, and SSL.

**ZscalerOS 10 virtual machines.** The Azure notice dated March 11, 2022 says Azure Virtual Machine deployments with ZscalerOS version 10 and earlier are no longer supported for ZIA Virtual Service Edges, VZENs and NSS as of June 24, 2022, and that the migration target is ZscalerOS version 24 or higher; it says no action is required for customers with VMs on AWS or ESXi. [portfolio.usage-terms.service-notices:zscaleros-10] The AWS notice dated May 4, 2022 says Amazon Web Services Virtual Machine deployments with ZscalerOS version 10 and earlier are no longer supported for NSS as of June 24, 2022. [portfolio.usage-terms.service-notices:zscaleros-10-aws] The ESXi notice dated May 4, 2022 says VMware ESXi Virtual Machine deployments with ZscalerOS version 10 and earlier are no longer supported for Index Tool, NSS, ZAB, ZIA Virtual Service Edge, VZEN and Zscaler Incident Receiver as of June 24, 2022. [portfolio.usage-terms.service-notices:zscaleros-10-esxi] Whether AWS and ESXi customers had to act is in dispute [divergence:portfolio.usage-terms.service-notices:zscaleros-10-action-required]. The ZscalerOS 24 target is covered in [Cloud Connector OS upgrades: redeployment and the ZscalerOS 24 end of support](kb:cloud-connector.upgrades.os-images).

**Hypervisor and Linux platforms.** The notice dated January 26, 2024 says Zscaler will no longer support ESXi version 5.5 after December 24, 2024, and that customers currently using ESXi version 5.5 will need ESXi version 7.0 Update 2 or later after this date. [portfolio.usage-terms.service-notices:esxi-55] The ESXi minimum differs from other pages [divergence:portfolio.usage-terms.service-notices:esxi-minimum-version]. The notice (revised December 9, 2024) says Zscaler will no longer support App Connectors and Private Service Edges on CentOS 7.x, RHEL 7.x and Oracle Linux 7.x after March 31, 2025, and customers will need to migrate to RHEL 9 or later and the EL9 ZPA repository. [portfolio.usage-terms.service-notices:el7]

**PAC parser, ciphers and cookies.** The notice dated July 21, 2023 says that on January 31, 2024 Zscaler will no longer support the SpiderMonkey-based PAC parsers and all customers will be moved to the V8 PAC parser. [portfolio.usage-terms.service-notices:spidermonkey] The notice dated August 25, 2017 says Zscaler no longer supports 3DES for IPSec encryption (IKE Phase 2 only), recommends null encryption for IPSec tunnels to the Zscaler service, and says to use AES encryption if IPSec encryption is required for regulatory or compliance reasons and the customer has the appropriate entitlement. [portfolio.usage-terms.service-notices:3des-ipsec] The notice dated September 13, 2017 says Zscaler will end support of 3DES for SSL after the 5.6 release, clients should change to AES256-GCM-SHA384, AES128-GCM-SHA256, AES256-SHA or AES128-SHA by October 31, 2018, and clients still using 3DES will be unable to connect to websites. [portfolio.usage-terms.service-notices:3des-ssl] The notice dated August 14, 2017 says Zscaler discontinued its support for Adobe Flash cookies in the 5.5 release; if no authentication mechanism is configured before support ends, the mechanism already configured on the ZIA Admin Portal is used to authenticate users. The notice describes cookies downloaded from admin.<Zscaler Cloud Name>/download/cookies as Flash cookie use. [portfolio.usage-terms.service-notices:flash-cookies]

**Authentication.** The notice dated May 20, 2026 gives May 22, 2026 as the date SMS MFA enrollment is no longer available for new tenants created after the announcement date, and says SMS-based OTP for hosted users and hosted admins of Authentication Service is disabled for sign-in as of August 07, 2026. [portfolio.usage-terms.service-notices:sms-otp] Whether the disablement has happened is open [gap:portfolio.usage-terms.service-notices:sms-otp-status].

**Certificates.** The notice dated October 8, 2026 says the TLS certificate for the Cloud Connector software update repository expires on March 18, 2027, and says customers must update their virtual machines or use the latest marketplace images, which Zscaler recommends completing by December 31, 2026; for existing Cloud Connector deployments it says no action is required because Zscaler is deploying an automatic update to the CA trust bundle, while new deployments after March 18, 2027 require the updated marketplace image. Cloud Connector or Branch Connector VMs below version 1.146 lack the DigiCert G2 certificate chain; the March 2026 patch is included in Cloud Connector 1.146.5 or higher and Branch Connector 1.146.0 or higher. [portfolio.usage-terms.service-notices:cc-repo-cert] The same notice says that, if you do not take action before March 18, 2027, existing Cloud Connectors lose the ability to receive future software updates if the automatic fix is not applied, new deployments from older marketplace images cannot connect to the update repository and come online, and Autoscaling Groups using old launch templates, warm pool or instance templates spin up instances that cannot connect, resulting in scale out failures. [portfolio.usage-terms.service-notices:cc-repo-cert-impact] See [The pkg-repo.zscaler.com certificate renewal: minimum images, autoscaling templates and why connectors might not update automatically](kb:cloud-connector.upgrades.repo-certificate). The outcome is open [gap:portfolio.usage-terms.service-notices:cc-repo-cert-outcome]. The notice dated July 11, 2023 says that as of September 1, 2023 the certificate download function is removed; Client Connector 2.1 and earlier downloaded the SSL inspection certificate from a CDN, and devices still on 2.1 or earlier continue to function but will not download the certificate if it is not trusted. [portfolio.usage-terms.service-notices:certificate-downloads]

Sources disagree (name both sides):
- The ESXi 5.5 notice says customers will need ESXi 7.0 Update 2 or later after December 24, 2024, while the Index Tool VMware page and the Virtual Service Edge platform table give VMware ESX/ESXi 6.0 or later as the hypervisor requirement. [divergence:portfolio.usage-terms.service-notices:esxi-minimum-version]
  - zscaler-help: Customers currently using ESXi 5.5 will need ESXi 7.0 Update 2 or later after December 24, 2024.
  - zscaler-help: The Index Tool VMware deployment requires VMware ESX/ESXi version 6.0 or later.
  - zscaler-help: Virtual Service Edge hypervisor support is VMware ESXi 6.0 and later.
  Impact: The notice names no product, so whether the 7.0 Update 2 floor applies to Index Tool or Virtual Service Edge, and whether those pages' 6.0 minimum is stale, is not stated.
- The Azure ZscalerOS 10 notice says no action is required for customers with VMs on AWS or ESXi because they are upgraded automatically, while the AWS and ESXi notices tell customers running ZscalerOS 10 and earlier on those platforms to migrate to ZscalerOS 24 or higher by June 24, 2022. [divergence:portfolio.usage-terms.service-notices:zscaleros-10-action-required]
  - zscaler-help: No action is required for customers with VMs on AWS or ESXi; those VMs are upgraded remotely and automatically (notice dated March 11, 2022).
  - zscaler-help: Customers running an AWS VM deployment with ZscalerOS version 10 and earlier must migrate to ZscalerOS 24 or higher by June 24, 2022 (notice dated May 4, 2022).
  - zscaler-help: Customers running an ESXi VM deployment with ZscalerOS version 10 and earlier must migrate to ZscalerOS 24 or higher by June 24, 2022 (notice dated May 4, 2022).
  Impact: An AWS or ESXi customer reading only the Azure notice would take no action, while the later AWS and ESXi notices ask for a migration; the pages do not say whether the later notices replace the Azure notice's statement.
