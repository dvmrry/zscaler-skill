# ZPA access policy global rules and policy groups (zpa.policy.policy-groups)
Also called: global rules; policy groups; access policy global rules; defined by my tenant; common criteria policy group; group order; default policy groups

A policy group is a collection of access rules that share the same common criteria; the configured criteria are applied automatically to every rule in the group, each group can have its own common criteria, and changes to them affect every rule in the group. The page says the AND operator is used to logically configure the criteria defined in each rule in the group. [zpa.policy.policy-groups:policy-group-definition]

**Availability.** Global rules and policy groups for access policy are in limited availability, and the pages say to contact Zscaler Support to learn more. [zpa.policy.policy-groups:limited-availability] The Global Rules page says to ensure that Application Scaling is enabled and all of its specific prerequisites are fully met, and that global rules are only supported for Experience Center; the Policy Groups page says policy groups are only supported for Experience Center. [zpa.policy.policy-groups:experience-center-and-scaling] The legacy Private Access cloud service API calls are not supported for managing policies after global rules and policy groups are enabled, and the Global Rules page says to ensure that legacy cloud service API automation and workflows for access policies are updated. [zpa.policy.policy-groups:legacy-api-unsupported]

**Global rules.** Global rules define access policies that apply across all policy groups, and can be created to enforce organization-wide access requirements before policy group rules are evaluated. [zpa.policy.policy-groups:global-rules-scope] To configure an access policy, the global rules and policy groups must be defined first. [zpa.policy.policy-groups:define-first] Global rules can be bulk moved or deleted, and a single global rule can be moved or copied. [zpa.policy.policy-groups:global-rule-bulk-actions]

**Policy groups.** Each policy group has a Group Order, its policy evaluation order number, and the Policy Groups page shows the number of rules in the group and whether common criteria are defined for it. [zpa.policy.policy-groups:group-order-columns] Access policies created in the legacy UI are automatically moved to default policy groups. [zpa.policy.policy-groups:legacy-ui-policies-moved]

**The Defined by My Tenant page.** On the Defined by My Tenant page each access rule shows the name of its associated policy group, and the page shows the total rules, total policy groups and the numbers of enabled and disabled rules; a rule's order is changed on the Global Rules or Policy Groups page. [zpa.policy.policy-groups:tenant-page-columns]

**Names.** Policy group names and global rule names cannot contain special characters except periods, hyphens and underscores. [zpa.policy.policy-groups:name-characters]

**Open points.** How global rules and policy group rules interleave in one rule order is not stated [gap:zpa.policy.policy-groups:global-group-interleave]. Whether common criteria are combined with a rule's own criteria is not stated [gap:zpa.policy.policy-groups:common-criteria-combination]. What the default policy groups are named, whether a group carries an action, and the limits are not stated [gap:zpa.policy.policy-groups:group-details-not-stated].

The criteria and conditions these rules use are in [Criteria available on ZPA access policy rules](kb:zpa.policy.criteria) and [Boolean logic and operand forms of ZPA policy conditions](kb:zpa.policy.conditions). The Python SDK controllers are in [Python SDK policy group, rule and set controllers](kb:zpa.sdk-python.policy-groups). Rule order is in [Access policy rule order](kb:zpa.policy.access-rule-order).

What to check:
1. Before relying on global rules or policy groups, check that the tenant has the limited-availability feature enabled, with Application Scaling, and that API automation no longer uses the legacy calls.
2. When a rule seems to be evaluated in the wrong place, check the Group Order of its policy group and whether a global rule applies first.
3. When a policy group's common criteria change, check every rule in the group, and whether the group's criteria combine with a rule's own.

Not known (open gaps):
- How global rules and policy group rules interleave in one rule order [gap:zpa.policy.policy-groups:global-group-interleave]: settled by lab-test or support-ticket
- Whether a group's common criteria are ANDed with a rule's own criteria or replace them [gap:zpa.policy.policy-groups:common-criteria-combination]: settled by lab-test or support-ticket
- Default policy group names, whether a policy group carries an action, and the limits [gap:zpa.policy.policy-groups:group-details-not-stated]: settled by source-read or lab-test
