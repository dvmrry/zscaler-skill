# App Connector, Private Service Edge and Private Cloud Controller dashboards (zpa.troubleshooting.component-dashboards)
Also called: app connectors dashboard; private service edges dashboard; private cloud controllers dashboard; activity monitor widgets

These are the console dashboards for the three infrastructure components, App Connectors, Private Service Edges and Private Cloud Controllers. The application, user and health dashboards are in [Applications, Users, Health, Extranet and Source IP Anchoring dashboards](kb:zpa.troubleshooting.dashboards). The components themselves: [App Connector health, status values and telemetry](kb:zpa.app-connectors.health), [ZPA Private Service Edges](kb:zpa.private-service-edges) and [Private Cloud Controllers and Private Clouds as the Business Continuity layer](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.private-cloud-controller). The VPN Service Edges, Network Connectors and BGP Peers dashboards are in [VPN Service Edges, Network Connectors and BGP Peers dashboards](kb:zpa.vpn.dashboards).

**Range and widgets.** The App Connector dashboard shows data over 30 Mins to 14 Days or a Custom Range whose start date can be within the last 14 days, by default the last 30 minutes, and displays the most recent information from the last five minutes. Because data is aggregated differently for different time period filters, the same point in time in an Activity Monitor widget may show slightly different values depending on the time chosen. [zpa.troubleshooting.component-dashboards:range-and-refresh] The App Connector dashboard gives two widget limits: at least four widgets must be selected and no more than 8 are available at one time, and at least three must be selected and no more than 6 are available at one time. The Private Cloud Controller dashboard allows a minimum of 3 and a maximum of 6 charts. [zpa.troubleshooting.component-dashboards:widget-limits] At most 25 App Connectors can be selected at a time; selecting a group of more than 25 with none selected uses the group's first 25. Connector and group filters combine with OR rather than AND, and with none selected the top App Connectors from the Peak Memory Utilization chart are selected by default. [zpa.troubleshooting.component-dashboards:selection-25]

**Reading the values.** On the App Connector dashboard Top Errors shows up to the top 100 App Connectors with the most errors in the selected range, and Peak CPU Utilization shows up to the top 10 using the most CPU, which is not the average CPU used over the time frame. [zpa.troubleshooting.component-dashboards:peaks-not-averages] The App Connector dashboard's Peak TCP Port Utilization counts TCP ports for IPv4 and does not show App Connectors using TCP ports for IPv6. The Private Service Edge dashboard has a Peak TCP IPv6 Port Utilization widget and a Peak System File Descriptor Utilization widget. [zpa.troubleshooting.component-dashboards:ports-ipv4] The App Connector dashboard's worked example computes a bit rate from total bytes at two times: 100 bytes at 7:00 and 1000 at 7:05 is 24 bits per second. The Application Tunnel Creation Rate is measured from the total application tunnels at two points in time as (a2-a1) / (t2-t1). [zpa.troubleshooting.component-dashboards:rates] On the App Connector and Private Service Edge dashboards dashed lines indicate the expected trajectory of the data and are not actual data, while the Private Cloud Controller dashboard says its widgets show solid lines that represent actual data for the time period. [zpa.troubleshooting.component-dashboards:dashed-lines] The App Connector page prints the bit-rate formula without the factor of 8 [gap:zpa.troubleshooting.component-dashboards:copy-defects].

**Details and latency.** An App Connector's details show, among other fields, the date and time of its next periodic software update, the next software version it upgrades to, and a Connected or Disconnected status for whether it was up or down during the time range; CPU and memory utilization there are the highest used in the past 5 minutes. [zpa.troubleshooting.component-dashboards:connector-details] The App Connector dashboard's Latency tab is limited to data from 30 Mins to 24 Hours with a default of 30 minutes, and has widgets named App Connector To Service Edge TCP Latency and App Connector To Service Edge Latency. The Private Service Edge dashboard has Private Service Edge to Default Gateway TCP Latency and Private Service Edge to Public Service Edge Latency widgets. [zpa.troubleshooting.component-dashboards:latency-tab] The App Connector dashboard's Peak Inspection Tunnel Count in Past 14 Days shows up to the top 10 App Connectors with the highest connections to AppProtection in the past 14 days. [zpa.troubleshooting.component-dashboards:inspection-tunnels]

**Private Service Edge and Private Cloud Controller.** A Private Service Edge's details include its active and disconnected connections to App Connectors, the number of applications configured for access through it, its file descriptor maximum limit, its number of usable ports and its total completed transactions. [zpa.troubleshooting.component-dashboards:pse-details] The Private Cloud Controller dashboard shows the App Connectors and Private Service Edges for Private Access connected to the top 10 controllers, the top 10 Client Connector redirections to Private Service Edges, and the logs successfully transmitted to the SIEM and failed to be transmitted, the latter a cumulative count of all diagnostic log types. [zpa.troubleshooting.component-dashboards:pcc-widgets]

What to check:
1. Whether a Peak widget is being read as an average, since the page says its ranking of up to the top 10 App Connectors is not the average over the time frame.
2. Whether a port-exhaustion question about an App Connector rests on IPv4 ports only.
3. Whether a dashed line is being read as data on the App Connector or Private Service Edge dashboards.

Not known (open gaps):
