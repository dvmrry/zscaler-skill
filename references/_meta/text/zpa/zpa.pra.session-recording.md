# PRA session recordings: status, transcoding, playback and sharing (zpa.pra.session-recording)
Also called: pra session recording; privileged session recording; recording status transcoding; transcode now; pra recording permalink; session recording full access; where are pra recordings stored; pra recording not streamable

Recordings are produced when a consoles policy turns on Record Sessions. They then move through transcoding states before they can be streamed.

**Recording.** Session recording is enabled through the Record Sessions setting of a consoles policy. A user in a recorded console sees a Recording notification at the top of the console window, and the recording ends when the user ends the session. [zpa.pra.session-recording:recording-enabled-by-policy] When an end user selects a privileged console with a Session Recording-enabled consoles policy, a Recording notification appears in the console. The recording starts when the user is in the privileged session, stops after the user disconnects it, and the recordings can be found on the Privileged Sessions page. [zpa.pra.session-recording:recording-lifecycle] Where recordings are stored is open [gap:zpa.pra.session-recording:storage-location].

**Status.** A recording's status is one of Failed (the session did not start, for example from invalid credentials or network issues), Completed (the session is done but the recording is not yet available), Available (downloadable and viewable but not ready for streaming), Enqueued (waiting at low priority for transcoding), Transcode Now (priority was requested), Enqueued On Priority, Transcoding (converting from a raw format), Ready for Streaming, and Transcoding Failed. [zpa.pra.session-recording:status-values] An admin can raise a recording's transcode priority with the Transcode Now action, which is available only when the recording is Available, Enqueued or Transcoding Failed. [zpa.pra.session-recording:transcode-now]

**Playing and sharing.** A recording in Ready for Streaming status plays directly; a recording in another status except Failed, Completed or Ready for Streaming must be downloaded and played offline. [zpa.pra.session-recording:play-when-ready] The Download action is available when Session Recordings is set to Full Access in the admin role. [zpa.pra.session-recording:download-needs-full-access] A recording can be shared with admins in the same tenant who have Session Recording enabled by copying the video page URL. A permalink starts the video at a specific time, and recordings an admin does not have access to cannot be viewed. [zpa.pra.session-recording:sharing] The permission that gates playback is open [gap:zpa.pra.session-recording:full-access-for-playback].

What a recording excludes is in [PRA consoles policy: file transfer, clipboard, recording and sharing](kb:zpa.pra.consoles-policy).

What to check:
1. Whether the recording's status is one that streams, or whether it is Enqueued, Available or Transcoding Failed and needs Transcode Now or a download.
2. Whether the admin's role grants Session Recordings Full Access for download.
3. Whether a storage or playback-permission answer rests on the unverified statements.
