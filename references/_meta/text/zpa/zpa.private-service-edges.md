# ZPA Private Service Edges (zpa.private-service-edges)
Also called: private service edge; zpa private service edge; zpa pse; on-prem private service edge zpa; pse

A Private Service Edge question in Private Access is one of twelve, and Internet & SaaS has its own Service Edges ([ZIA Private Service Edge](kb:zia.private-service-edge)). The VPN (for Legacy Apps) Service Edges are in [VPN Service Edges: what they are, statuses, client IP pools, update schedule, BGP configuration mode and China](kb:zpa.vpn.service-edges).

1. **What is it, and how does it differ from the Public tier and from ZIA?** [What a ZPA Private Service Edge is](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.what-it-is), [ZPA Public Service Edges](kb:zpa.public-service-edges).
2. **Do we need one?** The documented benefits and the claims that are not supported: [When a Private Service Edge is warranted](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.when-to-deploy).
3. **What does it connect to, and what must the firewall allow?** Tunnels, certificates, SSL interception: [Tunnels, certificates and firewall requirements on the Private Service Edge path](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.data-path).
4. **Why does a client use, or not use, this one?** Trusted networks, Publicly Accessible, grace distance, country policy: [How clients are steered to a Private Service Edge and which country policy applies](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.client-selection).
5. **Which platform, how much capacity, what limits?** [Private Service Edge platforms, host OS maintenance, capacity and limits](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.deployment). The software and steps for each platform: [Private Service Edge software and deployment steps by platform](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.platforms).
6. **How does an instance join a group?** [Enrolling a Private Service Edge](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.enrollment). The key itself, its reuse limit, naming and the keys page: [Private Service Edge provisioning keys: what a key is, reuse limits, naming, View or Export, signing certificate and the keys page](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.provisioning-keys).
7. **What does a group control?** Update windows, version profiles, Auto Delete, Microtenants: [Private Service Edge groups: membership, software updates and version profiles](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.groups).
8. **What happens in a global outage?** [Disaster recovery mode and Private Service Edge groups](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery), [Private Cloud Controllers and Private Clouds as the Business Continuity layer](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.private-cloud-controller). Business Continuity, for Private Access cloud outages or ISP outages: [Business Continuity for Private Access: prerequisites, domain names, settings, entering and exiting, limitations](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.business-continuity). The controller's sizing and network: [Private Cloud Controller sizing, network requirements, platforms and enrollment](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.private-cloud-controller.deployment); its console commands and restore: [Private Cloud Controller console commands and backup restore](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.private-cloud-controller.troubleshooting). The disaster recovery TXT records: [Disaster recovery activation TXT records and the Zscaler DNS Record Generator](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery.dns-records).
9. **How do users in mainland China reach private applications?** The partner-hosted Private Service Edge, its data centers, traffic flows and license: [China Premium Service for Private Access: the partner-hosted Private Service Edge, data centers, traffic flows and prerequisites](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.china-premium).
10. **How is it managed as code or through the API?** [The zpa_service_edge_group resource for Private Service Edge groups](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.terraform), [Management API endpoints for Private Service Edges, groups and schedules](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.api-surface).
11. **Which logs describe it?** [Private Service Edge logs in the Log Streaming Service](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.logging).
12. **What do the AWS and Azure reference modules deploy?** [Reference Terraform modules for Private Service Edges on AWS and Azure](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.reference-modules).

The Manager software, OAuth enrollment rules, network settings and STIG images that Private Service Edges share with the other software components are in [ZPA software components: Manager software, OAuth enrollment, network settings and STIG images](kb:zpa.software-components).

[zpa.private-service-edges.deployment:platform-prereqs]

[zpa.private-service-edges.groups:group-membership]
