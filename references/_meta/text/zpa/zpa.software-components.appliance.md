# The ZPA appliance: models, hardware and power, ports, installation troubleshooting and enrollment (zpa.software-components.appliance)
Also called: zpa appliance; ZS-ZPA-APPL-8010; ZS-ZPA-APPL-8010-APPC; ZS-ZPA-APPL-8010-PSE; ZS-ZPA-PSE-8010; ZT-8010; ZS-ZPA-APPL-800; ZS-ZPA-APPL-800-APPC; private access hardware appliance

For customers with multiple global sites that need high-performance connectivity between them (such as users accessing large files and medical records, without virtual computing power available), high-performance and high-throughput hardware appliances are available for Private Access App Connectors and Private Service Edges. ZPA appliances include a single high-performance App Connector or Private Service Edge, and the Understanding ZPA Appliances page says the feature is in limited availability, to be discussed with the Zscaler Account team. [zpa.software-components.appliance:what-it-is] The Understanding ZPA Appliances table names the App Connector appliance ZS-ZPA-APPL-8010-APPC and the Private Service Edge appliance ZS-ZPA-APPL-8010-PSE; both list 16C / 128GB / 1TB, ports of 10x 1GbE and 8x 10 GbE (SFP+), a 1U (2 x PSU) form factor and 3 Gbps throughput. [zpa.software-components.appliance:models-and-specs] The mount manual also covers a smaller App Connector appliance, ZS-ZPA-APPL-800-APPC, which the Understanding ZPA Appliances table does not list. It is rack-mountable, with a wall mount kit available only by request; its table gives DC power from an included external AC adapter, no redundant power supply, typical power consumption of 24W idle and 43W at full load, 60W maximum, and fan cooling. [zpa.software-components.appliance:appl-800] The Private Service Edge model is named two ways [divergence:zpa.software-components.appliance:pse-model-name].

**Hardware.** The ZS-ZPA-APPL-8010 (APPC and PSE) is a 1U, 19-inch rack-mountable form factor with a weight of 18.96 lbs (8.6 kg) and a total packaged weight of 33 lbs (15 kg). The environmental and power table for ZS-ZPA-APPL-8010 gives an operating temperature of 32 to 104°F (0 to 40°C), relative humidity of 5 to 90% (operating) and 5 to 95% (non-operating), internal AC power, 1 + 1 redundant power supply, typical power consumption of 107.8W idle and 225.5W at full load, and maximum power consumption of 300W with a redundant PSU or 350W with a single PSU. [zpa.software-components.appliance:physical-and-power] The ZS-ZPA-APPL-8010 package contains the ZS-ZPA-APPL-8010-XX (APPC/PSE) unit, 2 RJ45 cables, a USB serial console cable, 4 SFP+ FTLX8574D3BCL, 6 A screws for attaching the brackets to the appliance, 6 B screws for mounting the appliance to the rack and 2 ear brackets. [zpa.software-components.appliance:package-contents] Two or more appliances can be deployed in a network location and paired into groups for load balancing. [zpa.software-components.appliance:pairing] The meaning of pairing into groups is open [gap:zpa.software-components.appliance:appliance-details].

**Ports.** Ports can be configured using Linux commands from the command-line interface. The port table lists C as an RJ45 serial console; 1 to 6 (GE1/eno1, GE2/enp1s0, GE3/ens84f3, GE4/ens84f2, GE5/ens84f1, GE6/ens84f0) and 11 to 14 (GE11/ens83f0, GE12/ens83f1, GE13/ens83f2, GE14/ens83f3) as 1GbE RJ45 LAN ports; 7 to 10 (XE7/eno4, XE8/eno5, XE9/eno2, XE10/eno3) as 10G SFP+ LAN ports, with port 7 the default traffic port; and 15 to 18 (XE15/ens5f3, XE16/ens5f2, XE17/ens5f1, XE18/ens5f0) as Optional SFP+ 10G LAN ports. [zpa.software-components.appliance:port-map]

**Installation problems.** A continuous beep indicates that the second power source is unplugged or faulty, or that only one power supply is active or the power supply is defective; to silence it, press the round button located near or between the power supplies or connect the second power source, and if the power supply is faulty, contact Zscaler Support to request a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA). [zpa.software-components.appliance:power-beep] If the appliance does not power on, the page says to verify that the power outlet is active (a power LED should be on, otherwise there is no power to the unit) and that the power supply is securely plugged in. If a network cable is connected but no link light appears, there could be an issue with the cable, the appliance port or the connected switch port; the page says to replace the cable, try a different port on the appliance, and power cycle the device to confirm the LED returns to a normal state. [zpa.software-components.appliance:install-troubleshooting]

**Enrolling it.** The ZPA appliance is shipped with an App Connector or Private Service Edge installed on it, and before use the App Connector or Private Service Edge must be enrolled, including applying a provisioning key. For an App Connector the Admin Console steps are to add an App Connector group, add an App Connector provisioning key to use for enrollment and add the App Connector; for a Private Service Edge, to add a Private Service Edge. [zpa.software-components.appliance:preinstalled-and-enrollment] Through console access the appliance login is Username admin and Password zscaler, and the page says to change the password after logging in for the first time. [zpa.software-components.appliance:default-credentials] The page recommends connecting to port 7 XE7/eno4 SFP+, says link aggregation mechanisms are not supported at this time, and says to configure the network interface if necessary for an environment that is not a standard DHCP environment. It has the sshd service enabled if ssh to the appliance is wanted, and for an App Connector SSH disabled after the App Connector is enrolled. [zpa.software-components.appliance:network-and-ssh] The procedure stops the zpa-connector service and creates a provisioning key file with 644 permissions at /opt/zscaler/var/provision_key for an App Connector, and stops the Private Service Edge service and creates the file at /opt/zscaler/var/service-edge/provision_key for a Private Service Edge, then starts zpa-connector or zpa-service-edge with systemctl. If the provisioning key was not detected when the App Connector was first started, the App Connector is in a sleep cycle and looks for the key again every 24 hours. [zpa.software-components.appliance:key-files] After enrollment the page says to verify that the App Connector's Host Platform has ZS-ZPA-APPL-8010 and that the Private Service Edge's Host Platform is ZS-ZPA-PSE-8010, and to check that the appliance appears in the Appliances widget; the App Connector or Private Service Edge must have enrolled successfully at least once to appear within the Health dashboard. [zpa.software-components.appliance:verify-enrollment] The Host Platform string for a Private Service Edge appliance is worded differently on another page, see [zpa.private-service-edges.what-it-is:appliance-host-platform](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.what-it-is:appliance-host-platform).

What to check:
1. Whether the appliance model is the App Connector or the Private Service Edge one, before choosing which enrollment steps apply.
2. Whether a continuous beep means one power source is unplugged or a power supply is faulty.
3. Which port the appliance is cabled to, and whether link aggregation is in use, which is not supported.
