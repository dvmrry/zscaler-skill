# ZPA policy evaluation (zpa.policy)
Also called: zpa policy; zpa access policy; access policy precedence; zpa policy precedence; why was access blocked in zpa; zpa rule order; zpa first match; zpa policy rules

"Why was this ZPA request allowed or blocked?" needs five questions answered in order.

1. **Which policy types run, and in what order?** Client forwarding comes before access, a bypass skips access, and each type has a default when nothing matches: [Evaluation order across ZPA policy types](kb:zpa.policy.evaluation-order).
2. **Which application segment did the request map to?** The most specific one, unless Multimatch is on: [Segment matching and policy search with and without Multimatch](kb:zpa.policy.multimatch).
3. **Which rule is first?** First match by rule order, with the Deception exception: [Access policy rule order](kb:zpa.policy.access-rule-order).
4. **Do the rule's criteria match?** The criteria, their combinations and limits: [Criteria available on ZPA access policy rules](kb:zpa.policy.criteria); the AND and OR logic and operand forms: [Boolean logic and operand forms of ZPA policy conditions](kb:zpa.policy.conditions).
5. **What does the rule do?** The action and its prerequisites: [Rule actions on ZPA policy types](kb:zpa.policy.actions).

Segment matching is also covered in [Which application segment a destination maps to when several overlap](kb:zpa.app-segments.matching). Segment groups in rules are covered in [How access policy rules use segment groups](kb:zpa.segment-groups.policy-use), and microtenant evaluation in [Policy evaluation between Default and custom Microtenants](kb:zpa.microtenants.policy-evaluation). Identity criteria are covered in [How SAML attributes act as policy criteria](kb:zpa.saml-attributes.policy-criteria) and [SCIM groups and attributes as ZPA policy criteria](kb:zpa.scim.policy-criteria), with SCIM sync timing in [When SCIM data is ready for ZPA policy](kb:zpa.scim.provisioning-timing). Trusted networks, machine groups and federated rules are covered in [The TRUSTED_NETWORK operand in ZPA policy rules](kb:zpa.trusted-networks.policy-operand), [Using a machine group in a ZPA policy rule](kb:zpa.machine-groups.policy-use) and [Access policies for federated applications](kb:zpa.b2b-federation.access-policy).

Session timing is a separate rule set: [ZPA timeout policy rules](kb:zpa.policy.timeout-rules). Client forwarding rules are in [ZPA client forwarding policy rules: actions, precedence and criteria](kb:zpa.policy.forwarding-rules), redirection rules in [ZPA redirection policy rules: Private Service Edge selection methods and criteria](kb:zpa.policy.redirection-rules), and access policy global rules and policy groups in [ZPA access policy global rules and policy groups](kb:zpa.policy.policy-groups). Rule fields beyond criteria are in [Rule fields beyond criteria: reauth, posture notification and profile IDs](kb:zpa.policy.rule-fields). SCIM criteria depend on sync timing: [SCIM sync timing before SCIM criteria are used](kb:zpa.policy.scim-sync). Common rule layouts are in [Common ZPA access rule patterns and their ordering](kb:zpa.policy.patterns). Which rules and segments are used, and how user groups map to applications: [Usage insights: policy and segment usage, user-group relationships and quarterly reports](kb:zpa.policy.usage-insights).

How Terraform orders and writes these rules is in [How the ZPA Terraform provider applies policy rule order (BulkReorder)](kb:zpa.terraform.rule-order) and [What the ZPA Terraform provider sends and loses when writing policy rules](kb:zpa.terraform.policy-rule-writes). The server group a rule may name is in [ZPA server groups](kb:zpa.server-groups). The VPN (for Legacy Apps) rules have their own subjects: [VPN access policy rules: source, destination, default block, rule order and limits](kb:zpa.vpn.access-policy) and [VPN user enablement rules: who may open a VPN tunnel](kb:zpa.vpn.user-enablement).
