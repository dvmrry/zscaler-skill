# Disaster recovery mode and Private Service Edge groups (zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery)
Also called: use_in_dr_mode; exclusive_for_business_continuity; pse disaster recovery; dr mode private service edge; disaster recovery dns txt record; private access outage pse; business continuity pse

Disaster recovery is for a global Private Access outage and is switched on by DNS records, not by group health.

**What it is.** Disaster recovery provides Business Continuity when a disaster impacts the global Zscaler cloud infrastructure; Private Access supports it separately from Internet & SaaS. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:dr-purpose] To provide application access during a global outage, designated application segments, Private Service Edges and App Connectors must be enabled for disaster recovery, and a disaster recovery domain name must be configured in the Disaster Recovery Settings and the Client Connector profiles. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:dr-designation] A Private Service Edge group shows whether it is enabled for disaster recovery, and the Terraform use_in_dr_mode field "Whether or not the App Connector Group is designated for disaster recovery" is the group setting. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:group-dr-field] On the Add Private Service Edge page, Disaster Recovery designates the Private Service Edge group for disaster recovery and is disabled by default; groups designated for disaster recovery bypass the Private Access cloud to ensure business continuity in a disaster scenario, and Disaster Recovery Mode is triggered when the DNS TXT records are uploaded to the DNS server for the disaster recovery domain name. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:pse-group-dr-default] Disaster recovery application segments are application segments designated as available for disaster recovery; the Disaster Recovery Application Segments page lists them and links to the Disaster Recovery Settings page, where the authentication, disaster recovery public key and disaster recovery domain name are configured. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:dr-application-segments-page] A second field exists in the Go type [gap:zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:exclusive-for-business-continuity].

**Activation.** Disaster recovery is activated by uploading DNS TXT records to the Disaster Recovery domain name, which disrupts existing Client Connector connections; DNS A records are how Client Connector discovers the Private Service Edges, and enabled App Connectors and Private Service Edges restart on activation. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:dr-activation] In disaster recovery mode Client Connector forwards the M-Tunnels of the applications designated for disaster recovery to the Private Service Edge. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:dr-forwarding] Disaster Recovery Test Mode lets an organization test its business continuity processes; the Admin Console stays accessible, and changes made there do not take effect until test mode is disabled via the DNS records or the activation record expires. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:dr-test-mode] Whether designated groups are held out of normal selection is unverified [gap:zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:dr-reserve-semantics].

**During the outage.** Designated application segments are accessible in disaster recovery mode, and existing and disaster-recovery-generated App Connectors, Private Service Edges and Client Connectors with valid, unexpired certificates are supported, as are users enrolled before it was enabled. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:dr-supported] Disaster recovery mode does not support enrollment of new App Connectors, Private Service Edges or Client Connectors, authentication and reauthentication changes (SAML assertion validity is extended by 14 days by default), policies, Log Streaming Service, Browser Access, dashboards and diagnostics, among others. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:dr-not-supported] In disaster recovery mode, Private Service Edges and App Connectors mapped within a default or custom Microtenant provide traffic only to applications designated for disaster recovery. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:dr-microtenants]

**Configuring it.** Disaster Recovery must be enabled in the application segment, App Connector group and Private Service Edge group for disaster recovery to work; an application segment with disaster recovery enabled must be associated with a server group that is associated with at least one App Connector group designated for disaster recovery. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:enable-chain] The disaster recovery domain name is an administrator-controlled domain or subdomain used to trigger or exit Disaster Recovery Mode; it hosts a TXT record name and an A record name, and disaster recovery is triggered or exited through the TXT record. The IP addresses of all disaster-recovery-enabled Private Service Edges go into the A records under it, and Client Connector uses the domain to connect to the Private Service Edge based on the A records in the DNS response. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:domain-name] All the public IP addresses of the Private Service Edges designated for disaster recovery are captured into the A record for the disaster recovery domain name, and a Private Service Edge's public IP is found on the Private Service Edges page. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:a-records] The DNS Record Generator creates both signed and unsigned public and private keys but only the public key needs to be uploaded; it verifies that the DNS name is signed with the selected private key and is only applicable if the DNS TXT records are signed. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:public-key] Both signed and unsigned DNS TXT records are supported for disaster recovery activation. The Configuring Disaster Recovery page has the reader create a TXT record with On, or with Test, as the disaster recovery status when using the Zscaler DNS Record Generator, and with Off as the status in its procedure to disable Disaster Recovery Mode and Test Mode. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:txt-record-states] Disaster Recovery Mode is turned off when the DNS time to live (TTL) expires, and Zscaler does not recommend very high or very low TTL values (for example, greater than 15 minutes or less than 30 seconds). [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:ttl] The maximum age for authentication is entered in days, weeks or months and is how long current end user authentication is valid during Disaster Recovery Mode; the default is 14 days. It is extended starting from the time the user authenticated, not added to the end of the authentication period or after the SAML token has expired, and Zscaler recommends it be greater than or equal to the Authentication Timeout value of the timeout policy. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:max-age-for-authentication] What two page phrases mean is open [gap:zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:dr-config-wording]. The record format and the generator are in [Disaster recovery activation TXT records and the Zscaler DNS Record Generator](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery.dns-records).

**Mode behavior.** In Disaster Recovery Mode, all App Connectors in a group designated for disaster recovery restart their zpa-connector services and all Private Service Edges in a designated group restart their zpa-service-edge services; when the services restart, existing user connections to the test App Connectors and Private Service Edges designated for disaster recovery are dropped. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:service-restart-drops] During active Disaster Recovery Mode, App Connectors and Private Service Edges are disconnected from the Zscaler cloud, and the Zscaler Admin Console is not supported. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:dr-mode-disconnected] In Disaster Recovery Test Mode, designated App Connectors restart their zpa-connector services. New connections for regular users who are not in the disaster recovery test group are routed through Public Service Edges or regular Private Service Edges and App Connectors not designated for disaster recovery, while test-group users also form new connections to the ones in test mode; Zscaler says to create a separate App Profile for Test Mode and designate specific users. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:test-mode-routing] During non-disaster recovery conditions, Private Service Edges and App Connectors designated for disaster recovery are used for application access; after activation only the designated ones are used during active Disaster Recovery Mode; during active Test Mode normal cloud access and disaster recovery access happen concurrently. Normal App Connector and Private Service Edge resource specifications apply to the designated ones. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:sizing-partition] If a Microtenant is used, activating disaster recovery provides access to all applications designated for disaster recovery. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:microtenant-activation-scope]

**Snapshots.** After an App Connector group or Private Service Edge group is designated for disaster recovery, a configuration and binary snapshot is saved in the local configuration files on the App Connector or Private Service Edge. They are used when an admin wants to fix a misconfiguration during a disaster scenario, and an older binary snapshot can be installed if the binary is not compatible with an older configuration snapshot (the binary fails to start up or logs unexpected errors). [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:snapshots-purpose] Configuration snapshots contain application_conf.json (application-specific configuration data), customer_conf.json (customer-specific configuration data), override_conf.json (override data) and other_conf.json (uncategorized configuration data, such as App Connector-specific configuration data within a Private Service Edge). [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:snapshot-files] App Connector snapshots are in /opt/zscaler/var/config_snapshots and /opt/zscaler/var/binary_snapshots; Private Service Edge snapshots are in /opt/zscaler/var/service-edge/config_snapshots and /opt/zscaler/var/service-edge/binary_snapshots. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:snapshot-paths] The Zscaler cloud maintains up to 15 prior configuration snapshots and up to 5 prior binary snapshots and updates both periodically; Zscaler Support can be contacted to change the limits. If the disk space utilization of the App Connector or Private Service Edge exceeds 90%, the snapshots used for disaster recovery are not captured. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:snapshot-retention] The snapshots page's procedure first ensures Disaster Recovery Mode is enabled on the App Connector group or Private Service Edge group, then has a command entered in /opt/zscaler/var for App Connectors or /opt/zscaler/var/service-edge/ for Private Service Edges, where pressing 1 or 2 lists the configuration or binary snapshots and pressing 1 or 2 installs one; the service restarts after the snapshot is installed. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:snapshot-script] Configuration snapshots are organized in directories by date and time as config-<YYYY>-<MM><DD>, and binary snapshots are versioned as binary-<YYYY>-<MM><DD>_ver<binary_version>; an older binary snapshot may be needed if the current binary version does not match the one captured in the configuration snapshot. [zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:snapshot-format] The group pages word the retention limits differently [divergence:zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:snapshot-wording].

The Business Continuity construct for Private Service Edges, Private Clouds, is in [Private Cloud Controllers and Private Clouds as the Business Continuity layer](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.private-cloud-controller) and [Business Continuity for Private Access: prerequisites, domain names, settings, entering and exiting, limitations](kb:zpa.private-service-edges.business-continuity).

What to check:
1. Whether the question is about a global Private Access outage: disaster recovery is for that case and is triggered by DNS records, not by Private Service Edge health.
2. Whether the group, the App Connector group and the application segments are all designated for disaster recovery.
3. Whether a new Private Service Edge needs to be enrolled, which disaster recovery mode does not support.
4. Whether the tenant uses Microtenants, whose Private Service Edges then serve only the applications designated for disaster recovery.

Sources disagree (name both sides):
- The App Connector group page and the snapshots page say the cloud keeps up to 15 prior configuration and 5 prior binary snapshots; the Private Service Edge group page says 15 active and 5 active. [divergence:zpa.private-service-edges.disaster-recovery:snapshot-wording]
  - zscaler-help: Up to 15 prior configuration snapshots and up to 5 prior binary snapshots (App Connector groups).
  - zscaler-help: Up to 15 active configuration snapshots and up to 5 active binary snapshots (Private Service Edge groups).
  Impact: The numbers agree; the pages word what the limit counts differently, prior snapshots or active ones.
