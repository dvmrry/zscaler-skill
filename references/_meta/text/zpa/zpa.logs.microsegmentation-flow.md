# What a ZPA Microsegmentation Flow log record says and does not say (zpa.logs.microsegmentation-flow)
Also called: zms_flow_log record; microsegmentation flow log; EnforcementDisposition; EnforcementAction SIMBLOCK; EnforcementReason NO_POLICY_EXISTS; AppZoneName; SourcePorts aggregated; east-west flow log
Former IDs: log-18

The Microsegmentation Flow log is generated by Microsegmentation agents and describes network flows with a policy decision. Its enforcement fields have value lists but no documented combinations.

**What a record is.** A flow record comes from a Microsegmentation agent: ResourceID, ResourceName and AppZone fields describe the agent generating the flow log, and ConnectionStartTime is the timestamp when the earliest of an aggregated set of connections was initiated. [zpa.logs.microsegmentation-flow:agent-generated-aggregated] SourcePorts is the source port of the connection, and for multiple connections between the same endpoints (same source, destination address, protocol and destination port) it becomes an aggregated source port list. [zpa.logs.microsegmentation-flow:source-ports-aggregated]

**Arrays.** In the example record SourcePorts, AppName and AppExecutablePath are JSON arrays, although AppName and AppExecutablePath are described as a single application name and path. [zpa.logs.microsegmentation-flow:array-valued-sample] Protocol is the transport protocol used in the connection with a numeric specifier, and the example record has Protocol 6 and DestinationPort 1443 as numbers. [zpa.logs.microsegmentation-flow:protocol-numeric]

**Enforcement.** EnforcementReason allows POLICY_DISABLED, RULE, NO_POLICY_EXISTS and FORCED; EnforcementAction allows ALLOW, BLOCK and SIMBLOCK; EnforcementDisposition allows UNKNOWN, CONNECTED, REJECTED and DROPPED; and Direction allows UNKNOWN, INBOUND and OUTBOUND. [zpa.logs.microsegmentation-flow:enforcement-enums] EnforcementReason is described as the policy enforcement configured on the connection, EnforcementAction as the policy enforcement action configured on the connection, and EnforcementDisposition as the policy enforcement decision on the connection. [zpa.logs.microsegmentation-flow:enforcement-descriptions] The combinations are open [gap:zpa.logs.microsegmentation-flow:enforcement-triple]. Reading blocks as lateral movement is not sourced [gap:zpa.logs.microsegmentation-flow:lateral-movement-reading].

What to check:
1. Whether a SIEM rule for blocked flows relies on one enforcement field alone, given the combinations are undocumented.
2. Whether SourcePorts, AppName and AppExecutablePath are parsed as arrays.

Not known (open gaps):
- What combinations of EnforcementReason, EnforcementAction and EnforcementDisposition mean [gap:zpa.logs.microsegmentation-flow:enforcement-triple]: settled by tenant-data or lab-test or support-ticket. Blocks: filtering for flows that were actually blocked versus flows that would be blocked
- Reading blocked east-west flows as lateral movement [gap:zpa.logs.microsegmentation-flow:lateral-movement-reading]: settled by lab-test. Blocks: triaging blocked Microsegmentation flows
