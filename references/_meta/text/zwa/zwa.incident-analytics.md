# Workflow Automation Incident Analytics dashboard: access by workflow permission, time ranges, KPI tiles, triage and resolve times, and top-10 views (zwa.incident-analytics)
Also called: incident analytics; incident analytics dashboard; time to triage; time to resolve; top 10 dlp admins

The Incident Analytics dashboard (Analytics > Data Security > Incidents) gives high-level visibility into the organization's DLP incidents over a timeframe, such as the time taken to triage and resolve incidents, previous and current incident counts, and the cumulative number of new and resolved incidents. [zwa.incident-analytics:what-it-is] The Workflow Automation 2024 release summary, in its January 18, 2024 entry, records that a new Incident Analytics page was added to the Workflow Automation Admin Portal; that is a dated release note, and the current dashboard page places the dashboard at Analytics > Data Security > Incidents. The Managing Incidents page points to the dashboard for DLP incident visibility. [zwa.incident-analytics:owner] The Incidents page itself is in [Workflow Automation incidents: where they come from, who gets them, statuses, search limits and closing or deleting](kb:zwa.incidents).

**Access.** Admins with full workflow access can monitor all incidents in the organization and view the admins responsible for them; admins with restricted workflow access can only monitor the incidents assigned to them and cannot view incidents assigned to other admins. The table of DLP admins with the most incidents assigned is only available to admins with full workflow access permission. [zwa.incident-analytics:workflow-access] If the User Name or Client IP attribute is chosen for obfuscation, the dashboard's incident data cannot be filtered using those obfuscated attributes. [zwa.incident-analytics:obfuscation] The dashboard exports to a PDF file, either everything available on the page or what the applied filter criteria show. [zwa.incident-analytics:export-pdf]

**Time range.** By default the dashboard displays the current calendar week (Sunday through Saturday); the time range filter applies to all widgets and offers hour, day, week and month presets or a Custom Date Range with start and end dates and times, which only supports incidents up to 6 months old and a search window of at most 3 months. [zwa.incident-analytics:time-range] The Incidents page's equivalent range is in [The Incidents page searches by System Creation Date and sorts by Incident Date by default, shows the current calendar week (Sunday through Saturday) by default, and its custom date range supports only incidents up to 6 months old within a window of at most three months.](kb:zwa.incidents:date-range). Retention beyond the six-month custom range and the time zone of ranges are not stated [gap:zwa.incident-analytics:range-details].

**Tiles and charts.** Summary tiles give All (total incidents), Open and Resolved for the selected range, New and Resolved Incident Rate per day, and Response Available (incidents where a user or a manager or approver has responded, with the status Received Justification Response). [zwa.incident-analytics:summary-tiles] The Response Available status is also held in [The Open widget counts incidents with status New, Investigating or Received Justification Response; Waiting Feedback counts Validating with User or Escalated; Response Available counts Received Justification Response.](kb:zwa.incidents:status-widgets). Time to Triage is the time in hours from an incident's occurrence to its assignment to an admin, and Time to Resolve the time in hours for the admin to mark it resolved; the lighter blue bars show the previous time for the equivalent previous time range. [zwa.incident-analytics:triage-resolve] Incident by Status charts New, Open, Escalated and Resolved incidents, with darker blue bars for the current count and lighter blue bars for the previous count; a Pareto chart shows the new incident count with the cumulative new and cumulative resolved incident counts. [zwa.incident-analytics:status-and-trend]

**Top 10 views.** Top 10 lists cover the most violated DLP policy rules and DLP dictionaries, and the DLP policy rules and dictionaries with the largest number of resolved incidents marked as false positives. [zwa.incident-analytics:top-10-lists] Top 10 DLP Admins lists the 10 admins with the most incidents assigned to them (login ID, new and resolved incidents, average resolution time in hours); Top 10 Users lists end users by email with total, new, escalated, critical and high incidents, and clicking a user email opens the Incidents page showing that end user's incidents, with other applied filters kept. [zwa.incident-analytics:top-10-admins-users]
