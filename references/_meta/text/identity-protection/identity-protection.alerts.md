# The Identity Protection Alerts page in the Identities menu: list, bulk update, alert drawer, verdicts and score calculation (identity-protection.alerts)
Also called: identities > alerts; identity protection alerts; alert verdict; alert score calculation; alert drawer

Identity Protection alerts notify about anomalous activities related to identities, giving the detected activity, associated user or entity and risk level; similar alerts are correlated and grouped into incidents. [identity-protection.alerts:purpose] Alerts aggregate data from different sources such as ITDR Change Detection AD and ITDR Change Detection Entra. [identity-protection.alerts:sources] Which other integrated products can supply alerts, and how alerts are produced from change detection, is open [gap:identity-protection.alerts:alert-production].

**The list and alert details.** The Alerts page (Identities > Alerts) shows the number of alerts triggered per integrated product, the distribution of alerts across severity score ranges, and the number of alerts detected, incidents created by grouping similar types of alerts, and active incidents. [identity-protection.alerts:page-summaries] On the Alerts page, system-saved or saved views can be selected, alerts searched, filtered and grouped (for example by alert first seen, alert last seen, alert tags), the table refreshed, the alert list exported as a CSV file, and columns changed. [identity-protection.alerts:page-actions] The About Alerts page says that viewing the list of alerts shows details, and for each alert lists ID, Severity (Critical, High, Low, Medium or None), Severity Score, Title, Created, Sources (alerts triggered by Zscaler have the Zscaler logo), Tactic and Technique (MITRE ATT&CK). [identity-protection.alerts:list-columns]

**Changing alerts.** Selecting alerts and clicking Update on the Alerts page changes Severity (Critical, High, Low, Medium or None), Status (Remediated, Acknowledged, Confirmed or Discovered), Title and Verdict (Benign, False Positive, Inconclusive, Malicious, Suspicious, or True Positive). [identity-protection.alerts:bulk-update] Selected similar alerts can be turned into an incident with Create Incident on the Alerts page. [identity-protection.alerts:create-incident] The Update dialog and the alert drawer list different verdict values [divergence:identity-protection.alerts:verdict-values-differ].

**The alert drawer.** The alert drawer's top panel shows Name, First Seen, Last Seen, Alert ID and Risk Score, which by default reflects the highest severity score among the findings detected on the asset; Alert Status can be set to Discovered, Acknowledged, Confirmed or Remediated, and changes are saved with Apply Changes. [identity-protection.alerts:drawer-top-panel] The alert drawer's Details tab shows Source (for example Okta), Frequency (count of alerts with the same name for the last month), Tactic, Technique, Incidents (number of incidents associated with the alert), Created Time, Verdict and Description. [identity-protection.alerts:drawer-details-fields] The alert drawer's Verdict is used to calculate the alert risk score, with the values No verdict, Benign, False Positive, Inconclusive, Malicious, Suspicious and True Positive. [identity-protection.alerts:verdict-values] The alert Score Calculation is the alert's cumulative score, calculated by summing the severity scores of the alert (Critical, High, Medium, Low) and the alert verdict and rounding the result, and can be used to prioritize alerts. [identity-protection.alerts:score-calculation] How the Risk Score and Score Calculation relate is unclear [divergence:identity-protection.alerts:risk-score-basis]. The alert drawer's Entities tab lists the users or assets that received alerts, with the three entities with the most alerts listed at the top. [identity-protection.alerts:entities-tab] The alert drawer's Related Incidents tab lists incidents with ID, Alerts (number of alerts associated with incidents), Incident Risk Mass (the risk mass calculated for the incident), Assignee and Status (Discovered, Acknowledged, Confirmed or Remediated), and can export to CSV. [identity-protection.alerts:related-incidents-tab] The incident statuses differ from the Incidents page [divergence:identity-protection.alerts:incident-statuses-differ].

**Open questions.** What the Discovered, Acknowledged, Confirmed and Remediated statuses mean, and the weights in the score, are open [gap:identity-protection.alerts:status-and-score].

Sources disagree (name both sides):
- The Alerts page's Update dialog lists the verdict values Accepted through Recommendation Status Unspecified, while the alert drawer lists No verdict through True Positive. [divergence:identity-protection.alerts:verdict-values-differ]
  - zscaler-help: The Update dialog's Verdict is one of Benign, False Positive, Inconclusive, Malicious, Suspicious, or True Positive.
  - zscaler-help: The alert drawer's Verdict is one of No verdict, Benign, False Positive, Inconclusive, Malicious, Suspicious or True Positive.
  Impact: An answer naming the values an admin can set as an alert's verdict depends on whether it is set from the Update dialog or from the alert drawer; neither page says the lists apply to different fields. The Incidents page's alert drawer example in [[soc-workbench.incidents]] (gap drawer-example-values) names the same Accepted, Executed and Partial values.
- The alert drawer describes the Risk Score as the highest severity score among findings on the asset, while also saying the verdict is used to calculate the alert risk score and that the Score Calculation sums severity scores and the verdict. [divergence:identity-protection.alerts:risk-score-basis]
  - zscaler-help: Risk Score by default reflects the highest severity score among the findings detected on the asset.
  - zscaler-help: The verdict is used to calculate the alert risk score, and the Score Calculation is a sum of severity scores and the verdict, rounded.
  Impact: An answer giving an alert's Risk Score as a maximum, a sum or a verdict-adjusted value depends on which description applies; the page does not say how the Risk Score and Score Calculation relate, and the 'highest severity' wording also appears for assets in [[aem.assets]] and [[soc-workbench.assets]].
- The alert drawer's Related Incidents tab lists four incident statuses including Remediated, while the Taking Action on Incidents page lists Discovered, Opened, Acknowledged, Not Remediated, Confirmed and Closed, with no Remediated. [divergence:identity-protection.alerts:incident-statuses-differ]
  - zscaler-help: An incident's Status is Discovered, Acknowledged, Confirmed or Remediated.
  - zscaler-help: Analysts and AI agents set incident statuses Discovered, Opened, Acknowledged, Not Remediated, Confirmed and Closed, and Confirmed means the incident is closed.
  Impact: An answer listing the statuses an incident can have depends on which page is followed; neither page says whether the Related Incidents tab and the Incidents page show the same incident object.
