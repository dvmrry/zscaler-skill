# Legacy Workplace settings: offices, occupancy and attendance goals, department mapping and workplace tags (business-insights.legacy.offices)
Also called: legacy offices; legacy occupancy goal; legacy attendance goal; department mapping divisions; workplace tags

**Offices list.** In the legacy Business Insights help, An admin account with read-only access can only view offices and use the View icon to see office configuration details. [business-insights.legacy.offices:office-read-only] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Offices list includes Building ID, Site ID, Size in square feet, Lease Status (leased or owned), Lease End Date, Permanent Desks, Hotel Desks, Capacity and Assigned Headcount. [business-insights.legacy.offices:office-list-fields] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Offices list shows an Occupancy Goal (60 means at least 60% of assigned users) and an Attendance Goal of 20, 40, 60, 80 or 100 for 1 to 5 days per week. [business-insights.legacy.offices:office-goal-columns] The occupancy denominator differs [divergence:business-insights.legacy.offices:goal-denominator]. In the legacy Business Insights help, On the Offices list, hovering over the Error icon next to an office name gives reasons such as incorrect configuration, missing metadata, or an office configured less than 24 hours ago. [business-insights.legacy.offices:office-error-icon]

**Configuring an office.** In the legacy Business Insights help, Capacity helps calculate an office's occupancy and Assigned Headcount helps calculate its attendance metrics. [business-insights.legacy.offices:office-capacity-headcount] In the legacy Business Insights help, A location newly added in the ZIA Admin Portal appears in the Add Office locations field after one day, and several IP addresses can be entered separated by commas. [business-insights.legacy.offices:office-new-locations] In the legacy Business Insights help, At most 8 tags can be manually linked to an office. [business-insights.legacy.offices:office-tag-limit] In the legacy Business Insights help, Once an office is configured the service aggregates data for its locations and IP addresses, and deleting it stops that aggregation and shows metrics for each ZIA location, sublocation and IP address individually. [business-insights.legacy.offices:office-delete-effect]

**Goals.** In the legacy Business Insights help, The organization-level occupancy goal is entered as a value between 1 and 100, and the organization-level attendance goal as 20, 40, 60, 80 or 100 for 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 days per week. [business-insights.legacy.offices:goal-values] In the legacy Business Insights help, The office-level occupancy goal is entered between 1 and 100 and the office-level attendance goal as 20, 40, 60, 80 or 100 for 1 to 5 days per week. [business-insights.legacy.offices:goal-office-values]

**Department mapping.** In the legacy Business Insights help, Up to 32 divisions can be created in Department Mapping. [business-insights.legacy.offices:department-mapping-limit] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Department Mapping list shows each Division, its Departments, the Number of Departments and the Employees mapped to the division. [business-insights.legacy.offices:department-mapping-columns]

**Workplace tags.** In the legacy Business Insights help, Workplace tags allow at most 8 tag categories with 8 tags each. [business-insights.legacy.offices:workplace-tag-limits] In the legacy Business Insights help, When manually tagging offices, only one tag in a category can be linked to an office. [business-insights.legacy.offices:workplace-tag-single] In the legacy Business Insights help, Dynamic workplace tag criteria are Assigned Headcounts, Capacity, Hotel Desks, Lease End Date, Lease Status and Permanent Desks, with operators greater than, greater than or equal to, less than, less than or equal to, equal to, Contains and Not Contains. [business-insights.legacy.offices:workplace-tag-criteria]

Whether the legacy Offices list has a BSSIDs column is open [gap:business-insights.legacy.offices:bssid-column]. Sources disagree (name both sides):
- The Offices list describes an occupancy goal against an office's assigned users, while the office configuration and terminology articles calculate occupancy from Capacity. [divergence:business-insights.legacy.offices:goal-denominator]
  - zscaler-help: An occupancy goal is described as the share of an office's assigned users expected to occupy it.
  - zscaler-help: Capacity, not Assigned Headcount, is the office value that helps calculate occupancy.
  Impact: Whether an occupancy goal is compared against assigned users or against Capacity is not stated; the Offices list and the occupancy formulas name different denominators.
