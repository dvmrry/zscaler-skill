# Legacy Business Insights contracts: All Contracts, upload, processing queue, contract settings and contract tags (business-insights.legacy.contracts)
Also called: business insights contracts; contract processing queue; contract file deletion; contract tags

**All Contracts.** In the legacy Business Insights help, Uploaded contracts are analyzed for licensing models (per user, per GB of data, feature based, or a combination), contract end and renewal date. [business-insights.legacy.contracts:licensing-models] In the legacy Business Insights help, On the All Contracts page the Contract Name is auto-generated and Seller Name is the software value added reseller (SVAR), which can equal the Vendor. [business-insights.legacy.contracts:contract-name-seller] In the legacy Business Insights help, A contract's Status is Active, Expired or Pending, where Pending means the subscription has not yet started but the contract is established. [business-insights.legacy.contracts:contract-status] In the legacy Business Insights help, All Contracts shows Cancel By (deadline for canceling), Renewal and an Opt-Out Date (when you can opt out before auto-renewal takes effect). [business-insights.legacy.contracts:contract-deadline-fields] In the legacy Business Insights help, The availability of the contract file on All Contracts depends on the Contract File Deletion settings on the Contract Settings page. [business-insights.legacy.contracts:contract-file-availability]

**Upload and queue.** In the legacy Business Insights help, Contract upload accepts multiple files per attempt in PDF, DOC and XLS formats, and processing takes minutes, hours or days depending on size and number. [business-insights.legacy.contracts:upload-formats] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Processing Queue Processing Status is In Progress, Error (a processing error) or Warning (contract not mapped to an application). [business-insights.legacy.contracts:queue-statuses] In the legacy Business Insights help, Only AI processed contracts are available for download from the Processing Queue, which filters by Status, Date Added, Uploaded By, Vendor or Applications. [business-insights.legacy.contracts:queue-download-filter] In the legacy Business Insights help, A queue Error caused by items without a linked application shows missing application linkage in the Application column; link an application from the drop-down or, if it is not listed, submit a Create New Application Request. [business-insights.legacy.contracts:queue-error-missing-app] An unmapped application is a Warning in one article and an Error in the other [divergence:business-insights.legacy.contracts:unmapped-application-status]. In the legacy Business Insights help, When the service cannot parse any relevant metadata, the Contract Information table shows no data and the fix is to reupload the contract file in the supported format. [business-insights.legacy.contracts:queue-error-no-metadata]

**Details and settings.** In the legacy Business Insights help, In the Contract Details drawer, ACV and TCV are shown in the default currency set on the Contract Settings page. [business-insights.legacy.contracts:contract-value-currency] In the legacy Business Insights help, Contract File Deletion deletes contract files permanently after a chosen number of days (for example 7, 14, 30, 60 or 90) counted from either the upload date or the expiration date. [business-insights.legacy.contracts:contract-retention]

**Contract tags.** In the legacy Business Insights help, Contract tags allow at most 8 tag categories, each with at most 8 tags. [business-insights.legacy.contracts:contract-tag-limits] In the legacy Business Insights help, When manually tagging contracts, only one tag in a category can be linked to a contract. [business-insights.legacy.contracts:contract-tag-single] In the legacy Business Insights help, Dynamic contract tag criteria are TCV, ACV, Seller Name, Application, Vendor and License, with operators greater than, greater than or equal to, less than, less than or equal to, equal to, Is, Is not, Contains and Not Contains. [business-insights.legacy.contracts:contract-tag-criteria]

Application details and application tags are in [Legacy Application Insights: discovered, overlapping and cost savings tabs, application details, custom signatures and application tags](kb:business-insights.legacy.applications); the area hub is [Legacy Business Insights help: architecture, data sources and terminology (hub)](kb:business-insights.legacy).
