# Legacy Workplace Insights: discovered locations, insights page, map and chart views and workplace details (business-insights.legacy.workplace)
Also called: legacy workplace insights; discovered locations; offices and locations map; footfall by time of day; remote user density

**Discovered Locations.** In the legacy Business Insights help, On the Discovered Locations list a ZIA location-based workplace takes its city from the ZIA location configuration, and when it is not specified Zscaler relies on a MaxMind lookup of the IP address. [business-insights.legacy.workplace:locations-city-source] In the legacy Business Insights help, A workplace with an undefined city, state or country is categorized as Unknown City, State or Country. [business-insights.legacy.workplace:locations-unknown] In the legacy Business Insights help, On the Discovered Locations list, Attendance is visits divided by the possible assigned headcount, times 100, and Occupancy is visits divided by the workplace capacity, times 100. [business-insights.legacy.workplace:locations-formulas] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Discovered Locations list includes Visits Per Day (average unique visits per day), Percent Mapped (share of locations, sub-locations and IP addresses mapped to the office) and Office Mappings, and locations can be ignored from analytics. [business-insights.legacy.workplace:locations-columns] In the legacy Business Insights help, Hovering over the Error icon next to a workplace name gives reasons such as incorrect office configuration, missing metadata, or an office configured less than 24 hours ago. [business-insights.legacy.workplace:locations-error-icon] In the legacy Business Insights help, Selecting User Types does not filter data unless user types are defined for the organization in Business Insights, and enabling the feature requires contacting Zscaler Support. [business-insights.legacy.workplace:user-types-filter]

**Workplace Insights page.** In the legacy Business Insights help, The Workplace Insights Time Range offers the last 7 days, a week, a month or 3 months, and data aggregation for the custom week, month or quarter might take some time to process. [business-insights.legacy.workplace:time-range-aggregation] In the legacy Business Insights help, By default Saturdays and Sundays are excluded from the Workplace Insights metrics, which are calculated considering a 5-day work week. [business-insights.legacy.workplace:weekends-five-day] In the legacy Business Insights help, On Workplace Insights the department filter is available unless at least one department is mapped to a division, and departments come from the ZIA Admin Portal SCIM. [business-insights.legacy.workplace:department-filter] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Workplace Insights Overall Averages can show either configured offices or inferred locations at one time. [business-insights.legacy.workplace:insights-one-type] In the legacy Business Insights help, On Workplace Insights, total possible capacity is the capacity of all selected offices multiplied by the official working days in the time frame (capacity 100 over 5 working days gives 500), and the page also compares occupancy against the previous period (MoM, WoW or QoQ). [business-insights.legacy.workplace:insights-possible-capacity] In the legacy Business Insights help, On the Workplace Insights and Offices and Locations chart pages, total possible assigned headcount is likewise computed as the capacity of all selected offices multiplied by the official working days in the time frame, and on the Workplace Details page as the workplace capacity multiplied by the official working days. [business-insights.legacy.workplace:insights-possible-headcount] The headcount basis differs from the office articles [divergence:business-insights.legacy.workplace:possible-headcount-basis]. In the legacy Business Insights help, On Workplace Insights, the data in the Overall Averages section filtered to inferred offices is estimated because the service lacks precise metadata, and Zscaler recommends configuring offices; updated office metadata appears in Workplace Insights after the daily service scan. [business-insights.legacy.workplace:insights-inferred-estimated] In the legacy Business Insights help, Top Cities shows cities by number of employees and office-visit frequency in three bands: remote, up to 2 days a week and more than 2 days a week. [business-insights.legacy.workplace:insights-top-cities] In the legacy Business Insights help, Remote User Density lists Potential Sites Opportunities, cities with the number and percentage of remote users where an office could be opened. [business-insights.legacy.workplace:insights-remote-density]

**Map and chart.** In the legacy Business Insights help, A configured office missing from the map lacks metadata, and city, state or province and country must be specified when configuring the office. [business-insights.legacy.workplace:map-missing-office] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Show ZIA Sub-locations toggle shows ZIA sub-locations in the list and is available when filtering for inferred location-based workplaces. [business-insights.legacy.workplace:map-show-sublocations] In the legacy Business Insights help, In the Offices and Locations chart view, occupancy by day of the week is visits that day divided by possible capacity times 100, and attendance by day of the week is visits that day divided by possible assigned headcount times 100. [business-insights.legacy.workplace:chart-day-of-week-formulas] The per-day attendance basis differs by page [divergence:business-insights.legacy.workplace:attendance-by-day-basis].

**Workplace Details.** In the legacy Business Insights help, On the Workplace Details page, occupancy by day of the week is users who came into the workplace that day divided by capacity times 100, and attendance by day of the week is users who came that day divided by users assigned times 100. [business-insights.legacy.workplace:details-day-of-week-formulas] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Footfall by the Time of the Day section shows hourly footfall averaged for 7 days of the week when monthly data is selected, and its CSV export is available when the Time Range is Last 7 days or Week. [business-insights.legacy.workplace:details-footfall] In the legacy Business Insights help, On the Workplace Details page, Attendance by Days per Week is a pie chart of the percentage of users and their count who came to the workplace from less than a day to 7 days a week, and Attendance by Department can include unassigned users via Show Unassigned Users. [business-insights.legacy.workplace:details-days-per-week]

Office configuration, goals, department mapping and workplace tags are in [Legacy Workplace settings: offices, occupancy and attendance goals, department mapping and workplace tags](kb:business-insights.legacy.offices), the current Workplace UI is in [Business Insights Workplace Insights: discovered locations, map and chart views, workplace details, filters and occupancy and attendance formulas](kb:business-insights.workplace), and the area hub is [Legacy Business Insights help: architecture, data sources and terminology (hub)](kb:business-insights.legacy).

Sources disagree (name both sides):
- The Workplace Insights, chart and Workplace Details pages compute total possible assigned headcount from office capacity, while the terminology and office configuration articles define Assigned Headcount as the users assigned to an office. [divergence:business-insights.legacy.workplace:possible-headcount-basis]
  - zscaler-help: Total possible assigned headcount is the capacity of the selected offices times the official working days.
  - zscaler-help: Assigned Headcount is the number of users assigned to an office.
  - zscaler-help: Assigned Headcount is a number entered for the office, separate from Capacity, and helps calculate attendance.
  Impact: Whether the attendance denominator is built from capacity or from the assigned headcount entered for an office is not stated consistently.
- The three pages that describe Attendance by Day of the Week count visits, users who came, or headcount differently, and only two of them multiply by 100. [divergence:business-insights.legacy.workplace:attendance-by-day-basis]
  - zscaler-help: The chart page divides visits on that day by the total possible assigned headcount, times 100.
  - zscaler-help: The Workplace Details page divides users who came that day by the users assigned, times 100.
  - zscaler-help: The Workplace Insights page divides visits on that day by the headcount assigned, with no multiplication by 100.
  Impact: Whether the per-day attendance counts visits or distinct users, and whether the denominator is possible assigned headcount or assigned users, differs by page.
