# Legacy Business Insights reports: system, workplace, application, exclusion list and Data Explorer (business-insights.legacy.reports)
Also called: legacy business insights reports; scheduled reports business insights; exclusion list business insights; data explorer views

**System reports.** In the legacy Business Insights help, System Generated Reports are generated monthly: an Applications Report and a Workplaces Report, both AI-powered assessments. [business-insights.legacy.reports:system-reports]

**Workplace reports.** In the legacy Business Insights help, Scheduled workplace reports (Analytics > Reports > Workplace Reports) are emailed to recipients as links, and the list shows each report's Report Name, an autogenerated Summary and the Next Run date; a report can be enabled, disabled, edited, downloaded or deleted. [business-insights.legacy.reports:workplace-report-list] In the legacy Business Insights help, Scheduled workplace reports have a Time Period of Daily, Weekly or Monthly and an email schedule of Daily, Weekly on Mondays or Monthly on First Monday. [business-insights.legacy.reports:workplace-report-delivery] In the legacy Business Insights help, A workplace report's Data Aggregated value is Daily, Weekly or Monthly and must be less than or equal to the When - Time Period value. [business-insights.legacy.reports:workplace-report-aggregation] In the legacy Business Insights help, Workplace report location options are All Offices (excluding remote users), Select Offices, All Offices and Remote Users, and Remote Users, where users with zero office visits are categorized as remote users. [business-insights.legacy.reports:workplace-report-where] In the legacy Business Insights help, A workplace report includes All Users, Users by Department or Users by Division, and when the data is organized by Office the report shows Visits (the number of visits on that date), and when it is organized by User it shows Timespan (the time spent in the office during the visit). [business-insights.legacy.reports:workplace-report-who-organize]

**Application reports.** In the legacy Business Insights help, An application report is an Inactivity Period or Activity Period report with a cadence of 1 day, 30 days, 60 days or 90 days, scheduled Daily, Weekly, Monthly or Quarterly. [business-insights.legacy.reports:application-report-types] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Application Reports list describes Delivery Schedule as Weekly, Monthly or Quarterly. [business-insights.legacy.reports:application-report-list-schedule] The Daily schedule is stated differently [divergence:business-insights.legacy.reports:application-report-schedule-values]. In the legacy Business Insights help, An application report that includes a subset of users can select up to 128 users and departments. [business-insights.legacy.reports:application-report-subset] In the legacy Business Insights help, Application reports can be delivered by Workflow Automation, email or both, and the Workflow Automation integration can be tested by entering recipient emails and clicking Submit Test. [business-insights.legacy.reports:application-report-delivery-method] In the legacy Business Insights help, An application report can exclude users via the Exclusion List and/or a Custom list; modifying the exclusion list affects every scheduled report that applies it, so Zscaler recommends Custom for report-specific exclusions. [business-insights.legacy.reports:application-report-exclusions]

**Exclusion list.** In the legacy Business Insights help, An exclusion, added at Analytics > Reports > Application Reports > Exclusion List, sets criteria for excluding users and departments from a scheduled report through its Users, Departments, Applications and Enabled columns; if the exclusion list is applied when scheduling a report, the report excludes the users and departments. [business-insights.legacy.reports:exclusion-fields] In the legacy Business Insights help, Multiple exclusions can be added but only up to 8 can be enabled at any time, and an enabled exclusion is associated only with scheduled reports whose applications match the exclusion's. [business-insights.legacy.reports:exclusion-limit] In the legacy Business Insights help, When an exclusion is disabled, modified or enabled, scheduled reports that use the exclusion list are generated with the latest list, and the Exclusion List shows per exclusion the number of reports it is associated with. [business-insights.legacy.reports:exclusion-change-effect] In the legacy Business Insights help, Disabling an exclusion disassociates it from all scheduled reports using the exclusion list, and only enabled exclusions are associated with scheduled reports. [business-insights.legacy.reports:exclusion-disable] In the legacy Business Insights help, Disabling a scheduled report stops report generation and distribution, and enabling it resumes them. [business-insights.legacy.reports:enable-disable-report]

**Data Explorer.** In the legacy Business Insights help, A Data Explorer view groups by options including Department, Division, Geolocation and Work Mode, and the Aggregation Type function is not available for daily aggregation. [business-insights.legacy.reports:data-explorer-options] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Data Explorer Metrics section lists Bytes, Visits, Occupancy and Attendance, and filters can narrow a view by Division, Department and Work Mode. [business-insights.legacy.reports:data-explorer-metrics] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Data Explorer Time Period section offers Last 7 days, Week, Month or 3 Months. [business-insights.legacy.reports:data-explorer-time-period] In the legacy Business Insights help, Data Explorer reports can be scheduled only when the time period selected is Last Day, Last Week or Last Month. [business-insights.legacy.reports:data-explorer-schedule-condition] The schedulable periods are not among the offered ones [divergence:business-insights.legacy.reports:data-explorer-schedulable-periods]. In the legacy Business Insights help, Data Explorer results can be exported as a detailed report (Export Full Report) or a CSV file (Export). [business-insights.legacy.reports:data-explorer-export] In the legacy Business Insights help, A scheduled Data Explorer report is delivered Daily or Weekly to comma-separated email recipients. [business-insights.legacy.reports:data-explorer-delivery] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Data Explorer Reports page lists Name, Summary, Scheduled (active or not), Next Run, Last Updated and Last Updated By (the email address of the last editor). [business-insights.legacy.reports:data-explorer-reports-page]

The current report forms are in [Business Insights scheduled reports: workplace, application and Data Explorer reports, and the exclusion list](kb:business-insights.reports), and the area hub is [Legacy Business Insights help: architecture, data sources and terminology (hub)](kb:business-insights.legacy).

Sources disagree (name both sides):
- The scheduling article offers Daily among the application report delivery schedules, while the Application Reports list describes Delivery Schedule without Daily. [divergence:business-insights.legacy.reports:application-report-schedule-values]
  - zscaler-help: The delivery schedule is selected from Daily, Weekly, Monthly or Quarterly.
  - zscaler-help: The Delivery Schedule column is described as Weekly, Monthly or Quarterly.
  Impact: Whether an application report can be scheduled Daily is stated differently by the scheduling and list articles.
- The Data Explorer time periods offered in a view do not match the periods that the schedule condition names. [divergence:business-insights.legacy.reports:data-explorer-schedulable-periods]
  - zscaler-help: The Time Period section offers Last 7 days, Week, Month or 3 Months.
  - zscaler-help: Scheduling requires a time period of Last Day, Last Week or Last Month.
  Impact: The periods that allow scheduling (Last Day, Last Week, Last Month) are not among the periods the Time Period section lists, so which option permits scheduling is not stated.
