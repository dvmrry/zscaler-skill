# Legacy Business Insights Workflow Automation: SaaS application tenants and ServiceNow setup (business-insights.legacy.workflow)
Also called: business insights workflow automation; saas application tenants business insights; servicenow tenant business insights

**Tenants.** In the legacy Business Insights help, Workflow Automation tenants let, for example, a ServiceNow tenant create, manage and track tickets for inactive users observed via scheduled reports. [business-insights.legacy.workflow:tenant-purpose] In the legacy Business Insights help, A SaaS application is integrated with Workflow Automation either by adding it or by ensuring Workflow Automation is enabled for an already added application, which is done by selecting the Workflow Automation checkbox at Administration > Workflow Integration. [business-insights.legacy.workflow:integration-enable] In the legacy Business Insights help, A SaaS Application Tenant status is Active (in use), Inactive (not in use), Validation Failed (admin credentials or authorization could not be validated) or Reauthorization Required (edit the tenant and click Reauthorize). [business-insights.legacy.workflow:tenant-statuses] In the legacy Business Insights help, The tenant list shows an Owner (created in ZIA or Digital Experience) and the SaaS Security Data At Rest Scanning policies (DLP and Malware Detection) configured for the tenant. [business-insights.legacy.workflow:tenant-owner-policy] In the legacy Business Insights help, A SaaS application tenant name for Workflow Automation must be unique. [business-insights.legacy.workflow:tenant-name-unique]

**ServiceNow.** In the legacy Business Insights help, Configuring ServiceNow as a Workflow Automation tenant requires a ServiceNow user account with an admin role, the OAuth 2.0 plugin installed and active, and the com.snc.platform.security.oauth.is.active property set to true. [business-insights.legacy.workflow:servicenow-prerequisites] In the legacy Business Insights help, In the ServiceNow OAuth client for a Workflow Automation tenant, the Refresh Token Lifespan defaults to 8,640,000 seconds (100 days) and the Access Token Lifespan to 1,800 seconds (30 minutes), and Zscaler recommends larger values such as 157,700,000 seconds (5 years) and 86,400 seconds (24 hours). [business-insights.legacy.workflow:servicenow-token-lifespans] In the legacy Business Insights help, After the ServiceNow refresh token expires, ServiceNow must be configured as a new tenant. [business-insights.legacy.workflow:servicenow-refresh-reconfigure]

The validation error codes are listed for ZIA tenants in [CASB tenants: connecting a SaaS application, states and reauthorization](kb:zia.casb.tenants), and whether they apply to a Validation Failed tenant here is open [gap:business-insights.legacy.workflow:validation-error-codes]. Roles that grant Workflow Automation are in [Legacy Business Insights admin portal: activation, profile, remote assistance, roles, admins, audit logs and ZIA sync pages](kb:business-insights.legacy.admin); the area hub is [Legacy Business Insights help: architecture, data sources and terminology (hub)](kb:business-insights.legacy).
