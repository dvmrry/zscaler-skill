# Legacy Business Insights admin portal: activation, profile, remote assistance, roles, admins, audit logs and ZIA sync pages (business-insights.legacy.admin)
Also called: business insights admin portal; force activate business insights; remote assistance business insights; business insights admin roles

**Activation.** In the legacy Business Insights help, Configuration changes are saved and then activated to push them to the Central Authority; with concurrent edits of one setting only the last saved configuration persists, and activated changes cannot be undone, only deleted or modified and activated again. [business-insights.legacy.admin:activation-two-step] In the legacy Business Insights help, If other admins are still editing, an admin's activation is queued and pushed to the CA after all admins activate; a queued activation cannot be canceled, only super admins can Force Activate, and saved changes are activated automatically after 30 minutes of inactivity or on logout. [business-insights.legacy.admin:activation-queue] In the legacy Business Insights help, My Activation Status shows No Activations Pending, Editing or Activation Queued. [business-insights.legacy.admin:activation-statuses] In the legacy Business Insights help, Making changes across multiple browser sessions with the same admin account can cause activation errors. [business-insights.legacy.admin:single-browser-session]

**Your account.** In the legacy Business Insights help, Zscaler Support configures a super admin role with access to all functions in the portal, and the portal shows an EUSA on first login. [business-insights.legacy.admin:initial-super-admin] In the legacy Business Insights help, The My Profile password must be at least 8 ASCII characters with a number, an uppercase character and a special character, the portal defaults to English with French, German, Japanese, Spanish or Simplified Chinese available, and Auto Refresh Dashboard refreshes every 15 minutes. [business-insights.legacy.admin:profile-settings] In the legacy Business Insights help, The service saves transactions in UTC, and the profile time zone is used only when exporting logs to CSV files. [business-insights.legacy.admin:profile-timezone] The audit log CSV timezone differs [divergence:business-insights.legacy.admin:csv-export-timezone]. In the legacy Business Insights help, After an admin changes their password, the current portal session expires and the service prompts a login with the new password. [business-insights.legacy.admin:password-change-session] In the legacy Business Insights help, Table column settings are kept in the browser's local storage and are lost if the cache is cleared or another browser is used; tables under Administration pages must keep at least one column visible. [business-insights.legacy.admin:table-settings]

**Remote assistance.** In the legacy Business Insights help, Remote Assistance lets Zscaler Support log in with view-only or full admin privileges until a chosen date, without new admin accounts or shared passwords; Zscaler recommends at least one year for view-only and at least two days for full access. [business-insights.legacy.admin:remote-assistance] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Remote Assistance User Names option defaults to Visible, SSO users cannot change it, and only admins whose role enables Remote Assistance Management can change the Remote Assistance window. [business-insights.legacy.admin:remote-assistance-usernames]

**Roles.** In the legacy Business Insights help, Role-based administration lets an admin be given a scope and a permission level so they can reach only their designated areas, and the page's steps are adding admin roles and then adding admins; for an account subscribed to Authentication Service, the admins are configured in the Authentication Service Admin Portal. [business-insights.legacy.admin:rba-order] In the legacy Business Insights help, Zscaler provides a default super admin role with full access to the portal, and default admin roles can only be viewed, not edited. [business-insights.legacy.admin:default-roles] In the legacy Business Insights help, Configuring an admin role requires the proper permissions and organizational scope. [business-insights.legacy.admin:role-prerequisites] In the legacy Business Insights help, A role's permissions are Full (view, add, edit, delete), View Only, None, Visible or Obfuscated, depending on the feature. [business-insights.legacy.admin:role-permission-values] In the legacy Business Insights help, Each role needs permissions in General Permissions (administrator management, remote assistance management, ZIA user management, ZIA location management), Module Permissions, Feature Permissions and Data Visibility. [business-insights.legacy.admin:role-permission-categories] In the legacy Business Insights help, Module Permissions cover Application Insights, All Applications, Application Settings, Workplaces and All Contracts, and Feature Permissions cover scheduled reports, Workflow Automation and system-generated reports. [business-insights.legacy.admin:role-module-feature-permissions]

**Admins.** In the legacy Business Insights help, The default admin account can be enabled or disabled but not deleted, and for an account subscribed to Authentication Service, whose admins are configured in the Authentication Service Admin Portal, the Administrators page allows editing only an existing admin's scope. [business-insights.legacy.admin:default-admin] In the legacy Business Insights help, An admin's scope is Organization (all areas), Offices (specific configured offices) or Office Geolocations (offices by geography). [business-insights.legacy.admin:admin-scope-options] In the legacy Business Insights help, An admin's comments cannot exceed 10,240 characters, and a password for password-based login is 8 to 100 characters with a number, a special character and an uppercase letter. [business-insights.legacy.admin:admin-field-limits] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Administrators list shows each admin's Login Type, which is SAML SSO, direct password login, or both. [business-insights.legacy.admin:admin-login-type]

**Audit logs.** In the legacy Business Insights help, Audit Logs filter by Time Range, Action, Category, Sub-Category or Result, search by Resource, Admin ID or Client IP, and show an Interface that is currently BI UI for all logs. [business-insights.legacy.admin:audit-log-fields] In the legacy Business Insights help, Timestamps in the audit log CSV download are in PDT, and logs for certain changes show previous values in red and updated values in green. [business-insights.legacy.admin:audit-log-export]

**ZIA sync pages.** In the legacy Business Insights help, The ZIA Users page shows user data synced from the ZIA tenant, with a Status of Enabled or Disabled. [business-insights.legacy.admin:zia-sync-pages] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Departments page (Administration > ZIA Users > Departments) shows the users' department data synced from the ZIA tenant, with each department's Name and Comments; department changes made on the User Management pages in the ZIA Admin Portal are synced to it. [business-insights.legacy.admin:departments-page] In the legacy Business Insights help, The ZIA Locations page lists, per location, the number of sublocations and the static IP addresses of its local gateway, and can be downloaded to CSV. [business-insights.legacy.admin:zia-locations-fields]

**Navigation.** In the legacy Business Insights help, Portal search covers the menus in the left-hand navigation, except items in the Help menu, and does not cover tabs, field names, toggle names, tooltips or other text. [business-insights.legacy.admin:portal-search-scope] In the legacy Business Insights help, Help Browser customizations (position, size, font size) are saved until the admin logs out or the session expires, and closing the Help Browser clears its navigation history. [business-insights.legacy.admin:help-browser-session]

Sign-in, password expiration, SCIM and SAML are in [Legacy Business Insights admin authentication: password expiration, SCIM, SAML, IdP guides and sign-in OTP timeouts](kb:business-insights.legacy.authentication), and Workflow Automation tenants are in [Legacy Business Insights Workflow Automation: SaaS application tenants and ServiceNow setup](kb:business-insights.legacy.workflow).   - zscaler-help: The time zone chosen in the admin profile is used only for exporting logs to CSV files.
  - zscaler-help: Timestamps in the audit log CSV file are in PDT.
  Impact: Whether an audit log CSV export uses the admin's profile time zone or PDT is not stated.
