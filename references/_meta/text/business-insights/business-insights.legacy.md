# Legacy Business Insights help: architecture, data sources and terminology (hub) (business-insights.legacy)
Also called: legacy business insights; legacy zbi; business insights admin portal; legacy engagement index; legacy growth trend; high usage apps; low usage apps

**Prerequisites.** In the legacy Business Insights help, SaaS app insights require a Zscaler Internet Access (ZIA) subscription; workplace insights also require Zscaler Client Connector deployed on the relevant endpoints. [business-insights.legacy:prerequisites]

**Data sources.** In the legacy Business Insights help, Business Insights receives signals from 4 data sources: ZIA, identity providers such as Okta and Entra ID, SaaS application connector integrations for Okta, M365, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Slack, Box, Google and GitHub, and custom application signatures. [business-insights.legacy:data-sources] In the legacy Business Insights help, Zscaler can discover the usage of over 30K apps, but by default the portal shows only a subset depending on business relevance. [business-insights.legacy:discovery-scale] In the legacy Business Insights help, Business Insights concludes inferred IP addresses are a workplace based on the amount of traffic, the number of users behind the IP address, and more assuring metrics. [business-insights.legacy:inferred-location-basis]

**Application terms.** In the legacy Business Insights help, High Usage Apps have over 80% active users for the month compared to the total number of active users, and Low Usage Apps have less than 30%. [business-insights.legacy:usage-thresholds] In the legacy Business Insights help, Without plan details, the Engagement Index is the percentage of active users in the last 30 days over the last 90 days; with plan details it is active users in the last 30 days over purchased seats, falling back to assigned seats and then to the no-plan calculation, and showing the plan with the lowest index when several plans exist. [business-insights.legacy:engagement-index] In the legacy Business Insights help, Growth Trend is the percentage increase or decrease in user activity for every 30 days, calculated as the change in active users divided by the active users before the last 30 days, times 100. [business-insights.legacy:growth-trend] In the legacy Business Insights help, Active users have accessed or used the app at least once in the last 30 days; Purchased Seats is the seats bought within a subscription plan (one seat per user) and Assigned Seats is the seats assigned to users from them. [business-insights.legacy:active-users-seats]

**Workplace terms.** In the legacy Business Insights help, Workplaces are of two types, Inferred Locations (individual ZIA locations and inferred IP addresses) and Configured Offices (groupings of inferred locations with metadata). [business-insights.legacy:workplace-types] In the legacy Business Insights help, Average Attendance is total visits divided by total assigned headcount, times 100, and Average Occupancy is total visits divided by site capacity, times 100; Assigned Headcount is the users assigned to an office and Capacity is the employees it can accommodate. [business-insights.legacy:attendance-occupancy-terms]
