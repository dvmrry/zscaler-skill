# Legacy Application Insights: discovered, overlapping and cost savings tabs, application details, custom signatures and application tags (business-insights.legacy.applications)
Also called: legacy application insights; legacy saas efficiency matrix; custom application signatures; application tags business insights; licensing insights license usage funnel

**Application Insights tabs.** In the legacy Business Insights help, The Discovered Apps tab charts the number of high- and low-usage applications and newly discovered applications for the last 12 months, and lists the top 10 discovered applications. [business-insights.legacy.applications:discovered-apps-tab] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Data Source column names where usage was discovered: identity provider, ZIA, connector integration or custom application signature, shown as IDP, ZIA, CONNECTOR or CUSTOM ZIA, so one application tenant can appear several times; the Cost Savings tab's Data Source column names the source with IDP, ZIA or CONNECTOR as examples. [business-insights.legacy.applications:data-source-values] In the legacy Business Insights help, Until costs are entered, applications show Zscaler-estimated Spend that is underlined, conservative (enterprise premium solutions with over 50% discounts, scaled by deployment size), and savings based on estimates are marked with an asterisk. [business-insights.legacy.applications:cost-estimates] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Cost Savings tab lists the Top 50 Cost Savings Applications, where Cost Savings is the opportunity from removing inactive users, and plots a SaaS Efficiency Matrix by license usage percentage and expense with greater savings opportunities at the bottom right. [business-insights.legacy.applications:cost-savings-tab] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Overlapping Apps tab groups applications by capability, and Overlapping Users counts users using at least one of the applications listed for the capability. [business-insights.legacy.applications:overlapping-apps]

**Overlap details.** In the legacy Business Insights help, The Application Overlap Details page compares overlap metrics for up to 4 apps at a time and charts the weekly active user trend for each application. [business-insights.legacy.applications:overlap-details] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Application Comparison table shows the top 5 departments by app usage, Spend in US dollars and a Contract Date that is the date the app subscription ends. [business-insights.legacy.applications:overlap-comparison-columns]

**Application details.** In the legacy Business Insights help, The Application Details Status field shows whether the app is onboarded and, for connector-integrated apps, whether the API connection is valid or expired, and the Risk Index is configured in the ZIA Admin Portal. [business-insights.legacy.applications:details-status-risk] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Delete and Connect options on Application Details are available for connector-supported apps only. [business-insights.legacy.applications:details-delete-connect] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Application Details Users table filters by the last 30, 60 or 90 days (last month, 2 months or 3 months for the ZIA table), defaults to Active Users, shows the top 100 records by user activity and exports to CSV; the active vs. inactive graph filters by the last 30 days or 12 months. [business-insights.legacy.applications:details-users-graph-table] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Application Details Users table depends on the data source: ZIA discovered or CUSTOM ZIA apps add Total Bytes, Upload Bytes, Download Bytes and No. of Transactions, CONNECTOR apps add License Type, and IDP discovered apps have their own table. [business-insights.legacy.applications:details-users-by-source] In the legacy Business Insights help, For Microsoft 365, the User field on Application Details shows obfuscated data by default, and showing usernames requires enabling the setting in the Microsoft 365 admin center. [business-insights.legacy.applications:details-m365-obfuscated] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Application Details Contracts table filters by range operators greater than, less than, between, ends with, equal to and not equal to. [business-insights.legacy.applications:contracts-tab-filters] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Licensing Insights License Usage Funnel shows Purchased Seats split by SKU, Assigned Seats (applicable only to connector-based data sources) and Active Users (Last 90 days), filterable by data source and switchable between Plans and Contract views. [business-insights.legacy.applications:licensing-insights]

**Risk and compliance.** In the legacy Business Insights help, The Risk & Compliance tab has Hosting Information and Security sections and company details (headquarters, employees, year incorporated, application category, description), where a green thumbs up marks the desirable answer and a red thumbs down the undesirable one. [business-insights.legacy.applications:risk-compliance-sections] In the legacy Business Insights help, On the Risk & Compliance tab, Yes shows a thumbs down for Poor Terms of Service, Data Breaches in the Last 3 Years and File Sharing in Hosting Information, and for SSL Pinned, Evasive, Weak Cipher Support, Published CVE Vulnerability, Vulnerable to Heartbleed, Vulnerable to Poodle, Vulnerable to Logjam and Remote Access Screen Sharing in Security; Yes shows a thumbs up for Source IP Restrictions, MFA Support, Admin Audit Logs and Password Strength in Hosting Information, and for Data Encryption in Transit, HTTP Security Header Support, DNS CAA Policy, Valid SSL Certificate, Support for WAF, Vulnerability Disclosure Policy, Sender Policy Framework, DomainKeys Identified Mail, Domain-Based Message Authentication and Malware Scanning Content in Security. [business-insights.legacy.applications:risk-attribute-answers] In the legacy Business Insights help, In the Risk & Compliance Security section, an SSL certificate key size of less than 2048 bits shows a thumbs down and more than 2048 bits a thumbs up. [business-insights.legacy.applications:risk-ssl-key-size] The thumb for a key size of exactly 2048 bits is open [gap:business-insights.legacy.applications:risk-ssl-key-size-2048].

**Custom signatures.** In the legacy Business Insights help, Zscaler recommends configuring a SaaS application connector (which requires admin access to the app) for the supported apps, or creating custom application signatures to pull metrics from a specific URL of the application. [business-insights.legacy.applications:config-recommendation] In the legacy Business Insights help, You can configure 32 custom application signatures, each with one host and up to 10 URLs. [business-insights.legacy.applications:custom-signature-limits] In the legacy Business Insights help, Custom application signature metrics need SSL inspection of the traffic enabled; for example, without inspecting the traffic for the Miro application, metrics are not visible beyond the host www.miro.com. [business-insights.legacy.applications:custom-signature-ssl] In the legacy Business Insights help, Prefixes such as https://, http://, ftp:// and sftp:// are removed automatically from a custom signature host. [business-insights.legacy.applications:custom-signature-host] In the legacy Business Insights help, The Associated SaaS Application of a custom signature displays metadata for a known application and affects the Discovered Apps, Overlapping Apps and Cost Savings metrics. [business-insights.legacy.applications:custom-signature-associated-app] In the legacy Business Insights help, The custom application signature list on the Configuration page (Application > Application Settings) shows, for each signature, Application, Associated SaaS Application, Signature and Description. [business-insights.legacy.applications:signature-list-columns]

**Application tags.** In the legacy Business Insights help, Application tags allow at most 8 tag categories, each with at most 8 tags. [business-insights.legacy.applications:app-tag-limits] In the legacy Business Insights help, When manually tagging applications, only one tag in a category can be linked to an application. [business-insights.legacy.applications:app-tag-single] In the legacy Business Insights help, Dynamic application tag criteria are Engagement Index, Active Users, ACV, Growth Trend, Capability and Sanction Status, with operators greater than, greater than or equal to, less than, less than or equal to, equal to, Is, Is not, Contains and Not Contains. [business-insights.legacy.applications:app-tag-dynamic-criteria]

**Onboarding.** In the legacy Business Insights help, Add App at Application > Application Insights lists the connector-supported applications, and the Add icon on the All Applications page or the Top 10 Discovered Applications table is how to skip connector configuration for a connector-supported app. [business-insights.legacy.applications:onboarding-entry-points] The connector steps are in [Onboarding a SaaS application to Business Insights: connector or plan information, and per-application prerequisites](kb:business-insights.onboarding).

Contracts are in [Legacy Business Insights contracts: All Contracts, upload, processing queue, contract settings and contract tags](kb:business-insights.legacy.contracts), the current All Applications UI is in [Business Insights Application Insights: discovered apps, overlap, cost savings, All Applications and application details](kb:business-insights.applications), and the area hub is [Legacy Business Insights help: architecture, data sources and terminology (hub)](kb:business-insights.legacy).
