# Business Insights Workplace Insights: discovered locations, map and chart views, workplace details, filters and occupancy and attendance formulas (business-insights.workplace)
Also called: workplace insights; discovered locations; offices and locations; workplace details; occupancy; attendance; footfall

Workplace Insights has two types of workplaces: Discovered Locations (Internet & SaaS locations, including sub-locations, and inferred IP addresses) and Offices, which group inferred locations into a configured office with metadata. [business-insights.workplace:workplace-types] Zscaler recommends configuring offices with metadata for inferred-location workplaces for accurate insights; updated office metadata is reflected in Workplace Insights after the daily service scan, and the Overall Averages data filtered for inferred offices is estimated. [business-insights.workplace:recommend-office-metadata]

**Workplace Insights page.** The Workplace Insights time range offers the last 7 days, a week, a month or 3 months, and data aggregation for the custom week, month or quarter might take some time to process. [business-insights.workplace:time-range] By default, Saturdays and Sundays are excluded from the metrics on the Workplace Insights, Offices and Locations map, Offices and Locations chart and Workplace Details pages; on the Workplace Insights page, Custom Dates can include or exclude specific days. [business-insights.workplace:default-weekends-excluded] The Workplace Insights User Types filter does not filter the data unless user types are defined for the organization in Business Insights, and enabling it requires contacting Zscaler Support; the Workplace Details, Offices and Locations map and chart pages say the same. [business-insights.workplace:user-types-filter] On Workplace Insights, the department filter is available unless at least one department is mapped to a division on the Department Mapping page. [business-insights.workplace:department-division-filter] The Top Cities section of Workplace Insights shows cities by number of employees and office-visit frequency (remote, up to 2 days a week, more than 2 days a week), and can be exported to CSV. [business-insights.workplace:top-cities] The Remote User Density view lists Potential Sites Opportunities: cities with the number and percentage of remote users where an office could be opened. [business-insights.workplace:remote-user-density]

**Discovered Locations.** On the Discovered Locations list, a workplace with undefined city, state or country is categorized as Unknown; for Internet & SaaS location-based workplaces the City is mapped from the Internet & SaaS location configuration, with a MaxMind lookup used when no value is specified, and for configured offices it is mapped from the city value specified when configuring offices. [business-insights.workplace:discovered-locations-columns] The configured-office city conflicts with the removed office fields [divergence:business-insights.workplace:office-location-fields]. On the Discovered Locations list, Attendance is visits divided by the possible assigned headcount times 100 and Occupancy is visits divided by total capacity times 100, both estimated for inferred locations. [business-insights.workplace:list-view-formulas] When no data is shown for a workplace or office on the Discovered Locations and Offices pages, hovering over the Error icon next to its name shows the reason, for example that the office is not configured correctly, metadata is not added, or the office was configured less than 24 hours ago. [business-insights.workplace:error-icon-reasons]

**Offices and Locations map and chart.** The Offices and Locations map article says a configured office is missing from the map when its metadata is missing, and says to specify the city, state or province, and country when configuring offices to locate them on the map. [business-insights.workplace:map-missing-metadata] The office fields it names differ from the office configuration article [divergence:business-insights.workplace:office-location-fields]. The Offices and Locations map article says the Show ZIA Sub-locations toggle shows Internet & SaaS sub-locations in the list and is available when filtering for inferred location-based workplaces. [business-insights.workplace:show-zia-sublocations] The Offices and Locations chart can show data for either configured offices or inferred locations at one time, and the Workplace Insights page says the same. [business-insights.workplace:one-type-at-a-time]

**Workplace Details.** On the Workplace Details page, average occupancy is total visits divided by workplace capacity for the period, times 100, where possible capacity is capacity multiplied by the official working days in the time frame (capacity 100 over 5 days gives 500); metrics assume a 5-day work week. [business-insights.workplace:occupancy-formula] On the Workplace Details page, average attendance is total visits divided by total assigned headcount for the workplace, times 100. [business-insights.workplace:attendance-formula] [gap:business-insights.workplace:attendance-denominator] The Footfall by the Time of the Day section shows hourly footfall averaged for 7 days of the week when monthly data is selected, and its CSV export is available when the Time Range is Last 7 days or Week. [business-insights.workplace:footfall] In Attendance by Department, the Show Unassigned Users checkbox shows data for users not assigned to a department. [business-insights.workplace:unassigned-users] The day-of-week graphs are calculated differently on the details and chart pages [divergence:business-insights.workplace:day-of-week-metrics].

Configuring an office and its goals is in [Business Insights offices: configuring an office and organization- and office-level occupancy and attendance goals](kb:business-insights.offices); scheduled workplace reports are in [Business Insights scheduled reports: workplace, application and Data Explorer reports, and the exclusion list](kb:business-insights.reports); the product overview is in [Business Insights: SaaS usage and spend, workplace utilization, prerequisites and data sources](kb:business-insights.overview). The Business Insights terminology article defines Average Attendance as total visits divided by the total assigned headcount for the workplace, times 100, and Average Occupancy as total visits divided by the site capacity, times 100. [business-insights.workplace:terminology-formulas] [gap:business-insights.workplace:terminology]

Sources disagree (name both sides):
- The map and discovered locations articles tell the admin to specify city, state or province, and country when configuring an office, while the office configuration article has an Address field and a 2024 release note says fields were removed from the Add Office drawer. [divergence:business-insights.workplace:office-location-fields]
  - zscaler-help: City, state or province, and country are to be specified when configuring offices.
  - zscaler-help: The office configuration article describes an Address field that autopopulates.
  - zscaler-help: The August 30, 2024 release note says the Address field autopopulates and that a list of fields (City, Country and State/Province) is no longer available when configuring an office.
  Impact: Which fields an admin enters so an office has city, state and country metadata for the map and the Discovered Locations City column is not settled; the articles that name those fields predate or ignore the removal.
- The Workplace Details and Offices and Locations chart articles calculate the Occupancy by Day of the Week and Attendance by Day of the Week graphs from different quantities. [divergence:business-insights.workplace:day-of-week-metrics]
  - zscaler-help: On Workplace Details, occupancy by day is users who came in divided by capacity, and attendance by day is users who came in divided by users assigned to the workplace.
  - zscaler-help: On the chart, occupancy by day is visits divided by the possible capacity of the workplaces, and attendance by day is visits divided by the total possible assigned headcount.
  Impact: Whether users versus visits and one versus all selected workplaces describe different scopes or one calculation is not stated, so a day-of-week percentage on one page is not comparable with the other.
