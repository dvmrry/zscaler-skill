# Unified Vulnerability Management (UVM): what it does with findings, and what can be automated (uvm.overview)
Also called: uvm; unified vulnerability management; vulnerability management zscaler; uvm api; risk-based vulnerability prioritization

UVM manages vulnerability findings that other tools produce; ingestion is covered in [Feeding UVM with AnySource: retrieval methods, file structure and the fields to include](kb:uvm.anysource) and [UVM data sources: shared connector facts, availability, and the Jira, Zscaler, Okta, Code42, NetBox, runZero, SecurityScorecard and SFTP connectors](kb:uvm.data-sources), and outbound integrations in [UVM outegrations: setup procedure, storage and streaming, Splunk, Teams and Email, and Tenable scan outegrations](kb:uvm.outegrations) and [UVM ticketing outegrations (Jira, ServiceNow, Freshservice, Azure DevOps), the ServiceNow CMDB outegration, their webhooks and mapping](kb:uvm.outegrations.ticketing). UVM provides a single place for managing vulnerabilities, simplifying the process of identifying and remediating security risks. [uvm.overview:what-it-is]

**Prioritization.** Zscaler's UVM product page describes risk prioritization with contextual insight into risk factors and mitigating controls, out-of-the-box multifactor scoring that accounts for risk factors and mitigating controls, and adjustable weights for any risk factor or mitigating control. [uvm.overview:prioritization] Zscaler's UVM product page says 80% of "critical" issues are downgraded to "medium". [uvm.overview:marketing-downgraded] The product page says UVM is powered by the Data Fabric for Security, which ingests data from vulnerability and exploitability sources, Zscaler and third parties through 150+ prebuilt data connectors, and correlates security findings and context spanning identity, assets, user behavior, mitigating controls, business processes and organizational hierarchy. [uvm.overview:correlation] Scores, categories and SLAs are in [How UVM calculates severity scores, maps them to severity categories and sets SLAs](kb:uvm.severity-sla).

**Assets, tickets and exceptions.** [What UVM assets and findings are, what the Assets and Findings pages show, and how on-demand asset scans work](kb:uvm.assets-findings), [How UVM tickets work: the Tickets page, manual split and merge, automatic grouping rules, status buckets and ticket workflows](kb:uvm.tickets), [How UVM exception requests work: roles, statuses, review, permissions and settings](kb:uvm.exceptions), [What Remediation Copilot does in a UVM ticket, what it will not do, and its limits](kb:uvm.remediation-copilot) and [How EASM discovery profiles work: seeds and their formats, limits, scan cadence, permissions and reports](kb:uvm.easm-discovery).

**Workflows and reporting.** The product page lists prebuilt dashboards and reports, custom dashboards and reports, custom workflows that match the organizational structure, two-way ticketing integration that reconciles tickets including automatic closing and reopening, and exception management for requesting and approving extensions when remediations cannot be completed within SLAs. [uvm.overview:workflows] Dashboards and measurements are in [What UVM's built-in measurements count and what the Overview, Risk and Remediation History dashboards show](kb:uvm.measurements). Saved reports and their export, including the Reporting API, are platform-wide: [A saved report can be scheduled for delivery by email, to an AWS S3 bucket or to Slack, daily, weekly, monthly or on a custom schedule; reports are reachable from Explore > Reports and from My Reports under Vulnerabilities, Assets, Identities and SOC Workbench, and each scheduled run is listed on the report's Runs page.](kb:portfolio.secops-platform:report-schedule), [A saved report can be exported programmatically through the Reporting API, a GraphQL-based interface that exports the data to an AWS S3 bucket or returns a downloadable link; the client authenticates for an access token, submits a request to start the report and polls for its status.](kb:portfolio.secops-platform:reporting-api).

## Automation

No vendored client covers UVM: the Go and Python SDKs, the Terraform providers, the Ansible collections, the MCP server, the OneAPI Postman collection and the captured Automate contract have no UVM service. [uvm.overview:no-client-surface] Ingestion has an Upload File API whose contract is open ([Feeding UVM with AnySource: retrieval methods, file structure and the fields to include](kb:uvm.anysource)), and export goes through the platform's Reporting API ([The SecOps Reporting API's endpoint, token request, GraphQL schema and response shape](kb:portfolio.secops-platform:reporting-api-contract)).
