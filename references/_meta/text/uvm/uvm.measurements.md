# What UVM's built-in measurements count and what the Overview, Risk and Remediation History dashboards show (uvm.measurements)
Also called: uvm measurements; uvm dashboards; mean time to remediate; tickets over sla; risk mass; uvm kpi

Additional measurements can be added on request and default measurement calculations can be overridden by contacting the Zscaler Account team. [uvm.measurements:customization]

**Ticket measurements.** The measurement terminology article defines Total Open Tickets as tickets that are Active or Inactive and not in the Closed bucket, and Active Tickets (Open) as only Active tickets not in the Closed bucket, excluding inactive ones. [uvm.measurements:ticket-open-counts] Closed Tickets counts only tickets set to the Closed bucket within the date range, whereas Total Closed Tickets includes tickets closed before the range. [uvm.measurements:closed-measures] The measurement terminology article defines Total Tickets Over SLA as treating a ticket not in the Closed bucket as over SLA when the set date range is after its SLA, and a Closed-bucket ticket as over SLA when it was moved to Closed after the SLA date. [uvm.measurements:sla-measures] Ticket Mean Time to Remediate runs from ticket creation to the date the ticket state last turned Inactive, Mean Time to Assign to the first time the assignee became not null, and Mean Time to Close to the ticket's status bucket being Closed, all in days. [uvm.measurements:time-to] Mean Time to Remediate [divergence:uvm.measurements:mttr-definition]. Total Open Tickets [divergence:uvm.measurements:open-tickets-definition]. Total Tickets Over SLA [divergence:uvm.measurements:tickets-over-sla-scope].

**Finding and asset measurements.** New Findings counts findings first seen in the date range and excludes reopened findings, Detected Findings counts findings that turned Active in the range, and Active Findings is a unique count by finding key on the last day of the range. [uvm.measurements:finding-measures] Vulnerable Assets counts the assets linked to Active findings on the last day of the date range, and Risk Mass is the sum of severity scores of the Active findings of a ticket or asset. [uvm.measurements:asset-measures] The measurement terminology article defines the percentage measurements as % Remediation = Inactive findings / Total findings, % Total Tickets Over SLA = Tickets Over SLA / Total Tickets, and % Open Tickets Over SLA = Open Tickets Over SLA / Total Open Tickets. [uvm.measurements:pct-measures] % Open Tickets Over SLA [divergence:uvm.measurements:open-over-sla-pct].

**Overview dashboard.** The Overview dashboard displays source findings and source assets for each of the data sources configured in the account, after mapping but before unification; UVM merges the same vulnerability on the same machine reported by different sources into a single unified record, so the central Findings node might show a lower number than the sum of raw source findings; the trend arrow shows the percentage change in deduplicated findings in the past 30 days, and the flow includes the findings to tickets ratio. [uvm.measurements:overview-dashboard] The Overview dashboard has a Vulnerable Assets tile (unique unified assets with at least one active finding), a Total Unique CVE tile, a % Assigned Tickets tile (currently active tickets assigned to an owner or team) and a % Third-Party Tickets tile (tickets dispatched through outegrations). [uvm.measurements:overview-tiles] The Overview dashboard's Tickets by Severity section says the Mean Time to Remediate (MTTR) measurement shows the average number of days it takes for a ticket to move from opened to resolved. [uvm.measurements:overview-mttr]

**Risk dashboard.** The Risk dashboard offers three risk scoring methods, chosen from the Risk Scoring Method drop-down: AVG Risk (average severity score of the entity's findings), Max Risk (highest severity score) and Risk Mass (sum of severity scores of the active findings). [uvm.measurements:risk-methods] The Risk dashboard's Key Metrics Over Time chart shows the last 6 months with Active Findings as the default measurement and up to two measurements at a time, and the dashboard exports as a PDF. [uvm.measurements:risk-charts] The Risk dashboard's Findings - Last Week widget counts Discovered findings (became active in the last 7 days and are active currently) and Undetected findings (active and undetected in the last 7 days). [uvm.measurements:risk-weekly]

**Remediation History dashboard.** The Remediation History dashboard's top-panel tiles show current open tickets by severity and are not affected by the time filter; ticket measurement titles including Open refer to ticket status and those including Active to ticket state. [uvm.measurements:remediation-history] The Remediation History dashboard's Key Metrics Over Time chart describes Total Open Tickets as tickets that were open at some point during the period regardless of state, Total Tickets Over SLA as open tickets past their SLA date at some point during the period, and % Open Tickets Over SLA as relative to the total number of tickets. [uvm.measurements:rh-key-metrics] The Key Metrics Over Time chart of the Remediation History dashboard is limited to 8 metrics; the Open Tickets by Current Status chart omits Closed-tagged buckets and empty buckets; Analyze Remediation Work defaults to grouping by assignee and exports as CSV, and the dashboard exports as PDF. [uvm.measurements:remediation-limits]

Sources disagree (name both sides):
- The Overview dashboard says MTTR is the average days for a ticket to move from opened to resolved, while the terminology article defines Ticket Mean Time to Remediate as the days from ticket creation until the ticket state last turned Inactive. [divergence:uvm.measurements:mttr-definition]
  - zscaler-help: MTTR is the average number of days for a ticket to move from opened to resolved.
  - zscaler-help: Ticket Mean Time to Remediate counts days from the ticket's creation date to the date its state last turned Inactive.
  Impact: The two pages do not name the same start and end points for MTTR; state which page a figure follows.
- The terminology article defines Total Open Tickets as Active or Inactive tickets not in the Closed bucket, while the Remediation History page defines it as tickets that were open at some point during the period regardless of state. [divergence:uvm.measurements:open-tickets-definition]
  - zscaler-help: Total Open Tickets is the number of tickets set as Active or Inactive that are not in the Closed bucket.
  - zscaler-help: Total Open Tickets is the number of tickets that were open at some point during the specified period, regardless of state.
  Impact: A count labelled Total Open Tickets can follow either definition depending on the page; state which page a figure follows.
- The terminology article defines % Open Tickets Over SLA as Open Tickets Over SLA / Total Open Tickets, while the Remediation History page says it is relative to the total number of tickets. [divergence:uvm.measurements:open-over-sla-pct]
  - zscaler-help: % Open Tickets Over SLA = Open Tickets Over SLA / Total Open Tickets.
  - zscaler-help: % Open Tickets Over SLA is the percentage of open tickets past their SLA date at some point in the period, relative to the total number of tickets.
  Impact: The denominators differ between the two pages (Total Open Tickets versus total number of tickets); state which page a figure follows.
- The terminology article defines Total Tickets Over SLA over both open and Closed-bucket tickets, while the Remediation History Key Metrics chart describes it as open tickets past their SLA date. [divergence:uvm.measurements:tickets-over-sla-scope]
  - zscaler-help: Total Tickets Over SLA covers tickets not in the Closed bucket and tickets in the Closed bucket.
  - zscaler-help: Total Tickets Over SLA is the number of open tickets past their SLA date at some point in the period.
  Impact: Whether Closed-bucket tickets count toward Total Tickets Over SLA depends on the page; state which page a figure follows.
