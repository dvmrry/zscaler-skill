# Health360 Adoption and Executive Insights: license utilization, deployment status, and executive traffic, peer and incident views (unified.analytics.health360.adoption)
Also called: health360 adoption; service adoption; license utilization; health360 executive insights

The Adoption dashboard (Analytics > Health360 > Adoption) gives an overview of Zscaler platform adoption maturity at three levels: portfolio summaries for executive leadership, operational matrices by solution area, and product drilldowns. [unified.analytics.health360.adoption:adoption-what] The page can hide or unhide unsubscribed services; Zscaler Platform shows deployed services per platform category, such as Users, Data Security, Branch, Cloud, AI Security and Security Ops, and Zscaler Products shows the subscriptions deployed per service, each tile with its license-utilization percentage. [unified.analytics.health360.adoption:adoption-page]

**Adoption details.** A product's adoption details page gives Products Deployed (subscriptions deployed for the service), Licenses Utilized (utilization percentage, with the active and total available licenses), and per subscription its Deployment Status and Utilization (licenses used versus purchased). [unified.analytics.health360.adoption:adoption-details] The details page charts license utilization for the last 6 months; for Internet & SaaS only it charts SSL/TLS-inspected versus non-inspected traffic volume, and for Private Access only the number of application segments, each over the last 6 months. [unified.analytics.health360.adoption:adoption-trends] No selectable time range, no values for Deployment Status and no definition of an active license are stated [gap:unified.analytics.health360.adoption:adoption-definitions].

**Executive Insights.** Executive Insights (Analytics > Health360 > Executive Insights) gives a high-level overview of applications, network, cloud and incidents, with drill-down on each metric, and has tabs for private application insights, network insights, data center insights and incident insights. [unified.analytics.health360.adoption:executive-insights-tabs] The Applications tab shows a donut of traffic distribution across categories with the percentage change, a drill-down to the top 10 applications, and private-application traffic by host (for example AWS, GCP, Azure). [unified.analytics.health360.adoption:executive-insights-applications] The page states no time range, and introduces the Applications tab as private-application insights although its first section is about internet applications [gap:unified.analytics.health360.adoption:executive-insights-page].

**Peers, network, cloud and incidents.** The Applications tab also compares each category to industry peers: by accessing Health360 the organization gives Zscaler the right to compare its preceding month with peer organizations and all companies using the cloud service, with peers chosen by business vertical, geographic region and business size; the comparison covers overall traffic, configuration baseline, traffic distribution by application category and by region and access method, and product usage. [unified.analytics.health360.adoption:executive-insights-peers] The Industry Peer Comparison report is in [The Industry Peer Comparison Report compares the organization's performance and effectiveness for the preceding month to that of peer organizations and all companies using the cloud service. Peers are chosen based on business vertical, geographic region and business size, and accessing the feature gives Zscaler the right to make the comparison.](kb:zia.reports.executive:peer-comparison). The Network tab shows total traffic per country split between office and remote and the top locations user traffic comes from; the Cloud tab shows total traffic for the top ZTEs for Internet/SaaS traffic. [unified.analytics.health360.adoption:executive-insights-network-cloud] The Incident tab shows the number of network and application incidents for the top 5 locations in descending order and a month-over-month incident trend, both filterable by incident severity. [unified.analytics.health360.adoption:executive-insights-incidents]
