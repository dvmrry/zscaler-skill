# Health360: deployment health scores and bands, categories, Best Practices and Connectors Health (unified.analytics.health360)
Also called: health360; zscaler health360; health score; connectors health; best practices dashboard

Zscaler Health360 gives centralized visibility into the deployment, health and performance of the Zscaler environment, removing manual analysis across product consoles and custom reports, and helps monitor active subscriptions, deployment status, configuration quality and the value delivered. [unified.analytics.health360:what-it-is] Health360 covers four categories: Services (ISP networks, Zero Trust Exchange infrastructure and applications, with ISP-network and application health data powered by ZDX), Connectors (devices that connect to the Zero Trust Exchange, with versions, performance, capacity and health signals), Best Practices, and Adoption (deployment, entitlement and utilization per product). [unified.analytics.health360:categories]

**Dashboards.** The Analytics overview lists Health360's dashboards as Best Practices, Connectors Health, China Connectivity (a near real-time overview of China Premium bandwidth utilization against purchased bandwidth), Adoption and Executive Insights. [unified.analytics.health360:views] China Connectivity is held in [Bandwidth utilization for the premium services can be viewed on Health360. Health360 Service Health gives a near-real-time overview of China Premium connectivity, showing current and historic bandwidth utilization against purchased bandwidth. On the Service Health: China Connectivity page (Analytics > Health360 > China Connectivity) an admin can show the last 24 hours, 48 hours, 7 days, 30 days or a custom range, choose a data center, export to CSV, and switch between China Premium and Premium Plus.](kb:zia.traffic-forwarding.china-premium:health360). Adoption and Executive Insights are in [Health360 Adoption and Executive Insights: license utilization, deployment status, and executive traffic, peer and incident views](kb:unified.analytics.health360.adoption).

**Overall score.** The Health360 dashboard (Analytics > Health360) can show metrics from the last 24 hours, 48 hours, 7 days or 30 days. [unified.analytics.health360:dashboard-time-range] The overall health score (0-100) is averaged from all the categories of Health360 and banded Green 90-100 Healthy, Amber 70-89 Moderate, Red 40-69 Degraded and Crimson Red 0-39 Critical. [unified.analytics.health360:overall-score] The dashboard shows a Best Practices score (health signals for optimal configuration, resilience and performance), a Connectors Health score (health signals from devices such as Client Connector, App Connector and tunnels) and a Service Health score (signals such as availability, ISP network latency, Zero Trust Exchange and application performance), with a health score trend graph for the filter time frame. [unified.analytics.health360:category-scores] Overall Health by Regions shows regions with health indicators, each showing on hover its overall score and per-category breakdown, and Incidents Impacting Your Health Score lists the top incidents affecting the overall score. [unified.analytics.health360:regions-and-incidents] How the categories are weighted, and what signals feed each score, is open [gap:unified.analytics.health360:scoring].

**Best Practices.** The Best Practices page (Analytics > Health360 > Best Practices) evaluates configuration posture against Zscaler's recommended guidelines for each deployed Zscaler service; its score (0-100) is banded Green 90-100 Healthy, Amber 70-89 Moderate, Red 40-69 Degraded and Crimson Red 0-39 Critical, and its trend graph covers the previous month. [unified.analytics.health360:best-practices-score] Practices are split into Resiliency and Performance; each table row gives a Status of Followed or Not Followed, the Product, a Percent Compliant (only 100% compliant practices count as Followed), a Priority of Medium or High, and Recommendations with the remediation steps. [unified.analytics.health360:best-practices-table] A practice's details page gives its Category (Resiliency or Performance), Product, Priority (based on severity), Recommendation and Percent Followed, and a table of the parameters involved (for example policy name, forwarding profile, server, ports) whose filter options and data vary by practice. [unified.analytics.health360:practice-details] The pages give no catalogue of practices and do not say whether Percent Compliant and Percent Followed are the same figure [gap:unified.analytics.health360:best-practices].

**Connectors Health.** The Connectors Health page (Analytics > Health360 > Connectors Health) evaluates the health of Zscaler connectors, Private Service Edges, Client Connector and tunnels, for location-based diagnostics (offline, degraded or unreachable components by region or site), capacity management (overloaded connectors, saturated tunnels, uneven traffic distribution) and lifecycle management (connector versions, upgrade compliance, end-of-support risks). [unified.analytics.health360:connectors-health-scope] Connectors Health can show the last 24 hours, 48 hours, 7 days, 30 days or a custom range; its score (0-100) is banded Green 90-100 Healthy, Amber 70-89 Moderate, Red 40-69 Degraded and Crimson Red 0-39 Critical, and clicking the trend graph shows the health of all devices and the events affecting the score at that date and time. [unified.analytics.health360:connectors-health-view] Locations With Connectors shows device health by country, sortable by Most Impacted, Name or Locations Count, with a map switchable between Offices and Remote locations and filterable by health status. [unified.analytics.health360:connectors-health-locations]

**Retention.** How long Health360 data is kept, and where, is not on these pages [gap:unified.analytics.health360:retention].
