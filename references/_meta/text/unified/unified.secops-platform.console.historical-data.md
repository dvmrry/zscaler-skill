# Historical data in the admin portal: granularity, time filter, measurement types, filter evaluation time and caveats (unified.secops-platform.console.historical-data)
Also called: secops platform historical data; Was at Last at Turn to measurements; Historical State filter evaluation

**Controls.** The historical view has two controls, time granularity (month, week or day buckets) and a time filter (date range), and the time filter always takes precedence: with a range of Last 3 Days and Month granularity the month bucket holds only those 3 days. [unified.secops-platform.console.historical-data:controls] Historical data is available in system dashboards (some with a date selector, such as Remediation History), in custom dashboard widgets and in reports; custom widgets and reports show current data by default, and reports switch to history with History mode in the date selector. [unified.secops-platform.console.historical-data:where-available] Adding a dimension such as Assignee adds a grouping level on top of the time granularity, so measurements are split by the dimension value in each period. [unified.secops-platform.console.historical-data:dimensions]

**Measurement types.** Was at measurements count entities that met a condition at any point in the selected range even if they changed later; the examples are Total Open Tickets and Total Closed Tickets. [unified.secops-platform.console.historical-data:measurement-was-at] Last at measurements reflect whether the entity met the condition on the last day of the range, such as Active Findings and Max Finding Severity Score. [unified.secops-platform.console.historical-data:measurement-last-at] Turn to measurements count entities that transitioned to a condition during the period, such as Opened Tickets, Remediated Tickets (moved to Remediated) and Closed Tickets. [unified.secops-platform.console.historical-data:measurement-turn-to] Mean time to (MTTX) measurements average the days an entity takes to move between conditions (Ticket Mean Time to Remediate is Active to Inactive); current data uses only the most recent remediation period, while historical data captures every remediation period in the range with separate values. [unified.secops-platform.console.historical-data:measurement-mttx]

**Filter evaluation.** Filters on historical data are evaluated against the current state of each entity by default, so entities that matched in the range but no longer match are omitted; custom historical dashboards and reports can switch the evaluation to Historical State, evaluated for the selected range or each time bucket. [unified.secops-platform.console.historical-data:filter-evaluation] In the page's example with the filter Assignee = Team 1 and the date range January, Total Open Tickets is 1 under Current State and 2 under Historical State. [unified.secops-platform.console.historical-data:filter-evaluation-example]

**Caveats.** Historical data lives in a separate dataset of all entity changes, is slower to retrieve than current data (most noticeably for long ranges or detailed filters and dimensions), and is refreshed in processing intervals typically every 4 hours. [unified.secops-platform.console.historical-data:caveats] View Items on a widget segment shows items in their current state for current measures, or as they were at the selected point in time for historical measures. [unified.secops-platform.console.historical-data:view-items] The limits of the historical dataset are open [gap:unified.secops-platform.console.historical-data:historical-limits], and the page's worked examples do not all match their tables [gap:unified.secops-platform.console.historical-data:example-inconsistencies].
