# Networking and Connector Activity dashboards: traffic, data-center and connector maps, discovered devices, Cloud and Branch Connector activity (unified.analytics.networking)
Also called: networking dashboard; connector activity dashboard; traffic in my organization; road warrior traffic

The Networking dashboard provides information about traffic, data centers and devices; its time range runs between 1 Day and 90 Days, applies to all data within the dashboard, and defaults to 14 Days. [unified.analytics.networking:networking-time-range] The time range is said to apply to all data in the dashboard, yet Devices Discovered covers the most recent 24 hours, and the User Devices widget's percentage is undefined [gap:unified.analytics.networking:networking-details].

**Traffic and maps.** Traffic in my Organization charts the number of transactions and the overall traffic volume with their percentage change, and traffic for Road Warrior (totally remote) versus In-Office users, toggled between Internet and Private application traffic; further widgets show the road-warrior versus in-office distribution and the top locations sending traffic through Zscaler. [unified.analytics.networking:networking-traffic] The map has Data Centers (dot size shows traffic flowing through the location), App Connectors (dot size shows connectors in use) and Cloud & Branch Connectors tabs (dot size shows connectors in use); clicking a location shows the connector type, health status, location and traffic, boxes limit the view to Cloud Connectors or Branch Connectors, and View Connector Activity opens the Connector Activity dashboard. [unified.analytics.networking:networking-map]

**Devices.** Device widgets show the total number of devices discovered with a bar graph of operating system distribution, and the breakdown of devices (such as smartphones, computers, printers and peripherals, and IoT devices) discovered in the most recent 24 hours. [unified.analytics.networking:networking-devices]

**Connector Activity.** The Connector Activity dashboard toggles between Cloud Connectors and Branch Connectors and filters, each able to include or exclude options, by Geolocation, Status (Active or Inactive), Connector Type (Physical or Virtual Branch Connector) and Connector Location. [unified.analytics.networking:connector-activity-filters] The Traffic Volume Across Service and Session Across Service ring charts split the throughput and session counts of the provisioned and deployed connectors into Internet (through the Internet & SaaS cloud), Private (through the Private Applications cloud), Direct (forwarded directly) and Log & Control (through the Log and Control gateway). [unified.analytics.networking:connector-activity-rings] The Connectors table gives, among its columns, Group, Autoscaling (True or False), Status (Active or Inactive), HA Status (Branch Connector), VM Size (Cloud Connector, Small or Medium) and Model (for a physical Branch Connector the hardware device, for example ZT800; Virtual for a virtual connector); the same details are also available under Infrastructure > Connectors > Cloud > Cloud Connector Monitoring and Infrastructure > Connectors > Edge > Branch Connector Monitoring. [unified.analytics.networking:connector-activity-table] The Cloud and Branch Connector console counterpart is [The Cloud & Branch Connector Dashboard: Traffic Monitoring widgets, Geo View, monitoring table and Traffic Flow](kb:cloud-connector.insights.dashboard), and its Connectors table columns are in [Cloud Connector status: what Active, Inactive and Disabled mean, and the per-VM API record](kb:cloud-connector.insights.connector-status). The Connector Activity page states no time range [gap:unified.analytics.networking:connector-activity-range].
