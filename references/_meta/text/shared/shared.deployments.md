# Where the ZIA and ZPA deployment and operations guides and leading practices guides are recorded (shared.deployments)
Also called: deployment and operations guides; d&o guides; leading practices guides

**ZIA.** Authentication, bandwidth, Cloud App Control, URL filtering, DNS, firewall, IPS and Isolation are in [What the ZIA deployment and operations guides say about authentication, bandwidth, Cloud App Control, URL filtering, DNS, firewall, IPS and Isolation](kb:shared.deployments.zia.policy-controls); Sandbox, Threat Protection, DLP, SaaS Security and SSL inspection are in [What the ZIA deployment and operations guides say about Sandbox, Threat Protection, DLP, SaaS Security and SSL inspection](kb:shared.deployments.zia.threat-and-data); local breakouts and SIEM integration are in [What the ZIA local breakouts and SIEM integration deployment guides say about data center choice, tunnels and NSS log delivery](kb:shared.deployments.zia.network-and-logging); disaster recovery is in [What the ZIA disaster recovery deployment and operations guide requires and how clients behave](kb:shared.deployments.zia.disaster-recovery).

**Leading practices.** The ZIA policy guide is in [What the ZIA Policy Leading Practices Guide recommends for module flow, policy types, advanced settings, URL filtering and firewall](kb:shared.deployments.zia.policy-leading-practices), the TLS and SSL inspection guide in [What the Internet & SaaS TLS and SSL Inspection Leading Practices Guide recommends for rollout, rule layout, bypasses and certificates](kb:shared.deployments.zia.tls-leading-practices), and the ZPA guide in [What the ZPA Leading Practices Guide recommends for connector sizing, segmentation rollout, application segments and Private Service Edges](kb:shared.deployments.zpa.leading-practices).

**Client Connector.** The Client Connector deployment and operations guide is recorded with the Client Connector installation subject, not here: [Client Connector install parameters and deployment](kb:zcc.install).

**ZPA.** App Connector, Application Discovery, Browser Access, Client Forwarding Policy, log streaming and provisioning are in [What the ZPA deployment and operations guides say about App Connector, Application Discovery, Browser Access, Client Forwarding Policy, log streaming and provisioning](kb:shared.deployments.zpa.operations); disaster recovery is in [What the ZPA disaster recovery deployment and operations guide requires, leaves unsupported and how activation is detected](kb:shared.deployments.zpa.disaster-recovery).
