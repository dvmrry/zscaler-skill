# What fair-use thresholds, overage and burst rules each Zscaler product's page states (shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits)

ZIA fair use: average ZIA Monthly Traffic across all purchased seats should be below two times the cloud-wide average (currently 8 GB per seat per month) plus any additional bandwidth entitlement; unauthenticated traffic in excess requires a subscription for additional bandwidth. [shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:zia] ZPA fair use: average monthly traffic across all purchased seats should be below two times the cloud-wide average (currently 3 GB per seat per month) plus any additional bandwidth entitlement; excess requires a subscription for additional entitlements. [shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:zpa] Zero Trust Cloud Browser data consumption is limited to the SKU's stated data limits; if none is stated, average monthly traffic across all purchased seats must be below two times the cloud-wide average (currently 8 GB per seat per month) plus contractually stated bandwidth entitlements, covering all data uploaded and downloaded via Zero Trust Cloud Browser. [shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:ztbrowser] The Zero Trust Browser cap is stated on three pages [divergence:shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:ztb-traffic-cap], and the Source IP Anchoring cap on two [divergence:shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:sipa-traffic-cap]. The license units are in [What unit the products on the Logs and Fair Use pages are licensed by, and how it is counted](kb:shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics) and the hub is [Where to find a Zscaler product's log retention, log contents, license unit and fair-use limit](kb:shared.logs-fair-use).

**ZPA Autonomous App Shield.** For Autonomous App Shield, tokens and scans are metered in-product against total entitlements across licensed assets (4M tokens and 24 scans per year per asset); when exhausted, additional scans cannot be initiated until more entitlements are purchased. [shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:zpa-shield]

**ZPA China Premium.** ZPA China Premium is licensed by the bandwidth purchased for the subscription term. [shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:zpa-china-lic] ZPA China Premium bandwidth is measured in Mbps from five-minute samples; per Premium Data Center and calendar month the samples are sorted and the 95th percentile taken (P95) separately for Tx and Rx, the larger is that data center's usage, and the data center values are summed for the month. [shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:zpa-china-bw] ZPA China Premium usage must stay within purchased bandwidth 95% of any calendar month (about 36 hours of burst in a 30-day month); maximum burst may not exceed 130% of purchased bandwidth at any time, and excess is best-effort and may be throttled to purchased bandwidth. [shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:zpa-china-burst] The Supplemental Premium China Services Availability Agreement does not apply if burst limits are exceeded (greater than 130% at any time and/or greater than 5% of five-minute samples in a calendar month). [shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:zpa-china-sla] Zscaler may deploy bandwidth control policies to restrict ZPA China Premium consumption to purchased bandwidth, may use third-party network/ISP reports in addition to internal tools, and provides previous-month bandwidth usage reports on written request. [shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:zpa-china-ctrl] The page's tables are examples [gap:shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:zpa-china-examples].

**Per-seat and per-site thresholds.** SSPM fair use: average data transfer per subscribed seat should be below two times the cloud-wide average (currently 8 GB per seat per month); excess requires a subscription for additional entitlements. [shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:sspm] ZSDK fair use: average monthly traffic per client mobile app is expected to be below 950 TB. [shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:zsdk] Zero Trust Branch Connector fair use: average data transfer from 50 devices/servers per site is 100 GB per month. [shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:branch-connector] EASM fair use: under the seat license type, the asset to seat ratio may not exceed 0.75; exceeding requires a subscription for additional entitlements. [shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:easm] AI Asset Management Platform fair use: usage must match purchased seats, traffic must be end-user activity to Gen AI, Embedded AI and Endpoint AI apps, the service must not be used for automated, synthetic or non-user-generated traffic at scale, and the provisioned ZIA tenant must not be used outside AI Asset Management. [shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:ai-platform] The cloud-wide averages carry no as-of date [gap:shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:cloud-average-as-of].

**Scanned-data volume.** DSPM fair use: the average volume of scanned data per data store is expected to be less than two times the observed average across Zscaler customers, and the page adds '(currently 1TB per data store)'; individual data stores can exceed 1TB provided the average across all subscribed data stores remains below 1TB. [shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:dspm] DSPM's fair use includes an average volume cap for scanned data; exceeding it requires an additional volume add-on. [shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:dspm-cap] AI Asset Management for AI Infrastructure fair use: the average volume of scanned data per data store/platform is expected to be less than two times the cloud-wide average, and the page states 'Currently, 1TB per data store/platform per month'; the volume of some data stores/platforms can exceed 1TB provided the average across all remains below 1TB; the amount scanned follows your scan settings and excess requires a subscription for additional volume. [shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:ai-infra] Both pages pair 'two times the average' with a 1TB figure and an average that must stay below 1TB [gap:shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:volume-1tb-vs-two-times].

**Per-entity and per-asset processing.** Agentic SOC fair use: average data processing (uncompressed) no more than 10MB per entity per day, and average daily queries per account no more than 8,000. [shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:secops] AEM fair use: average data processing (uncompressed) no more than 3MB per asset per day, and average daily queries per account no more than 4K. [shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:aem] UVM fair use: average data processing (uncompressed) no more than 3MB per asset per day, and average daily queries per account no more than 4K. [shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:uvm] Identity Protection fair use: average data processing (uncompressed) no more than 3MB per user identity per day, and average daily queries per account no more than 4K. [shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:idp]

**Cellular.** If Zscaler Cellular data usage exceeds the data pool in a calendar month, service continues and an overage charge at the Order Form rate per GB is incurred automatically; overages are uncapped unless the Order Form says otherwise, and Zscaler provides no real-time usage alerts, spending caps, throttling or automatic suspension. [shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:cellular-overage]

What to check:
1. The fair-use threshold stated on the product's own page: ZIA below two times the cloud-wide average (currently 8 GB per seat per month), ZPA likewise at 3 GB per seat per month, Agentic SOC 10MB per entity per day.
2. Which cap bounds Zero Trust Browser and Source IP Anchoring traffic, since the ZIA and ZPA pages state the Source IP Anchoring cap against different products' traffic, and the Zero Trust Browser cap is stated on three pages.

Sources disagree (name both sides):
- Three pages state the traffic cap for Zero Trust Browser differently: its own page gives the SKU data limits or a 2x cloud-wide average of 8 GB per seat per month, while the ZIA and ZPA licensing pages each name a maximum aggregate amount of their own Monthly Traffic across all seats. [divergence:shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:ztb-traffic-cap]
  - zscaler-help: Zero Trust Cloud Browser data consumption is limited to the SKU's stated data limits, or without one to below 2x the cloud-wide average, currently 8 GB per seat per month, plus contractual bandwidth entitlements.
  - zscaler-help: On the ZIA licensing page, Zero Trust Browser is subscribed per seat with a maximum aggregate amount of ZIA Monthly Traffic across all seats.
  - zscaler-help: On the ZPA licensing page, Zero Trust Browser is subscribed per seat with a maximum aggregate amount of ZPA Monthly Traffic across all seats.
  Impact: None of the three pages states a figure for the ZIA or ZPA aggregate, and none says which page governs Zero Trust Browser traffic, so a Zero Trust Browser cap taken from one page does not hold for the others.
- The ZIA and ZPA licensing pages each say Source IP Anchoring (SIPA) is subscribed per seat with a maximum aggregate amount of their own product's Monthly Traffic across all seats. [divergence:shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits:sipa-traffic-cap]
  - zscaler-help: On the ZIA licensing page, SIPA is bounded by a maximum aggregate amount of ZIA Monthly Traffic across all seats.
  - zscaler-help: On the ZPA licensing page, SIPA is bounded by a maximum aggregate amount of ZPA Monthly Traffic across all seats.
  Impact: Neither page gives a figure for the cap, and the pages do not say whether SIPA traffic counts against the ZIA aggregate, the ZPA aggregate or both.
