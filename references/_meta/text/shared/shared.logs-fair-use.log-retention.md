# How long Zscaler keeps the logs of the products on the Logs and Fair Use pages, and where it stores them (shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention)

ZIA logs are retained for a rolling six months during the subscription term; storage is chosen at deployment as either the United States or the European Union and Switzerland. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:zia-logs] The Client Connector page lists different ZIA storage regions [divergence:shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:zia-storage-regions]. For IoT Advanced Security, unauthenticated, non-user-identifying web logs associated with the customer at org level are retained a rolling three months and stored in the United States. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:zia-iot] Per the ZIA logs page, Nanolog Streaming Service (NSS) streams logs in real time within your premises and can send them to multiple systems, allowing retention for any period. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:zia-nss] Retention for the other products is by product below; the hub is [Where to find a Zscaler product's log retention, log contents, license unit and fair-use limit](kb:shared.logs-fair-use).

**ZPA.** ZPA retains User Activity, User Status, AppProtection and App Connector logs a rolling at least 14 days and audit logs a rolling six months; with the Extended Retention feature, User Activity, User Status, Inspection and App Connector logs are kept a rolling six months. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:zpa-retention] The Extended Retention list names Inspection logs where the standard list names AppProtection logs [gap:shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:zpa-appprotection-vs-inspection]. ZPA logs are stored in the United States or the European Union, chosen at deployment, and can be viewed or streamed in real time with the Log Streaming Service (LSS). [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:zpa-storage] With Session Recording for PRA configured, session recordings are retained for one year during the subscription term and are encrypted and stored in the geographic region of the tenant. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:zpa-pra] For Zscaler Microsegmentation machine-learning recommendations (with opt-in), flow logs and resource inventory data are stored a rolling period of up to 180 days to generate recommendations. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:zpa-zms-ml]

**ZDX.** ZDX logs are kept for a rolling period of at least two weeks or two days, depending on the subscription. Storage is in the United States, European Union, Australia, Canada, Singapore or India, or in the region you select if you subscribe to the ZDX Data Residency feature. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:zdx-logs] Which subscription gets two weeks and which two days is open [gap:shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:zdx-subscription-mapping]. ZDX Diagnostics reports are retained for a rolling at least two weeks or two days, depending on the subscription; Diagnostics might capture packet capture files and process-level device telemetry (ZDX Deep Tracing data). [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:zdx-diagnostics] ZDX feature retention: Managed Monitoring logs 30 days, Network Intelligence aggregated logs one year, Remediation script results and telemetry 180 days (authorized users can delete script results at any time via the ZDX Admin Portal), Snapshots 90 days. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:zdx-features]

**Client and connector products.** Client Connector logs are retained a rolling period of at least 90 days and stored in the same location as your ZIA logs. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:zcc] Several pages state retention as a floor with no upper bound [gap:shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:at-least-bounds]. Cloud & Branch Connector logs are retained a rolling six months. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:cbc] Zero Trust Gateway logs are retained a rolling six months. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:ztg] Zscaler Cellular (SIM and Cellular Edge) logs are retained a rolling up to 180 days and audit logs a rolling six months, viewable or streamable via NSS; storage is US or EU chosen at deployment. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:cellular] ZSDK User Logs and App Connector Logs are retained a rolling at least 14 days; Audit Logs a rolling six months. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:zsdk] Storage location is not stated on some of these pages [gap:shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:storage-not-stated]. AI Guard's page gives no storage location either [gap:shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:storage-not-stated].

**Cloud posture and AI products.** DSPM keeps logs describing active cloud resources or issues for the subscription duration; once a resource is gone from the CSP it keeps them a rolling 180 days. When the subscription term ends or expires, it deletes the logs after the 180-day retention cycle or earlier upon request. Storage is US or EU, chosen at deployment. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:dspm] ZPC keeps logs describing active cloud resources or issues for the subscription duration; once a resource is gone from the CSP it keeps them a rolling 180 days. When the subscription term ends or expires, it deletes the logs after the 180-day retention cycle. Storage is US or EU, chosen at deployment. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:zpc] AI Asset Management for AI Infrastructure keeps logs describing active cloud resources or issues for the subscription duration, then a rolling 180 days once the resource is gone from the CSP; logs are stored in the United States. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:ai-asset-infra] AI Asset Management Platform logs are kept a rolling six months; storage is chosen at deployment as United States or European Union and Switzerland. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:ai-asset-platform] AI Guard logs are stored for 90 days. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:ai-guard] AI Scanning Platform operation metadata logs (timestamps, API request IDs, API status codes) are kept a rolling 28 days; storage is US or EU chosen at deployment. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:ai-scanning] During the subscription you may delete data sets such as uploaded CSV files from the Admin Portal; data that cannot be removed there can be removed by manual deletion on request. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:ai-scanning-delete]

**Agentic SecOps, identity and exposure.** Agentic SecOps Platform and Agentic SOC logs are retained for a rolling period of up to six months depending on the subscription, with individual products possibly differing; storage is US or EU, chosen at deployment, and storage locations for individual products may differ. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:agentic-secops] The page lists individual products with differing periods [gap:shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:agentic-secops-retention-range]. Platform-level retention: Mapping Copilot prompts and responses 90 days as session history; Configuration Audit Logs are kept on the platform 180 days with the preceding 90 days available in the Admin Portal. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:agentic-platform] Agentic SOC retains incident and alert information for one year, comments for up to one year while the related incident remains, asset/user/device information a rolling up to one year, and Agentic SOC Assist prompts and responses for 30 days. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:agentic-soc] Identity Protection data queried from the identity provider and processed analytics are kept in the SecOps Platform a rolling 12 months and deleted within 30 days after term end; logs are retained a rolling up to 180 days; storage is United States or European Union. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:identity-protection] AEM logs are retained a rolling period up to a year, Mapping Copilot prompts and responses 90 days, and logs are stored in the United States. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:aem] UVM logs are retained a rolling up to 180 days; Remediation Copilot and Mapping Copilot prompts and responses are stored 90 days; storage is EU or US. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:uvm] Breach Predictor logs are retained a rolling period of up to a year depending on the subscription SKU; AI Assist prompts and responses are kept 30 days; storage is EU or India, chosen at deployment. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:breach-predictor] EASM logs are retained a rolling 14 days during the subscription term; when the subscription term ends or expires, the logs are deleted after the 14-day retention cycle or earlier upon request. Stored in the United States. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:easm] Threat Hunting logs are processed and retained in the United States, with results afterward moved to the region you choose; logs are retained a rolling 90 days. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:threat-hunting]

**Other products.** For Authentication Service, Audit Logs are kept for a rolling six months during the subscription term, and the deployment process lets you pick United States, European Union or India for storage. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:auth-audit] Adaptive Access Engine keeps Signal History a rolling 90 days and Audit Log information a rolling 6 months during the subscription term. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:adaptive-access] Business Insights SaaS Application Usage Logs and Workplace Insights Logs are retained a rolling period of up to three years and stored in the European Union. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:business-insights] Deception logs are retained a rolling 12 months; Zscaler deletes them within 30 days after the term ends. Storage is US, EU or Asia Pacific chosen at deployment; GenAI Decoys (Built with Llama) inputs are processed in the US for all customers. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:deception] Endpoint AI Security logs are retained a rolling at least 90 days, with manual deletion available by request to Zscaler Support; storage is US, EU or Singapore chosen at deployment. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:endpoint-ai] Experience Center logs are retained a rolling 14 days, stored in the EU for EU customers and in the US for others. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:experience-center] Health360 retains general product insights data a rolling 30 days, aggregated trend data up to 180 days and executive insights data 12 months; stored in the European Union. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:health360] Network Observability logs are retained a rolling ninety days; storage is US or EU chosen at deployment. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:network-observability] SSPM logs are retained a rolling six months and stored in the United States. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:sspm]

**Zero Trust products.** Zero Trust Branch Session Logs are retained a rolling seven days and Audit Logs a rolling six months; storage is US or EU chosen at deployment. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:ztb-branch] Zero Trust Extension Tenant Dashboard Logs are retained a rolling six months (a shorter period can be requested) and stored in the US, EU, Singapore or India chosen at deployment; the Agent Logs and Workspaces Logs are stored on users' local machines and are not stored in Zscaler clouds, except that for a specific failure you report to Zscaler, Zscaler processes, stores and uses these logs for debugging. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:ztbrowser] Zero Trust Device Segmentation logs are retained a rolling six months; storage is US or EU chosen at deployment. [shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:device-seg]

What to check:
1. The retention window and storage choice the product's own Logs page states: ZIA six months, ZPA at least 14 days or six months with Extended Retention, Client Connector at least 90 days; ZIA storage regions differ between the ZIA and Client Connector pages.

Sources disagree (name both sides):
- Where ZIA logs can be stored: the ZIA Logs page says the United States or the European Union and Switzerland, while the Client Connector Logs page says the United States, European Union or Australia. [divergence:shared.logs-fair-use.log-retention:zia-storage-regions]
  - zscaler-help: The ZIA Logs page offers the United States or the European Union and Switzerland.
  - zscaler-help: The Client Connector Logs page says ZIA logs are in the United States, European Union or Australia.
  Impact: The pages do not agree on whether Switzerland or Australia is a ZIA log storage region, so the available regions for ZIA logs are not settled.
