# Subclouds: restricting which Service Edges handle a tenant's traffic (shared.subclouds)
Also called: subcloud; sub cloud; zscaler subcloud; traffic going to the wrong region; eu only service edges; conus subcloud; subcloud vs sublocation; restrict service edges to a region

By default a user's traffic goes to the Public Service Edge geolocation finds nearest; a subcloud narrows the set of edges the tenant's traffic may use. A subcloud is a subset of Public Service Edges for Internet & SaaS (ZIA). [shared.subclouds:definition]

**Why.** The service uses geolocation to find the Public Service Edge closest to the user and forwards web traffic there. When traffic must stay on Public Service Edges in one region, a remote user who has traveled outside it may be forwarded to an edge outside the preferred region; a subcloud ensures the traffic goes to the preferred Public Service Edges. [shared.subclouds:geolocation-default] Zscaler always recommends that organizations forward traffic to the Public Service Edges in the Zscaler cloud, which are deployed active-active worldwide for availability and redundancy. [shared.subclouds:recommend-public] Because a subcloud replaces nearest-edge selection with a fixed set, a user outside the subcloud's region is sent to an edge in the subcloud even when a nearer edge exists outside it. [shared.subclouds:may-be-farther]

**Shapes.** A subcloud can be a subset of Public Service Edges, a subset of Private Service Edges, or a subset of both; it cannot be a subset of Public Service Edges in only one data center. [shared.subclouds:three-shapes] The help's examples: a subcloud of Public Service Edges in one region (for example Europe only, for compliance or regulatory requirements); a subcloud of only the organization's Private Service Edges, so traffic is not forwarded to Public Service Edges; and a subcloud of preferred Public and Private Service Edges, so traffic goes only to the Public Service Edges in that subcloud. [shared.subclouds:shape-uses] A subcloud can ensure that traffic from Zscaler Client Connector, PAC or SD-WAN only connects to specific Zscaler data centers, allow Client Connector and PAC file users to securely connect to regional surcharge data centers, redirect them to the next closest data center, and allow them to securely connect to privately hosted data centers. [shared.subclouds:uses]

**Editing one.** The Subclouds page shows each subcloud's Number of Data Centers (the data centers configured for the subcloud) and Number of Disabled Data Centers (those currently removed from the subcloud data center list). [shared.subclouds:page-columns] An admin can temporarily disable a subcloud's associated Zscaler data centers for scheduled maintenance or data centers with capacity issues, trust incidents, site or region service issues, excessive latency, congestion, peering issues and so on, for a maximum of two weeks. Data Center Disabled Until (UTC Time) shows and edits the date and time until which the selected data center is disabled; the subcloud Name cannot be modified, and disabling all the data centers in a country brings up a confirmation window. [shared.subclouds:disable-data-center] After the data center list of a subcloud is edited, the Zscaler Client Connector traffic and PAC file users are redirected 15 minutes after the app policy push; to fail over immediately, DNS requests can be triggered with app policy updates on Zscaler Client Connector. [shared.subclouds:redirect-after-edit] The hosted PAC files reflect an edit about 5 minutes after the edit, while users are redirected about 10 to 20 minutes after the changes are activated: [A change to a subcloud's data center list takes about 5 minutes to reach Zscaler-hosted PAC files; Client Connector refreshes the app profile PAC every 15 minutes, so users are redirected about 10 to 20 minutes after the change, and Update Policy in the app syncs the app profile PAC immediately.](kb:shared.pac-files.hosting:subcloud-propagation). The two redirect figures differ [divergence:shared.subclouds:redirect-timing]. Whether the two-week limit binds the API's exclusion times is open [gap:shared.subclouds:two-week-api].

**Getting one.** A subcloud is requested with a Zscaler Support ticket; the service sets up the subcloud if the organization has access only to limited, restricted or private data centers. [shared.subclouds:setup-by-support] A subcloud name has up to 32 characters of upper- and lower-case letters, numerals or hyphens, and its first and last characters must be a letter or numeral. [shared.subclouds:name-rules] Whether surcharge or restricted data centers are reachable only through a subcloud is open [gap:shared.subclouds:surcharge-dc-selection]. Reading and editing a subcloud's data center exclusions through the API is in [The subclouds API: reading subclouds and editing their data center exclusions](kb:shared.subclouds.api).

## Pointing clients at a subcloud

Each subcloud is associated with a DNS name that resolves the Public Service Edges in that subcloud; in the subcloud variables, <Subcloud> is replaced with that DNS name and <Zscaler cloud> with the tenant's cloud name. [shared.subclouds:dns-name] [A PAC file for a subcloud must not use ${GATEWAY} or gateway.<Zscaler cloud> in its return statement, or traffic goes to the nearest Public Service Edge, which may be outside the subcloud; it uses ${GATEWAY.<Subcloud>.<Zscaler cloud>} and ${SECONDARY.GATEWAY.<Subcloud>.<Zscaler cloud>}, with _HOST forms for Kerberos and plain host names for applications that do not support PAC files.](kb:shared.pac-files.variables:subcloud-vars) How the secondary variable is spelled differs between help articles: [shared.pac-files.variables:subcloud-secondary-syntax](kb:shared.pac-files.variables:subcloud-secondary-syntax). Whether the subcloud name in a variable is case-sensitive is open [gap:shared.subclouds:name-case].

To keep traffic forwarding within US data centers, PAC files can use the Zscaler-managed subcloud CONUS on `zscaler.net`, `zscalertwo.net` or `zscalerthree.net`, with `${GATEWAY.CONUS.<Zscaler cloud>}` and `${SECONDARY.GATEWAY.CONUS.<Zscaler cloud>}` in the return statement. [shared.subclouds:conus] Because CONUS is Zscaler-managed, using it is a PAC file change rather than a subcloud request to Support. [shared.subclouds:conus-no-request]

Private Service Edge PAC variables must name the subcloud Zscaler configured: [If the organization uses PAC files to forward traffic to the Zscaler service, the PAC variables that point to the Private Service Edges must specify the subcloud Zscaler configured for the organization.](kb:zia.private-service-edge.deployment:pac-subcloud). Edits to a subcloud's data centers reach hosted PAC files and clients in stages: [A change to a subcloud's data center list takes about 5 minutes to reach Zscaler-hosted PAC files; Client Connector refreshes the app profile PAC every 15 minutes, so users are redirected about 10 to 20 minutes after the change, and Update Policy in the app syncs the app profile PAC immediately.](kb:shared.pac-files.hosting:subcloud-propagation).

## What a subcloud does not change

A subcloud and a sublocation act on different things: a subcloud is a set of Service Edges that decides where traffic is processed, while a sublocation classifies traffic inside a location by internal IP address for policy, authentication, bandwidth and reporting, and does not choose the Service Edge. [shared.subclouds:not-sublocations] Dedicated IP egress is independent of subclouds: [Excluding a subcloud does not affect data center selection for dedicated IP addresses: subclouds manage the data centers that service ingress traffic, and dedicated IPs are used on the egress path.](kb:shared.source-ip-anchoring.egress-options:subcloud-independent).

Whether moving traffic to other edges changes which location-based rules apply is open [gap:shared.subclouds:policy-after-move], and so is whether the Central Authority is subcloud-aware [gap:shared.subclouds:central-authority-scope]. How the Business Continuity Cloud treats a subcloud is open [gap:shared.subclouds:business-continuity].

What to check:
1. For traffic seen on unexpected Service Edges, check whether the PAC file or application uses the unqualified gateway variable instead of the subcloud-qualified one.
2. Use the subcloud's DNS name, as configured, in the variable, and the tenant's own cloud name after it.
3. After a subcloud edit, allow for the staged propagation before concluding the change failed; request the app policy update only once the app profile PAC reflects the change.
4. Do not use a sublocation to steer traffic to other Service Edges, or a subcloud to change per-subnet policy.
