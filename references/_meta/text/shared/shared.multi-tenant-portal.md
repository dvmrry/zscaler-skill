# What the Zscaler Multi-Tenant Portal for partners is, how a partner signs in, and where its settings, admins, audit logs, policy templates and tenants are covered (shared.multi-tenant-portal)
Also called: Multi-Tenant Portal for Partners; Management Portal for Partners

The Multi-Tenant Portal is used by Zscaler partners to manage their provisioned customers and tenants, which can use different Zscaler clouds. [shared.multi-tenant-portal:purpose] A partner can have multiple customers, each customer might have multiple tenant organizations linked under it, and the portal shows this hierarchy from the partner account. [shared.multi-tenant-portal:hierarchy] The key features listed are oversight of customers across Zscaler clouds, visibility of customer and tenant configurations and policies, white labeling of tenant admin portals, predefined policy templates, and SSO access to tenant admin portals. [shared.multi-tenant-portal:key-features]

**Signing in.** Zscaler Support configures a default admin role with access to all Multi-Tenant Portal functions and emails a request that the password be reset for that role. [shared.multi-tenant-portal:first-access] The login page language defaults to English (US), and partners who have moved to Authentication Service are redirected to the Authentication Service SSO screen after the Login ID screen. [shared.multi-tenant-portal:login-defaults] The Multi-Tenant Portal logs a user out automatically after 15 minutes of inactivity. [shared.multi-tenant-portal:session-timeout] Whether the idle logout applies on the Authentication Service sign-in path is not stated [gap:shared.multi-tenant-portal:session-timeout-auth-service]. The Multi-Tenant Portal cannot be accessed during an upgrade; a maintenance page is shown for the active upgrade period. [shared.multi-tenant-portal:upgrade-maintenance]

**Navigation.** The Account icon in the left-side navigation shows account information such as the partner account name and admin user ID. [shared.multi-tenant-portal:navigation-items] The icon that opens the partner Authentication Service Admin Portal is visible only to users who have migrated to Authentication Service and have the permissions to access and manage the Multi-Tenant Portal within it. [shared.multi-tenant-portal:authentication-service-icon]
