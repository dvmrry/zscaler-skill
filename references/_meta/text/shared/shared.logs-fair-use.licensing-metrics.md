# What unit the products on the Logs and Fair Use pages are licensed by, and how it is counted (shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics)

ZIA is licensed by Seat (unique ZIA users over the previous rolling 90-day period; total endpoints across all seats may not exceed three times the seats purchased), Site, ZIA Monthly Traffic, Workload or Data Center. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:zia-types] ZIA Monthly Traffic is the Authenticated plus Unauthenticated Traffic uploaded and downloaded via ZIA in a calendar month, measured in GB; Authenticated Traffic is traffic of a known seat, Unauthenticated Traffic is traffic not related to a known seat, including inline guest Wi-Fi, device, server and workload traffic. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:zia-traffic] For ZIA Site licenses, an IoT site counts only the parent location, and in an IoT hub-and-spoke deployment each spoke or branch behind the hub counts as a site; a Workload is a unique domain name, an IP address or a set of IP addresses; a Data Center license's IP address pair cannot be moved to another data center during the subscription. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:zia-site-workload] In the ZIA licensing page, Zero Trust Browser (Isolation or Cloud Browser Isolation) and Source IP Anchoring (SIPA) are subscribed per seat with a maximum aggregate amount of ZIA Monthly Traffic across all seats. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:zia-addons] The fair-use thresholds are in [What fair-use thresholds, overage and burst rules each Zscaler product's page states](kb:shared.logs-fair-use.fair-use-limits) and the hub is [Where to find a Zscaler product's log retention, log contents, license unit and fair-use limit](kb:shared.logs-fair-use).

**ZPA.** ZPA is licensed by Seat (unique ZPA users over the previous rolling 90-day period), Site (enables Zero Trust SD-WAN), ZPA Monthly Traffic (GB per calendar month), System (each RDP, SSH, VNC or RealVNC target enabling PRA), Workload, and Asset (for Autonomous App Shield). [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:zpa-types] For ZPA Autonomous App Shield, an asset is a host, application or domain in ZPA, and each asset includes an allocation of Shield Tokens and scans; additional tokens may be purchased separately. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:zpa-asset] In the ZPA licensing page, Zero Trust Browser and Source IP Anchoring (SIPA) are subscribed per seat with a maximum aggregate amount of ZPA Monthly Traffic across all seats, and ZPA B2B Federation is subscribed per seat. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:zpa-addons]

**Seat-counted products.** ZDX is licensed by seats, counted as unique ZDX users over the previous rolling 90-day period; it should be deployed for the licensed number using selective entitlement and need not cover all Client Connector users. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:zdx] Zero Trust Browser (Cloud Browser, Browser Extension, Enterprise Browser) is licensed by seats, counted as unique users over a rolling 90-day period, and Cloud Browser usage is also subject to the data limits in the SKU. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:ztbrowser] Breach Predictor is licensed by seats counted as unique Breach Predictor users over the previous rolling 90-day period. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:breach-predictor] Business Insights is licensed by seats, being users for which it tracks SaaS license usage, counted as unique users over the previous rolling 90-day period. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:business-insights] Deception is licensed by seats counted as unique Deception users over the previous rolling 90-day period. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:deception] Risk360 is licensed by seats, but the page counts the licenses required as unique ZIA users (not Risk360 users) over the previous rolling 90-day period. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:risk360] SSPM is licensed by Seat (unique users over the previous rolling 90-day period) and Application; the "2 Apps" subscription allows up to two supported SaaS applications with one tenant per application, and seat licensing continues to apply alongside application licenses. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:sspm] EASM is licensed by Seat (unique EASM users over the previous rolling 90-day period) or Asset (unique public internet-facing assets: domains, hosts, web pages, SSL/TLS certificates, IP addresses). [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:easm]

**Cloud and workload products.** DSPM is licensed by data stores: each unique VM counts as one data store, plus storage buckets, databases and any additional supported service configured as a scanned service over the subscription period; deploy using selective account onboarding. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:dspm] ZPC is licensed by workloads: each VM counts as one workload, every 5 serverless functions count as one workload and every 5 containers count as one workload, over the subscription period. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:zpc] Effective April 19, 2024, the Zscaler Workload Communications licensing and fair use policy was replaced by the ZIA and/or ZPA policies, respectively; the page points to the licensing metrics for Workload under those policies. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:workload-comms] Zero Trust Gateway is licensed by Monthly Traffic (GB; includes ZIA and ZPA workload entitlement, with an additional 10 GB of Layer-4 east-west and ingress enforcement for every 1 GB of ZIA and ZPA egress) and Gateway Instance (an instance in an AWS Availability Zone; at least two instances are needed to use a gateway in an AWS region). [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:ztg] AI Asset Management for AI Infrastructure is licensed by AI assets: custom AI applications, AI models, MCP endpoints or AI agents in public cloud environments or SaaS AI builder platforms, counted as unique ones configured as scanned over the subscription period. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:ai-infra]

**Device and branch products.** Zero Trust Branch Connector is licensed by sites/locations protected and data usage; protected devices are unique IP addresses of the endpoint types, counted hourly and averaged over a month, and Guest Wi-Fi SKU endpoints and GB are excluded. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:branch-connector] Zero Trust Branch is licensed by the number of appliances enabled for protected devices, counted as installed appliances for each of the protected devices. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:ztbranch] Zero Trust Device Segmentation (formerly Airgap) is licensed by gateways enabled for protected devices, counted as installed gateways, with additional entitlements allowing more devices per gateway. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:devseg] ZSDK is licensed by Monthly Active Users (MAUs) for a client mobile app: an active user logs into the app at least once in a calendar month, and the MAUs required are unique monthly active users counted over the previous rolling 90-day period. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:zsdk] Zscaler Cellular is licensed by protected cellular devices (Zscaler SIMs purchased, or unique IP addresses accessing Cellular Edge) and data usage; a data pool resets on the first day of each calendar month, unused data is not carried over, and a partial first month is prorated. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:cellular] Endpoint AI Security Security is licensed by protected endpoints: host machines where the Endpoint AI Security Security module is installed and running, whether via Client Connector or a standalone installation. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:endpoint-ai]

**AI products.** AI Guard is licensed by a platform subscription fee and tokens; a token is generally four characters (UTF-8 code points, excluding white space) and consumption is the total tokens in prompts and responses. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:ai-guard] AI Scanning Platform is licensed by a platform fee and Probe Units: a Standard Probe is a single analysis function and a Large Probe is one that exceeds 200 test cases; Probe Units are consumed when you run a Standard Probe, Large Probe or Scanner Run. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:ai-scanning] AI Asset Management Platform is licensed by seats for users whose traffic is monitored for AI application usage (generative, embedded and endpoint AI applications); a provisioned ZIA tenant is for AI Asset Management traffic visibility only, with no other ZIA capabilities unless separately licensed. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:ai-platform]

**Entity and asset products.** Agentic SOC is licensed by entities, each a unique identity, endpoint or cloud resource, counted as the entities processed and stored in Agentic SOC. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:secops] AEM is licensed by assets, counted as the assets processed and stored into AEM. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:aem] UVM is licensed by assets, counted as the total assets processed and stored in UVM. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:uvm] Identity Protection is licensed by user identities, counted as those processed and stored in it; a user identity is each unique individual provisioned, managed or represented in the application whether or not they log in. [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:idp] Network Observability is licensed by Device (unique IP addresses configured over the subscription period) and Metrics (monitored metrics, which vary by networking device type). [shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:netobs] Some pages repeat wording from a different license unit [gap:shared.logs-fair-use.licensing-metrics:copied-unit-wording].
