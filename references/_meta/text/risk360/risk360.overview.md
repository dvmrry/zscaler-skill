# Risk360: what it measures, which data it uses, how long it keeps logs, and what can be automated (risk360.overview)
Also called: risk360; zscaler risk360; risk 360; cyber risk quantification; risk360 api; risk360 data sources; risk360 log retention; zra

Risk360 quantifies cyber risk from data the tenant's other Zscaler services already produce. Risk360 analyzes an organization's security data and provides real-time risk metrics at the organization level and for four attack stages: External Attack Surface, Compromise, Lateral Propagation and Data Loss. [risk360.overview:what-it-is]

**Features.** Its listed features are holistic risk analysis with the factors driving risk, financial exposure through Monte Carlo simulation, risk reporting for stakeholders, peer benchmarking against industry peers, compliance support against major frameworks, and deployment through the Zero Trust Exchange without additional hardware upgrades. [risk360.overview:features] How the scores, factors and financial estimates are computed is in [How Risk360 scores risk: the organization score, factors, asset-level risk and Monte Carlo financial estimates](kb:risk360.scoring), the Financial Risk and Monte Carlo Simulation pages are in [Risk360 Financial Risk page, Financial Risk Settings and the Monte Carlo Simulation page](kb:risk360.financial-risk), and compliance frameworks and downloadable reports are in [Risk360 compliance frameworks, framework pages and downloadable risk reports](kb:risk360.frameworks-and-reports). Alerting is in [Risk360 alerts: rule criteria and throttling, alert lifecycle, causes, webhooks, email and the ServiceNow sample](kb:risk360.alerts); Seeds Management, admin roles, audit logs and the Score Change Log are in [Risk360 administration: Seeds Management domains, admin roles and permissions, audit logs and the Score Change Log](kb:risk360.administration).

## Data it uses

Zscaler's Risk360 product page says Risk360 delivers cyber risk quantification by ingesting data from the existing Zscaler deployment, naming data from ZIA, ZPA, DLP policies, ThreatLabz security research and external attack surface metrics as the telemetry it uses. [risk360.overview:telemetry-sources] Risk360 supports integrations with other vendors, such as CrowdStrike, to gather risky events and activities and translate them into Risk360 factors. [risk360.overview:crowdstrike] Risk360 integrates with Zscaler Data Fabric, which ingests Zscaler and third-party data sources and harmonizes, deduplicates, correlates and enriches them for Risk360 risk quantification; connectors are configured in the Data Fabric. [risk360.overview:data-fabric] Third-party integration is available with the Risk360 Advanced license, and third-party custom risk factors are defined and implemented through the Zscaler Account team. [risk360.overview:third-party-license] Out-of-the-box CrowdStrike factors need a connector: Zero Trust Score and Endpoint Security CrowdScore use CrowdStrike Crowdscore; Unsupported Devices, Unmanaged Devices and End-of-Life Operating System use CrowdStrike Environment Assets; Critical and High Incidents uses CrowdStrike Incidents; High Severity XDR Detections uses CrowdStrike Alerts. [risk360.overview:crowdstrike-factors] Out-of-the-box identity factors: CrowdStrike - Critical Domain Users Having High Privileges uses the CrowdStrike Identity Protection - Domain Users connector, and CrowdStrike - Identity Protection for Active Directory uses CrowdStrike Identity Protection - Security Assessment. [risk360.overview:crowdstrike-identity-factors] The factors Critical and High Severity Vulnerabilities, Highly Exploitable Vulnerabilities and Unaddressed Critical and High Severity Vulnerabilities each list the connectors CrowdStrike Vulnerabilities, Qualys Vulnerabilities, Tenable Vulnerability Management, Rapid7 InsightVM, Wiz Vulnerability Findings and Microsoft Defender for Endpoints - Vulnerabilities. [risk360.overview:vulnerability-factors] The factors Is WAF Enabled, Is WAF Enforced and Is DDoS Protection Enabled use the F5 WAF Data connector. [risk360.overview:waf-factors] Connector deployment guides: Microsoft Defender for Endpoints - Vulnerabilities, the Zscaler UVM and Microsoft Services Deployment Guide; CrowdStrike Incidents, CrowdStrike Alerts, CrowdStrike Environment Assets and CrowdStrike Vulnerabilities, the Zscaler and CrowdStrike Deployment Guide; Qualys, Tenable and Wiz, UVM guides named for each; Rapid7 InsightVM, the Zscaler and Rapid7 Deployment Guide. [risk360.overview:connector-deployment-guides] The article lists no guide for the CrowdStrike Crowdscore, CrowdStrike Identity Protection or F5 WAF Data connectors [gap:risk360.overview:connector-guides-missing], and the Advanced license is not defined [gap:risk360.overview:advanced-license]. For Risk360, logs are duplicates of the configuration and transaction logs of other Zscaler SaaS products, such as ZIA or ZPA. [risk360.overview:logs-definition] Zscaler retains Risk360 logs on a rolling period of up to a year during the subscription term, depending on the subscription, deletes them by its retention cycles after the term ends, and stores them in the United States or the European Union as chosen during deployment. [risk360.overview:logs-retention]

**ZAgent.** Certain Risk360 features operate through the ZAgent service, which may use one or more sub-agents. [risk360.overview:zagent-features] The Risk360 agent's entitlement is in [An agent's availability depends on subscriptions: the Digital Experience Agent is complimentary with Digital Experience's Advanced Plus (without it, only that agent's Knowledge Skill is available), the Risk360 Agent with Risk360, and the Data Protection Agent with Data Protection.](kb:unified.zagent:agent-subscriptions).

## Automation

No vendored client covers Risk360: the Go and Python SDKs have no Risk360 service, and the Terraform providers, Ansible collections, MCP server, OneAPI Postman collection and captured Automate contract have no Risk360 resource, module, tool or operation. [risk360.overview:no-client-surface] The MCP server's entitlement filter, which maps the product codes in the OneAPI bearer token's service-info claim to its services, lists RISK360 among product codes with no MCP service: such codes are skipped, with a warning naming each one. [risk360.overview:mcp-no-service] A ZIdentity resource-server response recorded in the Python SDK's integration tests lists, under the Zscaler APIs resource server, a service named `ZRA` with display name Risk360 and the scopes Role:Risk360 Read-only Admin and Role:Risk360 Super Admin. [risk360.overview:zidentity-resource-server] Whether a Risk360 API or export automation exists is open [gap:risk360.overview:api-surface]. The Risk360 cloud domain is in [The same list gives `goairgap.com` for Zero Trust Branch, `ztsim.com` for Zscaler Cellular, `zscalerrisk.net` for Zscaler Risk360 and `zslogin.net` for the Authentication Service.](kb:shared.cloud-architecture.cloud-names:other-services).
