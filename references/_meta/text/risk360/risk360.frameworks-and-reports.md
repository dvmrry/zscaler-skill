# Risk360 compliance frameworks, framework pages and downloadable risk reports (risk360.frameworks-and-reports)
Also called: risk360 compliance; risk360 reports; risk360 ciso board slides; risk360 cyber insurance addendum

Risk360 supports these risk frameworks: ISO 27001, MITRE ATT&CK, NIST CSF, NIST SP 800-53, DORA, NIS2, HIPAA, PCI DSS, CIS, CAF, HITRUST, HECVAT, SOC 2 and GDPR. [risk360.frameworks-and-reports:supported-frameworks] Risk360 supports both NIST CSF versions, 1.1 and 2.0, in the Zscaler Admin Console. [risk360.frameworks-and-reports:nist-versions]

**Framework pages.** On a framework page each tile shows a technique name and ID; blue tiles are covered by Zscaler protection, and a green or red mark at the right of a tile shows whether the protection is configured correctly or not. [risk360.frameworks-and-reports:framework-page-colors] The framework page Legend shows a donut chart of techniques covered by Zscaler versus not, with the total percentage of technique coverage at its center, and a Configurations count of misconfigured and correctly configured configurations or policies. [risk360.frameworks-and-reports:framework-legend] In the drawer view for a technique, Zscaler Comment is a note on how Zscaler can help mitigate the attack technique and shows no information if the mitigation strategy isn't available, TTP to Zscaler Product Mapping gives the feature, whether it is licensed and the Risk360 category, and TTP to Risk360 Factor Mapping maps the sub-techniques to factors; in the drawer view for a sub-technique, Zscaler Comment is the same kind of note, and TTP to 3rd Party Tools Mapping shows whether the sub-technique is securely configured, misconfigured, or not covered by third-party security control policies. [risk360.frameworks-and-reports:technique-drawer] Techniques can be searched by name or by the mapped factor, and hovering a tile shows the Zscaler Control, Related Risk360 Factors and Licensed?. [risk360.frameworks-and-reports:technique-search-hover] Control counts and how coverage and misconfiguration are decided are not stated [gap:risk360.frameworks-and-reports:framework-method].

**Reports.** The Risk360 Reports page (Analytics > Reports > Risk360 Reports) holds CISO Board Slides (a PowerPoint file), an Attack Surface Report, a Cybersecurity Maturity Assessment, SEC Disclosures, Miscellaneous materials, the Risk Management Mitigation Strategy Report, Operating Entity Risk and a Cyber Insurance Addendum. [risk360.frameworks-and-reports:report-catalog] The Reports page says a new CISO Board Slides slide is generated for a period of 7 days; the Risk Management Mitigation Strategy Report is autogenerated once a week; Operating Entity Risk is a weekly and on-demand report; the Cyber Insurance Addendum is a quarterly and on-demand report. [risk360.frameworks-and-reports:report-schedules] The Cybersecurity Maturity Assessment is generated by a custom large language model developed by Zscaler; SEC Disclosures is a document with a sample format and content that can be a starting point for security and legal teams addressing the SEC's new cyber risk reporting regulations. [risk360.frameworks-and-reports:report-llm-sec] Lead time, retention and entitlement per report are not stated [gap:risk360.frameworks-and-reports:report-details].

What to check:
1. Which framework page and technique tile the question is about, and whether its blue coverage tile carries a green or red configuration mark.
2. Which report's stated cadence applies (weekly, on-demand, quarterly or the CISO Board Slides' 7-day period) before saying when a report is available.

Not known (open gaps):
- Control counts per framework and how coverage and misconfiguration are decided [gap:risk360.frameworks-and-reports:framework-method]: settled by source-read or lab-test
- Report generation lead time, retention and entitlement [gap:risk360.frameworks-and-reports:report-details]: settled by source-read or support-ticket
