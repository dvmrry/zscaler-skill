# How Risk360 scores risk: the organization score, factors, asset-level risk and Monte Carlo financial estimates (risk360.scoring)
Also called: risk360 score; risk360 severity ranges; risk360 factors; risk360 factor count; risk360 asset risk; risk360 monte carlo; risk360 financial risk; risk360 peer score; exclude risk360 factor; risk360 dashboard; top 10 factors; risk360 top factors
Former IDs: risk360-02

## The organization score

The organization risk score is an average across four categories: External Attack Surface, Compromise, Lateral Propagation and Data Loss; the Dashboard shows it beside the industry peer average. [risk360.scoring:org-score] Score severities are Low (0–25), Medium (26–50), High (51–75) and Critical (76–100). [risk360.scoring:severity-ranges] Different risk factors bear different weights on the score; for example, an active infection is more severe than a blocked access attempt to a blocked destination. [risk360.scoring:weights] The Risk Score Trend shows the organization and peer average scores for the last 90 days and can be downloaded as CSV; the peer score's calculation strategy is Zscaler-defined by default, and a custom strategy can be created. [risk360.scoring:trend-and-peers] A custom peer score strategy's attributes are chosen from Industry Vertical, Geographical Region and Revenue ID (revenue range). [risk360.scoring:peer-strategy-attributes] Deleting a peer score strategy that is enabled makes the Zscaler service enable the default strategy; the default strategy cannot be deleted, only modified. [risk360.scoring:peer-strategy-delete] The Dashboard has a Top 10 Factors section, the top 10 factors contributing to the organization's risk score, and a Top 10 Events section, the top 10 events observed within the last 90 days numbered in order of occurrence. [risk360.scoring:dashboard-top-ten] The Dashboard's factors table shows each contributing factor's Your Score (0 being a healthy score), a Last 30 Days graph and Recommended Actions. [risk360.scoring:dashboard-factor-columns] The Dashboard's Key Events enlarged view shows the top 10 events on the Risk Score Trend graph, with the score change and contributing factors on hover; View All Events opens the Score Change Log page. [risk360.scoring:dashboard-key-events] The High Impact Recommendations section shows top factors with high impact on the risk score and the recommendation to lower it; each recommendation has its category name, the day discovered, the consequence of not implementing it and the trend if applicable, and View All opens the Insights page. [risk360.scoring:dashboard-high-impact] The Dashboard's Risk Event by Location map plots risky events by geolocation derived from the client or server IP; because geo-IP lookup is only possible for a subset of risky events, it represents a small fraction of overall risky events. [risk360.scoring:dashboard-risk-event-map] Hovering the dollar symbol in the Organization Risk Score section shows the financial risk estimates, and View Details opens the Financial Risk page. [risk360.scoring:dashboard-financial-hover]

## Factors

Risk360 quantifies each contributing factor by its risk weight, which adds to the organization score, and maps factors to risk and security frameworks such as MITRE and NIST. [risk360.scoring:factor-weighting] The Dashboard attributes contributing factors to four entities: Workforce (risky user activity), 3rd Parties (activity by third-party users such as contractors), Applications (unsanctioned or less secure SaaS applications) and Assets (exposed organizational assets). [risk360.scoring:entities] The Factors page has two views: Attack-Based, which lists factors in a single list, and Entity-Based, which lists factors within their category. [risk360.scoring:factor-views] Each factor shows a Licensed? value saying whether the tenant subscribes to the feature required to implement the recommended action. [risk360.scoring:licensed-column] Every factor is included in the score by default; a factor can be excluded (for example, for a compensating control) only with an explanation, and the change is recorded in the audit logs with the username and reason. [risk360.scoring:factor-exclusion] The contributing factors can be exported to a CSV file, which contains all factors regardless of any filter selected at download time. [risk360.scoring:factor-csv-export] The Include column shows whether the factor is enabled for risk score computation. [risk360.scoring:factor-include-column] Factors can be excluded from score computation for a compensating control or any other reason; compensating controls are supplementary security measures against identified risks, for example multi-factor authentication, firewalls and antivirus software. [risk360.scoring:factor-compensating-controls] Clicking any of a factor's columns except Licensed?, Include and Entities opens a drawer with a Details tab (severity, description with help links and a link to the responsible Zscaler service's admin portal if available, related recommendation cards) and a Compliance tab listing the recognized cybersecurity frameworks and control IDs mapped to the factor. [risk360.scoring:factor-drawer] How many factors exist depends on the source [divergence:risk360.scoring:factor-count], and the catalog, weights and peer method are not published [gap:risk360.scoring:factor-methodology]. The frameworks a factor maps to are in [Risk360 compliance frameworks, framework pages and downloadable risk reports](kb:risk360.frameworks-and-reports).

## Insights and investigation

The Insights page updates in real time with risks and recommendations discovered in the Zscaler environment, and lists each problem with its title, category, day generated, problem statement (Problem), recommendation and trend (if applicable). [risk360.scoring:insights-page] The Explore page presents multiple sections of a problem's affected areas, and each section's Investigate link leads to the Investigate page, which shows detailed data in tabular form for each asset involved, with filter options that vary by section. [risk360.scoring:explore-investigate] On the Investigate page, where available, an entity can be included or excluded from risk score computation via an Include toggle with an explanation in the Entity Override Note window; several entities can be changed at once with one explanation, and the change is captured in the audit logs with the username and reason. [risk360.scoring:entity-override] The Score Change Log and audit logs are in [Risk360 administration: Seeds Management domains, admin roles and permissions, audit logs and the Score Change Log](kb:risk360.administration).

## Asset-level risk

The asset-level risk model considers more than 65 indicators, each contributing by the severity and frequency of the associated threat, grouped into three categories: pre-infection behavior, post-infection behavior and suspicious behavior. [risk360.scoring:asset-indicators] The Assets page's Risk Score view groups asset risk into five severities: No Risk (0–1), Low (>1–25), Medium (>25–50), High (>50–75) and Critical (>75–100). [risk360.scoring:asset-bands] The Assets page's asset-level bands differ from the organization-level score severities: they add a No Risk band (0–1) and start Low above 1. [risk360.scoring:asset-bands-differ-from-org] The Risky Asset Inventory table shows the top 1,000 risky assets, each with an asset ID assigned by the Zscaler service, private and egress IP address, username, asset type, authentication status, risk score, last seen time and location (city), and can be downloaded as CSV. [risk360.scoring:asset-inventory] The Risky Asset Inventory shows Road Warrior as the Location if the asset isn't tied to a location, can be filtered by Asset ID, Asset Type or Location, and searched by asset ID. [risk360.scoring:asset-inventory-operators] Sample asset-risk indicators are: pre-infection, blocked malware (Advanced Threat Protection or inline Sandbox), malicious URLs, phishing sites and browser-exploit pages; post-infection, botnet and command-and-control traffic; suspicious, denylisted URLs and anonymizing sites. [risk360.scoring:asset-indicator-samples] Suspicious-behavior indicators are similar to pre-infection indicators, with less severity and less guarantee of leading to infection. [risk360.scoring:asset-suspicious-vs-pre] The Assets page Location view's map shows the country where assets are located, with bubble size signifying asset count relative to other locations, the number of assets with unknown locations at top-left, and a Top Risky Locations list of the locations with the highest percentage of risky assets in descending order. [risk360.scoring:asset-location-view] The Assets page shows a pie chart of the percentage split of authenticated versus unauthenticated assets, and an Authenticated Assets chart of asset types with counts for authenticated assets only. [risk360.scoring:asset-auth-charts] The Asset Details page shows Asset ID, Username, Private IP, Egress IP, Location, Department, Asset Type, Last Seen, Authentication Status, Enrolled Device Type Version (the Zscaler Client Connector version installed on the asset), Device Hostname, OS Type and OS Version. [risk360.scoring:asset-details-metadata] The Asset Details page lists the top events of the last 7 days that changed the asset's risk score in ascending order with timestamp, each with Threat Category, Threat Name, URL Category and URL Name; they can be exported to CSV and all events viewed in a drawer. [risk360.scoring:asset-details-events] The Asset Details page's asset risk score severities are Low (1–25), Medium (26–50), High (51–75) and Critical (76–100). [risk360.scoring:asset-details-severity] The Asset Details page lists different severity bands from the Assets page [divergence:risk360.scoring:asset-severity-bands], and describes Location differently from the Risky Asset Inventory [divergence:risk360.scoring:asset-location-meaning].

## Financial estimates

Zscaler's Monte Carlo explainer says Risk360 runs the simulation for an organization one thousand times per day; each iteration measures financial loss from a randomized cyber breach event and a randomized financial loss within a predefined confidence interval. [risk360.scoring:monte-carlo-runs] The explainer says the simulation results produce a yearly average loss and a loss exceedance curve, the probability that a loss exceeds a given amount. [risk360.scoring:monte-carlo-outputs] The explainer says the process is repeated four times to calculate those outputs under four scenarios: inherent risk (the current score), residual risk (after mitigating the top ten risk factors), the last 30-day average risk, and industry peer risk (the average score of peer organizations). [risk360.scoring:monte-carlo-scenarios] The Financial Risk page, its settings and the Monte Carlo Simulation page are in [Risk360 Financial Risk page, Financial Risk Settings and the Monte Carlo Simulation page](kb:risk360.financial-risk).

What to check:
1. Whether a factor was excluded from the score (excluding needs a reason and is recorded in the audit logs) when a score looks lower than expected.
2. Whether the recommended action for a factor needs a feature the tenant does not have, from the Licensed? column.
3. Which peer score strategy is in use (Zscaler-defined default or custom) before comparing the score with the peer average.
4. Quote the factor count with its source and date, not as a fixed number.

Sources disagree (name both sides):
- The Assets page and the Asset Details page list different asset risk severity bands. [divergence:risk360.scoring:asset-severity-bands]
  - zscaler-help: The Assets page lists No Risk (0–1), Low (>1–25), Medium (>25–50), High (>50–75) and Critical (>75–100).
  - zscaler-help: The Asset Details page lists Low (1–25), Medium (26–50), High (51–75) and Critical (76–100), with no No Risk band.
  Impact: State which page a severity band comes from; an asset scoring between 0 and 1 has a No Risk band only on the Assets page.
- The Assets page and the Asset Details page describe an asset's Location differently. [divergence:risk360.scoring:asset-location-meaning]
  - zscaler-help: The Risky Asset Inventory's Location is the city where the asset is located.
  - zscaler-help: The Asset Details page's Location is the location ID assigned to the asset's workplace.
  Impact: Do not equate the inventory's Location with a location ID when filtering or matching assets across the two pages.
- Zscaler's Risk360 pages give three different counts of factors. [divergence:risk360.scoring:factor-count]
  - zscaler-help: Risk360 accounts for 140+ predefined factors.
  - zscaler-help: Risk360 develops a risk score based on more than 115 predefined risk factors.
  - zscaler-help: Asset-level risk is described as built on over 100 factors that can drill down to specific users and locations.
  Impact: No exact factor count can be stated; the catalog grows and the pages were written at different times.
