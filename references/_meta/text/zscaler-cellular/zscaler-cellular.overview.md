# Zscaler Cellular: Zscaler SIM, Cellular Edge and how SIM traffic reaches ZIA and ZPA policy (zscaler-cellular.overview)
Also called: zscaler cellular; zcell; zscaler sim; cellular edge; iot sim zero trust; esim zscaler; cellular imei imsi policy

Zscaler Cellular (ZCell) secures IoT and mobile devices through a Zscaler-issued SIM instead of an agent. This subject covers the architecture, Cellular Edge deployment and the setup sequence; SIMs, deployment credentials, monitoring and the API have their own subjects.

**How traffic flows.** Zscaler Cellular provides connectivity for IoT and mobile devices on a Zero Trust architecture and is made up of two products, Zscaler SIM and Zscaler Cellular Edge. [zscaler-cellular.overview:what-it-is] A device with a Zscaler SIM connects to public 4G/5G networks; the SIM routes its traffic to the nearest Cellular Edge, which forwards it to the Zero Trust Exchange for inspection and policy enforcement. [zscaler-cellular.overview:traffic-path] A Zscaler SIM is a data-only SIM that integrates directly with the Zero Trust Exchange, for IoT devices where agent-based solutions are not feasible; devices on it need no software agents. [zscaler-cellular.overview:sim-data-only] Cellular Edge forwards traffic from or to a Zscaler SIM to the Zero Trust Exchange as an egress point, and provides traffic aggregation (funnelling cellular traffic to the ZTE), bidirectional traffic control (secure communication between devices and their endpoints), high availability through failover infrastructure and telemetry on SIM traffic. [zscaler-cellular.overview:edge-role]

**Policy.** Traffic is subject to ZIA or ZPA policy based on constructs such as IP address, IMEI and IMSI. [zscaler-cellular.overview:policy-identifiers] After successful deployment of Cellular Edges, policies are configured in the Zscaler Admin Console, the Cellular Edge appears as a location in Internet & SaaS, and different types of locations or devices can be configured with sublocations. [zscaler-cellular.overview:edge-as-location] Which ZIA or ZPA objects carry those identifiers, and tenant entitlement, are open [gap:zscaler-cellular.overview:entitlement-and-policy-mapping].

**Offerings and admin tasks.** The product page lists two deployment options: Zscaler Cellular Service, plug-and-play agentless security for cellular IoT devices such as vending machines, EV chargers and kiosks that forwards traffic to the Zscaler platform for inspection, granular policy enforcement and anomaly detection; and Zscaler Cellular Partner Service, a partner-managed solution that forwards IoT cellular traffic via SIM infrastructure to the Zscaler platform with high availability and failover. [zscaler-cellular.overview:service-motions] The Cellular help index has articles on Cellular Edge deployment and monitoring, SIM status, IMEI association, eSIM assignment and activation, SIM tags, SIM location groups and geofence anomaly detection policies. [zscaler-cellular.overview:help-topics] The step-by-step guide orders setup as Step 1: Set Up Your Account, Step 2: Configure Credentials for Cellular Edge Deployment, Step 3: Deploy Cellular Edges, Step 4: Configure Polices, Step 5: Deploy Zscaler SIMs and Step 6: Monitor SIM Usage. [zscaler-cellular.overview:setup-steps]

**Cellular Edge deployment.** Zscaler deploys and maintains Cellular Edges, which are access on-ramps to the Zero Trust Exchange, and the deployment and service status of the Cellular Edges can be monitored in the Admin Console. [zscaler-cellular.overview:edge-operations] Zscaler Cellular offers coverage expansion in three regions: AMER, EMEA and APAC. [zscaler-cellular.overview:edge-regions] New Cellular Edges are requested on the Deployment Regions page by choosing a region and clicking Request Deployment, which submits the request to Zscaler; a subscription for the region is needed first. [zscaler-cellular.overview:edge-request] The Deployment Regions map colours each region by status: Online (green, fully operational), Disruption (red, complete outage or unavailability), Degradation (gray, reduced performance or partial issues) and Pending Deployment (yellow, deployment underway but not yet active). [zscaler-cellular.overview:edge-status-colours] After a Cellular Edge deployment request, Zscaler starts deploying and setting up the hardware and software systems that provide the egress points for the customer's IoT and mobile devices. [zscaler-cellular.overview:edge-request-followup] Lead time and approval after a request are open [gap:zscaler-cellular.overview:edge-request-lead-time].

**Entitlements.** Administrative entitlements for Zscaler Cellular need the Cellular Edge service enabled; enabling Cellular Edge needs Internet & SaaS, Private Access and Branch Connector, and Cellular Edge is not supported for an organization with more than one Internet & SaaS or Private Access service. [zscaler-cellular.overview:cellular-edge-prereqs]

What to check:
1. Whether the device's traffic is reaching a Cellular Edge at all before looking at ZIA or ZPA policy.
2. Which identifier (IP address, IMEI or IMSI) the policy is meant to match on.
3. Which step of the setup sequence the customer has reached: account, credentials, Cellular Edge deployment, policies, SIM insertion or monitoring.
