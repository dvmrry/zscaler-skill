# DLP domain profiles and recipient profiles (zia.dlp.email-profiles)
Also called: domain profile; domain profiles; recipient profile; recipient profiles; email profiles

Domain profiles and recipient profiles are reusable groupings that DLP policy rules include or exclude. Domain profiles are used as part of Zscaler's Data Loss Prevention tools to detect and take action on sensitive data across multiple channels. They group organizational domains, custom domains and personal email providers into a single entity that is applied to policy rules, and editing a profile updates all policy rules where it is used. [zia.dlp.email-profiles:domain-profile-what] A domain profile has a Profile Name, Top Personal Email Service Providers chosen from a list, and Custom Domains entered separated by line breaks. Enabling Include Organizational Domains automatically includes the domains in the Organization Profile. An optional description cannot exceed 256 characters. The list shows each profile's recipient domains, and profiles can be edited, duplicated or deleted. [zia.dlp.email-profiles:domain-profile-fields] Recipient profiles help detect and manage sensitive data being sent to specific people, and are part of Zscaler's Data Loss Prevention tools for policy rules across multiple channels. The page says they manage individual users and groups to include in or exclude from outbound email policy rules, and editing one updates all policy rules where it is used. [zia.dlp.email-profiles:recipient-profile-what] A recipient profile has recipient email addresses entered separated by line breaks, and an optional description that cannot exceed 256 characters. Editing offers deleting a recipient email address, and profiles can be edited, duplicated or deleted. [zia.dlp.email-profiles:recipient-profile-fields] The Outbound Email DLP configuration guide calls domain profiles and recipient profiles together email profiles, which give the flexibility to apply policy actions to specific domains, users, groups and departments. [zia.dlp.email-profiles:email-profiles-term] Outbound email rules take up to 8 of each, in [Outbound Email DLP policy: smart host flow, per-recipient evaluation, criteria and header actions](kb:zia.dlp.outbound-email-policy). The Ranges & Limitations page lists 32 domain profiles and 32 recipient profiles per organization, 32 recipients per recipient profile, 8 domain profiles and 8 recipient profiles per rule, and 32 custom domains per domain profile; for the organization-level profile counts, the recipients per profile and the custom domains per profile it says to contact Zscaler Support for a possible increase, to 8,192 total recipients and from 32 to 1,024 domains respectively. [zia.dlp.email-profiles:profile-limits-from-ranges] What the limits do not cover is open [gap:zia.dlp.email-profiles:profile-limits].
