# Excluding a data center from a forwarding method (IPSec tunnels) for a period (zia.traffic-forwarding.dc-exclusion)
Also called: dc exclusion; data center exclusion traffic forwarding; exclude data center ipsec; dcExclusions

DC Exclusion for Traffic Forwarding lets an admin take a data center out of service to the organization for a period; the data center exclusions of a subcloud are in [The subclouds API: reading subclouds and editing their data center exclusions](kb:shared.subclouds.api). To enable the DC Exclusion for Traffic Forwarding option for a tenant, a Support ticket is submitted from the Zscaler Admin Console. [zia.traffic-forwarding.dc-exclusion:enable-by-ticket]

**What it does.** Admins can disable all IPSec VPN tunnels terminating at a virtual IP (VIP) address of the affected data center from the Admin Console, which triggers a failover from primary to secondary tunnels at the endpoint on the organization's side; tunnel failover is then performed according to the VPN endpoint device configuration. The page gives IPSec VPN tunnels as an example in the Traffic Forwarding Method field. [zia.traffic-forwarding.dc-exclusion:what-it-does] When the exclusion is saved and activated, disabling the tunnels is logged in the Audit Logs; a notification from the Zscaler Central Authority is sent to the data center that it is excluded from service to the organization; any existing tunnels to it are brought down immediately and no new tunnels from the organization can be established during the exclusion; and the tunnel status and the reason they are down are logged in the Tunnel Insights Logs. [zia.traffic-forwarding.dc-exclusion:on-activation] Whether methods other than IPSec can be excluded is open [gap:zia.traffic-forwarding.dc-exclusion:method-scope].

**Time window and restore.** An admin selects the data center and the duration (up to 15 days) of the exclusion. The exclusion can be set to begin within a month of the current date, with a Begin Time at least 5 minutes from the current time; the expiration can be set within 15 days of the Begin Time and at least 2 hours from the Begin Time. A warning icon appears when the exclusion is expired. [zia.traffic-forwarding.dc-exclusion:timing-rules] The data center is restored to service when the configured exclusion expires, or if an admin takes action before the expiration; a new Central Authority notification is then sent, existing tunnels can be re-established and new tunnels can be established. An exclusion can be renewed after it expires or edited. Separately, the page lists among its benefits disabling and enabling tunnels according to the organization's evolving requirements without needing to delete credentials. [zia.traffic-forwarding.dc-exclusion:restore]

**Tunnels this affects.** The tunnels are described in [IPSec tunnels to ZIA: limits, IKE and device settings](kb:zia.traffic-forwarding.ipsec).
