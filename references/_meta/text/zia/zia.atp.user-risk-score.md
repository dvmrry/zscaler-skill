# User Risk Score: its static and real-time parts, the indicators and variables the page names, and where it is used (zia.atp.user-risk-score)
Also called: user risk score; risk score; static risk score; real-time risk score; risky user

The User Risk Score is a per-user value that Zscaler builds from the organization's user behavior trends. Zscaler analyzes the organization's user behavior trends and builds a risk profile dynamically by assigning a User Risk Score to each user. [zia.atp.user-risk-score:profile-built] The computation happens in Internet & SaaS, using a proprietary algorithm, for each user authenticated to Internet & SaaS, and the score is then propagated to Private Access, where it can be used unchanged or overridden for individual users. [zia.atp.user-risk-score:computed-in-zia]

**The two parts.** The score has a static baseline built from the user's behavior over the past 7 days and updated every 24 hours, and a real-time score that updates the baseline every two minutes throughout the day, changing whenever the user interacts with known or suspected malicious content, and is reset to the static baseline at a 24-hour window. [zia.atp.user-risk-score:static-and-realtime] Internet & SaaS provides an option to reset the User Risk Score to Low for individual users. [zia.atp.user-risk-score:reset-to-low]

**What feeds it.** Zscaler considers over 65 indicators of risky behavior and classifies them into three categories: post-infection behavior (past victimization by cyberattacks), pre-infection behavior (near-miss malicious infections) and suspicious behavior (behavior that could lead to a breach). [zia.atp.user-risk-score:indicators-categories] The page's examples of post-infection indicators are blocked botnet traffic and blocked command-and-control traffic attempted after a user was infected. [zia.atp.user-risk-score:post-infection-examples] The page's examples of pre-infection indicators include malware blocked by ATP or inline Sandbox, blocked known or suspected malicious URLs, blocked phishing sites, blocked pages with known browser exploits, blocked adware and spyware, blocked pages with a high Page Risk Index score, quarantined pages, blocked files with known vulnerabilities, blocked emails containing viruses and detected mobile app vulnerabilities. [zia.atp.user-risk-score:pre-infection-examples] Among the page's suspicious-behavior indicators are URLs placed on the denylist, DLP compliance violations, anonymizing sites, attempts to browse blocked URL categories, tunnel blocks, fake proxy authentication, IPS blocks of crypto mining and blockchain traffic, and disallowed use of a DNS-over-HTTPS site. [zia.atp.user-risk-score:suspicious-examples] The policy match information in the log data is the basis for identifying whether a user has engaged in a risky behavior, and is then used in the score computation. The page's example is that a blocked malicious URL (suspicious behavior) poses less of a threat of a breach than an active malware infection (post-infection behavior). [zia.atp.user-risk-score:log-basis] The computation begins when log data is collected and aggregated by user, and scaling factors are used where necessary to ensure a more accurate risk assessment for the users. The page's examples of the variables, which it says are not a complete list, are the category the indicator belongs to, the severity of the threat, and the frequency of occurrence and length of infection. [zia.atp.user-risk-score:scaling-factors] Which indicators exist in full and what they weigh is open [gap:zia.atp.user-risk-score:indicator-catalog].

**Where it is used.** Defining the User Risk Score as a policy criterion lets an admin configure the Access Policy in Private Access and the URL Filtering, Cloud App Control and Data Loss Prevention policies in Internet & SaaS. [zia.atp.user-risk-score:policy-criterion] The Risk Score Report is at Analytics > Risk360 in the Admin Console, and its Top Risky Users widget drills into a user's current score and trend, the events that influenced the score in chronological order, DLP violations, unsanctioned cloud applications accessed and risky URLs the user attempted. [zia.atp.user-risk-score:risk360-report] The risk scores assigned to users influence the organization's overall risk score, and the Company Risk Score Report shows how scores are distributed across users and locations and compares the distribution with peer organizations. [zia.atp.user-risk-score:company-score] The page says the User Risk Score is used across the Zscaler platform, driving policies for Internet & SaaS (ZIA) and Private Access (ZPA) and feeding data into Risk360. [zia.atp.user-risk-score:platform-use] The bands are not on this page [gap:zia.atp.user-risk-score:score-bands], and the report-side computation question is partly open [gap:zia.atp.user-risk-score:realtime-setting]. Report widgets are in [Risk and posture scoring reports: Company Risk Score, User Risk, Configuration Risk, Security Policy Audit and the Attack Surface Report](kb:zia.reports.risk) and the Private Access side is in [User risk scores in Private Access](kb:zpa.authentication.user-risk-scores).
