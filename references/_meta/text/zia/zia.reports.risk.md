# Risk and posture scoring reports: Company Risk Score, User Risk, Configuration Risk, Security Policy Audit and the Attack Surface Report (zia.reports.risk)
Also called: company risk score; user risk report; configuration risk report; security policy audit report; attack surface report

Three score scales appear in these reports and stay separate: the Company Risk Score and User Risk Report scale, the Configuration Risk Report scale, and the User Risk Profile levels of URL Filtering in [How URL Filtering rule criteria combine](kb:zia.url-filtering.criteria). Risk360 has its own score: [How Risk360 scores risk: the organization score, factors, asset-level risk and Monte Carlo financial estimates](kb:risk360.scoring).

**Company Risk Score.** Risk scores are calculated for each user, location and company. Each user's behavior is analyzed against pre-infection, post-infection and suspicious behavior; risky behavior affects the score for up to 7 days, different factors bear different weights, and unauthenticated users are excluded from calculations. [zia.reports.risk:company-risk-what] Risk scores are calculated only for organizations with Advanced Threat Protection, which is available in the Internet & SaaS Business bundle and higher or sold separately. [zia.reports.risk:company-risk-needs-atp] Risk scores are calculated on a 7-day moving window of risk events. If there are not at least 100 authenticated users in the organization, each with at least one transaction within that window, the score is not computed for that day. The score is also not calculated for a duration when the total percentile of risky users is less than 1 percentile of the total risky users. [zia.reports.risk:company-risk-threshold] When there is insufficient user activity in a given day, the company risk score is recorded as 0, and that daily 0 is included in the running company risk score calculation, reducing the score. [zia.reports.risk:company-risk-zero-days] The Current Risk Score widget shows the organization's score, its industry vertical's average and the average of all organizations, in bands Low (0–25), Medium (26–50), High (51–75) and Critical (76–100). [zia.reports.risk:company-risk-bands] The contributing events are Active Infections (transactions suspected to be botnet callbacks), Suspicious Activity (potentially malicious transactions) and Threats Blocked (transactions blocked as more likely to result in an infection); the Risk Score Trend graph spans 7, 15 and 30 days. [zia.reports.risk:company-risk-events] The score is computed from authenticated user traffic, so locations with a high number of unauthenticated transactions cannot have a score computed and are listed separately; the trend and that section are not computed when there is a low number of authenticated users. [zia.reports.risk:company-risk-unauth-locations] Per-factor weights and the meaning of the 1 percentile rule are open [gap:zia.reports.risk:company-risk-weights].

**User Risk Report.** The User Risk Report's risk score is generated based on DLP violations, risky user behavior, unsanctioned cloud application usage and other suspicious factors. [zia.reports.risk:user-risk-inputs] Recent increases in the risk score can be viewed only when Real-Time Risk Score Updates is enabled in the Advanced Settings. [zia.reports.risk:user-risk-realtime] The User Risk Report's Risk Score Trend graph shows the trend for the user, company and cloud and can be filtered for the last 7, 15 or 30 days. It also shows DLP violations by DLP engine, the user's unsanctioned cloud applications, and top risky URL categories identified by the Zscaler research team. [zia.reports.risk:user-risk-sections] Its bands differ from the URL Filtering levels [divergence:zia.reports.risk:user-risk-bands], and the existing claim is [The console's User Risk Profile criterion selects user risk score levels (by default Low 0–29, Medium 30–59, High 60–79, Critical 80–100). The API carries it as `userRiskScoreLevels`.](kb:zia.url-filtering.criteria:user-risk-score-levels). How the score is computed is open [gap:zia.reports.risk:user-risk-computation].

**Configuration Risk Report.** The Configuration Risk Report evaluates the organization's policy configuration, traffic patterns and feature capabilities against Zscaler's best practices to calculate the risk of a breach, in four categories: Web-Based Threats, File-Based Threats, Network-Based Threats and Uninspected Encrypted Traffic. [zia.reports.risk:config-risk-what] The page lists these contributing factors: Advanced Threat Protection, Browser Control Settings, Mobile Malware Protection, Sandbox, File Type Control, IPS Control Settings, Firewall Traffic Analysis, Firewall Filtering Rule, IPS Traffic Analysis, FTP Settings and SSL/TLS Inspected. [zia.reports.risk:config-risk-factors] The Configuration Risk Report's bands are Low (0–29), Medium (30–59), High (60–79) and Critical (80–100); the trend shows the last 30 days by default, or weekly, monthly or quarterly. [zia.reports.risk:config-risk-bands] Each setting shows a status of Protected, Moderately Protected, Mildly Protected or Not Protected based on its risk contribution; the status and the risk contribution are computed independently, so the same risk contribution for two settings might generate similar or different statuses. [zia.reports.risk:config-risk-status]

**Security Policy Audit Report.** The Security Policy Audit Report's possible grades are A, B, C or N/A, with C being the lowest. If the organization has no subscription for an area (for example Sandbox), non-compliance in that area does not affect the grade. [zia.reports.risk:audit-grades] Not following the best practice for the settings in the page's C table gives a grade of C, including Inspect Inbound Traffic, Inspect Outbound Traffic and Inspect HTTP enabled, and Sandbox blocking known malicious files of any file type from any URL category with First Time Action Allow and Scan for all other combinations. Not following the best practice for all other areas gives a B. [zia.reports.risk:audit-c-table] The Advanced Threats rows of that table are the ATP settings in [The recommended policy sets Page Risk to 35; in Botnet Protection it blocks Command & Control Servers, Command & Control Traffic and Domain Generation Algorithm (DGA) Domains and enables Alert for Unknown/Suspicious C2 Traffic; it blocks everything in Malicious Active Content, Fraud, Unauthorized Communication and XSS protection, and blocks BitTorrent, Tor and Google Hangouts.](kb:zia.atp:recommended-policy). The report has three main sections, Traffic Inspection, Threat Protection and Security Exceptions. Its checks include the percentage of encrypted traffic inspected, the Revoked Server and Untrusted SSL Server certificate settings, inbound and outbound traffic, protocol inspection, malware, advanced threats, Browser Control, and Sandbox policies 1 through 4; the Browser Vulnerability Protection section is only applicable with browser-based authentication. [zia.reports.risk:audit-sections] How often it recomputes is open [gap:zia.reports.risk:recompute-cadence].

**Attack Surface Report.** Whether the report is still reachable in the console is documented two ways [divergence:zia.reports.risk:attack-surface-access]. The About page, which says the report is no longer accessible from the Admin Console, lists the Attack Surface Report's sections as Known Vulnerabilities (a Vulnerabilities column of CVE IDs for servers), SSL/TLS Risks (servers supporting older SSL/TLS versions), Exposed Servers, Namespace Exposure and Public Cloud Instances; reports download as PDF, PPT or CSV for the last 12 months. [zia.reports.risk:attack-surface-sections] The notification page, for a report the About page says is no longer accessible from the Admin Console, says the Attack Surface Report notification keeps administrators posted on the latest available report for download by sending an email notification to the configured email address. [zia.reports.risk:attack-surface-notify] How often it is generated is open [gap:zia.reports.risk:attack-surface-cadence].

What to check:
1. Whether the organization has Advanced Threat Protection, since risk scores are calculated only for those that do.
2. Whether a missing company score is an insufficient-activity day, and whether a 0 day lowered the running score.
3. Which scale a user risk band is quoted on before comparing it with a URL Filtering risk level, since the pages differ.
4. Whether the Attack Surface Report can be opened in the console at all, before sending an administrator to it.

Sources disagree (name both sides):
- The User Risk Report bands and the URL Filtering user risk score levels give different ranges for the same user risk score. [divergence:zia.reports.risk:user-risk-bands]
  - zscaler-help: The User Risk Report bands are Low (0-25) and Critical (76-100), with no condition stated.
  - zscaler-help: By default, the URL Filtering user risk score levels are Low (0 to 29) and Critical (80 to 100).
  Impact: A score of 28 is inside the URL Filtering Low level (0 to 29) but outside the User Risk Report's Low band (0-25); the URL Filtering page calls its levels the default ones. The Configuration Risk Report bands (0-29 to 80-100) are a different score and are not evidence for either.
- The Attack Surface Report About page says the report is no longer accessible from the Admin Console, while the same page and the notification page still describe the console page. [divergence:zia.reports.risk:attack-surface-access]
  - zscaler-help: The report is no longer accessible from the Admin Console; it can be requested once from the Zscaler Account team, and regular access needs Risk360.
  - zscaler-help: The same page still describes an Attack Surface Report page at Analytics > Reports > Attack Surface Report.
  - zscaler-help: The notification page still sends the administrator to Analytics > Reports > Attack Surface Report.
  Impact: Whether the console page and its notification setting still work, or only the Risk360 route and a one-time request remain, is not settled by the pages; the sections and download formats described are for a report the first position says is retired from the console.
