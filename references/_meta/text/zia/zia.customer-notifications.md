# Zscaler customer notifications: Trust Portal subscriptions, Admin Console contacts and the Service Continuity notification protocol (zia.customer-notifications)
Also called: customer notifications; trust portal subscriptions; service continuity notification protocol; maintenance notice; upgrade notification lead time

The Zscaler Trust Portal at https://trust.zscaler.com shows cloud operation status, upcoming maintenance and security updates, with access to 90 days of historical data. [zia.customer-notifications:trust-portal] The Trust Portal subscription service gives granular control over which data centers and services updates are received for, and a mailing list can be added; a group email entered under Group Settings receives the same subscriptions as the account holder. [zia.customer-notifications:trust-subscription-scope] On the Trust Portal Subscriptions page, subscriptions are managed per cloud: Scheduled and Completed maintenance, In Progress and Resolved advisory notifications, core cloud services (all or selected) and data centers (all or selected). [zia.customer-notifications:trust-subscriptions-page]

**Admin Console contacts.** Zscaler sends Security Updates, Service Updates and Product Updates to the Technical and Business contacts on the Organization page (Administration > Administration > Organization > Contacts). [zia.customer-notifications:admin-console-contacts] A group email address can be entered as a notification contact to reach a whole team, and an admin can be subscribed to these updates when the admin is added or edited. [zia.customer-notifications:contacts-group-email]

**Notification protocol.** In the Service Continuity Customer Notification Protocol table, a Service Upgrade (typically every 3-6 months) is posted on Trust at least 24 hours ahead and emailed at least 7 days ahead; an Infrastructure Upgrade (monthly, customer-visible data center and IP address additions or removals) is posted at least 24 hours ahead and emailed at least 60 days ahead; Secrets Management (quarterly SAML Certificate Update) is posted and emailed at least 2 weeks ahead. [zia.customer-notifications:protocol-upgrades] In the same table, Security Update (continuous, real-time threat updates) has no Trust notice and no email; Service Update (weekly) follows the standard published maintenance window with no email; Infrastructure Update (weekly, no customer-visible IP address addition or removal) is posted on Trust at least 24 hours ahead with no email; a Service Incident is posted on Trust during the incident period with no email. [zia.customer-notifications:protocol-updates-incidents] Zscaler's Service Continuity Policy governs how and when changes are made to its services, and the End-of-Sale (EOS) and End-of-Life (EOL) Policy is separate from it. [zia.customer-notifications:policy-scope] The EOS and EOL policy itself is in [Zscaler EOS and EOL Policy: SaaS, software and hardware-refresh milestones](kb:portfolio.usage-terms.eos-eol-policy).
