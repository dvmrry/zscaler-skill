# 3rd-Party App Governance policies and notification channels, including the NSS channel (zia.app-governance.policies)

Policies set and perform automatic actions such as classifying an app as sanctioned or unsanctioned, or taking automated action such as revoke, ban or review, and set notifications for new applications added to the inventory that match a rule (view). [zia.app-governance.policies:what-a-policy-does] A policy's condition is a custom view, so the policy triggers every new app in that view, and only one custom view is supported per policy. Adding the App Status filter when creating a custom view for a policy makes the policy trigger again if an app is reinstalled. [zia.app-governance.policies:condition-is-a-view] The portal is in [3rd-Party App Governance: governing third-party apps, add-ons and extensions connected to SaaS platforms, and its portal, roles and dashboard](kb:zia.app-governance), and custom views are in [App and user inventory in 3rd-Party App Governance: how apps get in, search, catalog sandboxing, bulk upload, custom views, client-ID exports](kb:zia.app-governance.inventory).

**Responses.** A policy's response for new apps matching it can set a Classification value and an Automated workflow. The page also lists End User Review (through Slack or email, where a personalized message can also be added) and Revoke/Ban options. It can also set how findings not resolved on those apps are marked, and the policy can be enabled or disabled. [zia.app-governance.policies:responses] Revoke, ban and review are in [Revoke, ban and end user review in 3rd-Party App Governance: what each does and where it is recorded](kb:zia.app-governance.remediation). On the Policies page, attributes such as Notify Via, Classification, Automated Workflow and Enabled/Disabled can be edited inline, and changes are saved immediately. [zia.app-governance.policies:inline-edit]

**Out-of-the-box policies.** 3rd-Party App Governance supports out-of-the-box policies such as new risky apps, app risk increased and new high/medium severity findings. They are marked Zscaler in the Author column, and not all of their attributes can be modified: the condition cannot be changed. [zia.app-governance.policies:out-of-the-box-policies] The policies page lists best practice policies as views, each with a view link and a Suggested Policy: apps with mail access, Reviewing; high-risk apps, Reviewing > Unsanctioned; GDPR violation, Unsanctioned; open source apps, Reviewing; gaming apps by category, Unsanctioned. [zia.app-governance.policies:example-views]

**Alerts.** Each time a policy is triggered, its alert is sent via one of Slack, Email, In-app, Zscaler Workflow Automation or Zscaler Nanolog Streaming Service. The Nanolog Streaming Service option sends 3rd-party app logs to NSS, and the page says NSS and Cloud NSS feeds must be configured for 3rd-party app logs to stream log data to a SIEM. [zia.app-governance.policies:alert-channels] The log type is [ZIA 3rd-Party App Governance log (NSS 3rd-Party App Governance feed)](kb:zia/3rd-party-app-governance-log), and the feed subscription requirement is [Access to 3rd-Party App Governance and Posture Management logs requires Advanced SaaS Security Posture Management (SSPM), available from the Zscaler Account team; their Log Type values are App Governance and Posture.](kb:shared.nss.feeds:sspm-subscription). What the Zscaler Workflow Automation channel carries is open [gap:zia.app-governance.policies:workflow-automation-channel].

**Outbound integrations.** The Slack outbound integration populates a list of public Slack channels, and a private channel can be selected only after 3rd-Party App Governance is added to it. [zia.app-governance.policies:outbound-slack] The email outbound integration takes one or more Policy Email addresses from which a notification is sent when a policy is triggered, a Revocation Email address from which users are notified when a previously accessible app is revoked or banned, and an access token created through SendGrid. [zia.app-governance.policies:outbound-email]
