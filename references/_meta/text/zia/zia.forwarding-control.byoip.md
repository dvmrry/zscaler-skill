# Customer-owned Dedicated IP addresses (BYOIP) (zia.forwarding-control.byoip)
Also called: byoip; bring your own ip; dedicated ip roa; customer-owned ip zscaler; AS53813 AS22616 AS62044

A customer who wants their own addresses as the egress address may mean this or Source IP Anchoring. Zscaler's dedicated IP service provides dedicated IP addresses and also lets you bring your own IP address range into the Zscaler cloud, which is called BYOIP. [zia.forwarding-control.byoip:definition] BYOIP needs at least one /24 for each Zscaler data center where addresses are to be provisioned, a subscription to the dedicated IP service, a prefix registered with the Regional Internet Registry and associated with an ASN, and verification by a cryptographic Route Origin Authorization (ROA). [zia.forwarding-control.byoip:prerequisites]

**Provisioning.** The ROA names a Zscaler ASN by region: AS53813 for APAC, AS22616 for the Americas and AS62044 for EMEA; the ROA must be for the same prefix length as the prefix announced from the data center. [zia.forwarding-control.byoip:roa-asns] The customer attaches a self-signed X.509 public certificate to the prefix's RIR record (comments for ARIN, remarks for RIPE and APNIC), then signs a message in the form <version>|<prefix>|<mesg_expire_date> with SHA-256 and RSA-PSS. Zscaler recommends an expiry at least one month after creating the message. [zia.forwarding-control.byoip:signing] Provisioning is requested through a Zscaler Support ticket naming the data centers and carrying the message and its signature; Support validates the routes, advertises them from the requested data centers and provisions addresses from the prefix to the tenant. [zia.forwarding-control.byoip:support-ticket]

**Not Source IP Anchoring.** BYOIP and Source IP Anchoring are different mechanisms: BYOIP brings a customer-owned, ROA-authorized prefix into the Dedicated IP service, while Source IP Anchoring makes the Private Access App Connector's address the source. Licensing and mechanism assumptions for one do not carry to the other. [zia.forwarding-control.byoip:not-sipa] The anchoring mechanism is in [Source IP Anchoring: what it needs on each side](kb:zia.forwarding-control.sipa); a rule uses Dedicated IP addresses through the Dedicated IP method and gateway: [Forwarding methods and their required fields](kb:zia.forwarding-control.methods).
