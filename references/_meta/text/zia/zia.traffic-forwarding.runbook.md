# How the Internet & SaaS traffic forwarding troubleshooting runbook triages a case and routes it to its pages (zia.traffic-forwarding.runbook)
Also called: Internet & SaaS Traffic Forwarding Support Troubleshooting Runbook; traffic forwarding troubleshooting runbook

**Sections.** The Internet & SaaS forwarding runbook has sections for PAC files and Zscaler Client Connector (endpoint-based forwarding), GRE tunnels and IPSec tunnels. [zia.traffic-forwarding.runbook:sections] PAC file and explicit proxy diagnosis is in [Runbook: PAC file, explicit proxy and DPP outages, PAC causes and authentication loops](kb:zia.traffic-forwarding.runbook.pac), GRE tunnels are in [Runbook: GRE tunnel hard down, flapping and up-but-no-traffic](kb:zia.traffic-forwarding.runbook.gre), IPSec tunnels are in [Runbook: IPSec tunnel hard down, Tunnel Insights status and event values, flapping and up-but-no-traffic](kb:zia.traffic-forwarding.runbook.ipsec), and MTR collection, WinMTR for Client Connector and PAC, and latency checks for GRE and IPSec gateways are in [Runbook: MTR collection, WinMTR for Client Connector and PAC, and latency checks for GRE and IPSec gateways](kb:zia.traffic-forwarding.runbook.tunnel-path).

**Verifying the method.** To verify that a location's traffic arrives over a GRE tunnel, note the tunnel's public egress IP address with ip.zscaler.com, then find a matching Web Insights transaction from the affected location where Traffic Forwarding is Zscaler Client Connector over GRE Tunnel or PAC File over GRE Tunnel and Client External IP is that egress IP. [zia.traffic-forwarding.runbook:verify-web-insights] From ip.zscaler.com, Connection Quality or speedtest.zscaler.com shows the traffic forwarding method below the World Map as Connected via <traffic forwarding method>. [zia.traffic-forwarding.runbook:verify-speedtest]

**Full outage and Client Connector errors.** For a complete internet outage (all destinations), the runbook says to check the Zscaler Trust Portal for open cloud incidents for the affected domain or data center; if an active incident post is found, the potential workaround is to fail over to another Zscaler data center (PAC modification or tunnel failover). [zia.traffic-forwarding.runbook:full-outage-incidents] If Zscaler Client Connector shows a specific error message, the Internet & SaaS runbook sends the reader to the Client Connector forwarding runbook for the errors. [zia.traffic-forwarding.runbook:zcc-error-redirect] Those errors are in [Client Connector forwarding runbook: the five errors and where each is worked (captive portal and driver error held here)](kb:zcc.troubleshooting.forwarding-runbook).
