# Executive Insights, company summary (CIO, CSO), QBR, Industry Peer Comparison and CIPA Compliance reports (zia.reports.executive)
Also called: executive insights report; CIO report; CSO report; company summary report; QBR; quarterly business review; industry peer comparison; CIPA compliance report

The Executive Insights Report provides an organization's key contacts with a monthly overview of the traffic volume and security posture of their organization. [zia.reports.executive:executive-insights-monthly] There are two sets of recipients: the first receives the report on the first weekend, when it is generated, and the second 7 days after the first. To add a recipient, contact the Zscaler Account team and name the set. [zia.reports.executive:executive-insights-two-sets] Its widgets include a 6-month overall traffic trend, a Transactions Blocked Average Comparison of three averages, including a peer average and a cloud average, the top 5 locations by bandwidth, a 6-month trend of threats blocked and policy violations, and the top Microsoft 365 apps in the last month. [zia.reports.executive:executive-insights-widgets]

**Company summary reports.** The Company Summary Report for Chief Information Officers (CIO Report) is an interactive report whose sections include Peak Transactions, Top Blocked URL Categories and Mobile Traffic by Device Type. The page says a chart can open its logs with View Logs or open in Web Insights with Analyze Chart, and the report can be given notes, favorited, copied, scheduled and printed. [zia.reports.executive:cio-report] The Company Summary Report for Chief Security Officers (CSO Report) is an interactive report whose sections include Overall Threat Posture, Top Blocked Threat Super Categories for Road Warriors, DLP Engine Violations, DLP Dictionary Violations, Top Categories for Liability Exposure Allowed, Bandwidth Loss Trend and Top Users Infected by Threats. [zia.reports.executive:cso-report]

**Quarterly Business Review.** A new QBR is generated on the first weekend of every month, or on the following weekend if the first weekend falls on the first or second day of the month. QBRs are stored as PowerPoint files in the Zscaler cloud, up to 15 reports. [zia.reports.executive:qbr-generation] A QBR that is not visible is enabled through the support team or a Zscaler Support ticket, and including or excluding remote user traffic goes through the Zscaler Account team. [zia.reports.executive:qbr-enable] The QBR's growth section covers transaction count, bandwidth per user, threats stopped and policy blocks for the current, previous and last year's same quarter, and peers are defined by analyzing industry, geography and company size. [zia.reports.executive:qbr-peers] In the QBR's SSL/TLS section, qualified SSL/TLS refers to SSL/TLS traffic that excludes UCaaS traffic and, when O365 is enabled, O365 traffic. [zia.reports.executive:qbr-qualified-ssl] QBR sections include a 12-month internet-bound traffic growth graph, Protection Against Advanced Threats, Threats and Locations by Botnet Callbacks, real blocked phishing examples, Office 365 Growth, a Standard or Advanced Sandbox section, and Zscaler Support incidents by priority over the last 90 days. [zia.reports.executive:qbr-sections] In the QBR Sandbox section, Files required Sandbox is the total number of files sent to the Sandbox for analysis (known files with a unique MD5 and a verdict, or unknown files submitted for offline analysis), and Files unique to your org is the total number of files the organization submitted for offline analysis. [zia.reports.executive:qbr-sandbox-terms] The ZDX product has its own QBR: [Console reports: predefined reports, Data Explorer views and Quarterly Business Review reports](kb:zdx.reports.console). Whether the two are one report is open [gap:zia.reports.executive:zia-zdx-qbr].

**Peer comparison.** The Industry Peer Comparison Report compares the organization's performance and effectiveness for the preceding month to that of peer organizations and all companies using the cloud service. Peers are chosen based on business vertical, geographic region and business size, and accessing the feature gives Zscaler the right to make the comparison. [zia.reports.executive:peer-comparison]

**CIPA Compliance.** The CIPA Compliance Report provides information on the top URL categories blocked from the Legal Liability class, and the top users and domains blocked from accessing obscene or harmful material. To display it within Interactive Reports, CIPA Compliance must be enabled within Advanced Policy Settings. [zia.reports.executive:cipa-report] The CIPA Compliance Report cannot be copied and shows data only for the last 7 days, so the Monthly option is grayed out when scheduling it. [zia.reports.executive:cipa-7-days] Its widgets include Top URL Categories Blocked from Legal Liability, top blocked users per category (for example Adult Materials and Newly Registered and Observed Domains) and Top Domains Blocked. [zia.reports.executive:cipa-widgets] The setting it needs is constrained in [Advanced URL Filtering settings that change outcomes](kb:zia.url-filtering.advanced-settings).
