# Installing ZT400, ZT600 and ZT800 devices: mounting, console, cabling, port maps and reset (cloud-connector.sdwan.hardware-install)
Also called: zt400 install; zt600 install; zt800 install; zero trust sd-wan device rack mount; zero trust sd-wan device console settings; zt800 wall mount

The Zero Trust SD-WAN Device 400 and 600 mount on rack posts using the rack mount kit; the 800 can be mounted on the wall using the wall mount kit, which is available only by request. [cloud-connector.sdwan.hardware-install:mounting] The package contents lists for the Zero Trust SD-WAN Device 400, 600 and 800 each include 19-inch mounting brackets. [cloud-connector.sdwan.hardware-install:package-brackets] The Zero Trust SD-WAN Device requires an RJ45 interface for the console, and a DB9 serial connection might also need a serial adapter; the serial connection is configured with baud rate 115200, 8 data bits, 1 stop bit, no parity and XON/XOFF flow control. [cloud-connector.sdwan.hardware-install:console-serial]

**Cabling.** The installation page lists CAT6 Ethernet cables among items not included but required. For non-gateway (one-arm) installation the ZT400 and ZT600 sections each list a minimum of 2 (3 when deploying with App Connector) and the ZT800 section a minimum of 4 (5 when deploying with App Connector). For gateway installation the ZT400 section lists a maximum of 4, the ZT600 section a maximum of 6, and the ZT800 section a minimum of 3 and a maximum of 6. In gateway mode App Connector does not require its own cable. [cloud-connector.sdwan.hardware-install:cables] In non-gateway (one-arm) installation of the ZT400 the OS/Management Port is the port labeled 1 (also on the ZT400 with App Connector); the Zscaler Service Port, which hosts the IP address of a Zero Trust SD-WAN Device, is the port labeled 2 on the ZT400 and 3 on the ZT400 with App Connector; and the optional App Connector Port is the port labeled 2 on the ZT400 with App Connector. [cloud-connector.sdwan.hardware-install:one-arm-ports-zt400] In non-gateway (one-arm) installation of the ZT600 the OS/Management Port is the port labeled 1 (also on the ZT600 with App Connector); the Zscaler Service Port, which hosts the IP address of a Zero Trust SD-WAN Device, is the port labeled 2 on the ZT600 and 3 on the ZT600 with App Connector; and the optional App Connector Port is the port labeled 2 on the ZT600 with App Connector. [cloud-connector.sdwan.hardware-install:one-arm-ports-zt600] In non-gateway (one-arm) installation of the ZT800 the OS/Management Port is the port labeled 3; the Zscaler service ports are the ports labeled 5 and 6 on the ZT800 and 6 and 7 on the ZT800 with App Connector; the Load Balancer Port, used to load balance traffic across multiple Branch Connectors, is the port labeled 4 on the ZT800 and 5 on the ZT800 with App Connector; and the optional App Connector Port is the port labeled 4 on the ZT800 with App Connector. [cloud-connector.sdwan.hardware-install:one-arm-ports-zt800] The ZT800 service ports are labeled with a different speed from the other ports [gap:cloud-connector.sdwan.hardware-install:zt800-port-labels].

**Gateway mode ports.** In gateway installation the ZT400 uses GE1 OS/Management Port 1 for device management and port 4 as the default WAN port (ports 2-3 can also be used as a WAN port); the ZT600 uses port 6 as the default WAN port (ports 2-5 can also be used); and the ZT800 uses GE3 OS/Management Port 3 for management, port 8 as the default WAN port (ports 4-7 can also be used) and LAN Ports 5, 6 and 7. On the ZT400 the WAN port 4 receives its IP address from the DHCP client by default to support Zero Touch Provisioning. [cloud-connector.sdwan.hardware-install:gateway-ports] How the ZT800 resolves its overlapping WAN alternates and LAN ports is not stated [gap:cloud-connector.sdwan.hardware-install:zt800-wan-lan-overlap]. The modes are described in [Zero Trust SD-WAN devices: gateway and one-arm modes, zero touch provisioning](kb:cloud-connector.sdwan.device-modes) and the template side of the management interface in [On a hardware template the serial number drop-down shows only devices associated with the organization and for the specified platform type; the management interface provides out-of-band access and is enabled by default, receives its IP address by DHCP by default, and defaults to 192.168.1.1/24 if the DHCP client is enabled and receives no DHCP offer.](kb:cloud-connector.sdwan.configuration-templates:hw-mgmt-interface).

**Power and reset.** When the power button is pressed the LED light on the power button turns red and then turns green; if the device does not turn on, check the adapter connection, and if the unit still does not power on, contact Zscaler Support. Pressing the reset button for 10 seconds turns the system LED green and initiates the reset sequence. [cloud-connector.sdwan.hardware-install:power-and-reset] What the reset sequence resets is not stated [gap:cloud-connector.sdwan.hardware-install:reset-scope].
