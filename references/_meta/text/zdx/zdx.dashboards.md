# ZDX console dashboards and overviews: time ranges, filters, VIP tagging, Private Service Edge Health and Self Service (zdx.dashboards)
Also called: digital experience dashboard; performance dashboard; applications overview; users overview; user details page; vip users; very important person; private service edge health; self service dashboard; self service notifications; zdx time range

The ZDX console shows experience data in dashboards and overviews; the API's report reads are in [Reading ZDX report data: time ranges, paging, users and what the reads return](kb:zdx.reports). This subject covers the Digital Experience dashboard, the Performance Dashboard, the Applications and Users Overviews, the user details page, VIP tagging, the ZIA Private Service Edge Health dashboard and Self Service.

## Digital Experience dashboard

The Digital Experience dashboard gives an overview of the organization's digital experience in terms of user, devices, applications and unified communication experience, as well as overall network activity and latency. Depending on role and limitations, an admin might not see all of its widgets. [zdx.dashboards:de-overview] The Digital Experience dashboard's Time Range filter chooses a range between 2 hours and 48 hours; a custom range must start within the last 14 days, be at least 15 minutes long and be set in 5-minute increments. The selected period applies to all data within the dashboard, and the default is 48 hours. In the subscription table the Query Limit Time Span Selection Range is 2 to 24 hours on Standard and 2 to 48 hours on the other tiers, and Query Limit Data Retention is 2 days on Standard and 14 days on the others. [zdx.dashboards:de-time-range] The Digital Experience dashboard has an overview of the total incidents, impacted users and total active users based on device performance, and widgets providing network anomalies from Last Mile ISPs to the Zero Trust Exchange to applications. It lists score bands Poor (0-33, Red), Okay (34-65, Orange) and Good (66-100, Green), and a Hardware Usage Analysis of impacted devices and their hardware usage from the previous 14 days. [zdx.dashboards:de-widgets]

## Time ranges and their limits

The Performance Dashboard, the Applications Overview and the Users Overview each default to a 2-hour time range and offer Current, which views the most current ZDX Score captured within the previous 30 minutes. The Performance Dashboard's custom range must start within the last 14 days, be at least 15 minutes long and be set in 5-minute increments. The subscription table limits Standard to a 2 to 24 hour selection range and 2 days of data retention. [zdx.dashboards:time-ranges-other] In the subscription table, Query Limit Data Retention is 2 days on Standard and 14 days on Microsoft 365, Advanced and Advanced Plus, and Query Limit Time Span Selection Range is 2 to 24 hours on Standard and 2 to 48 hours on Microsoft 365, Advanced and Advanced Plus. [zdx.dashboards:time-range-limits-by-tier]

## Performance Dashboard

The Performance Dashboard filters by Departments, Zscaler Locations, User Groups, Geolocations, Location Groups, Last Mile ISPs, Operating Systems, and Zscaler Data Center, and each filter allows including or excluding individual options. [zdx.dashboards:perf-filters] The Performance Dashboard preselects the application with the lowest score, and the rest of the page tracks information based on that application. Its ZDX Score graph also tracks the score of the most impacted application, as determined by the applied filters, up to four additional applications can be added for comparison, and the selection is also reflected in the Page Fetch Time graph. [zdx.dashboards:perf-app-selection] The Performance Dashboard's Page Fetch Time graph tracks, in milliseconds, how long the selected application takes to transfer the fetched page to the user during the selected period, with a line for the 95th percentile (P95) indicating that 95 percent of the fetch time is below that amount. [zdx.dashboards:perf-page-fetch-time] The Regions by ZDX Score map takes the geographic locations of all users accessing the selected application and organizes the data for the score down to the city level for major cities around the world; hovering over a marked location shows the city name, the ZDX Score and the number of users. [zdx.dashboards:perf-regions-map] ZDX locates users as described in [ZDX geolocations: active geolocations, geo filters and coordinates](kb:zdx.devices.geolocation).

## Applications Overview and application details

The Applications Overview filters by User Priority, Departments, Zscaler Locations, User Groups, Geolocations, Location Groups, Last Mile ISPs, Operating System, Monitoring Type and Zscaler Data Centers. Disabled applications are shown in gray and deleted applications with a strikethrough on the name, and each application lists its ZDX Score (from 1 to 100, with 1 the lowest), active users, RUM users, and the geolocation, Zscaler location and department most impacted by the application. [zdx.dashboards:apps-overview] Filters selected on the Applications Overview remain in place after selecting an application. The application's probe status gives the minimum, average and maximum of Page Fetch Time, Server Response Time, DNS Time and Availability for the Web probe, and of End-to-End, Packet Loss, Total Hops and Packet Count for the network probe, and the Web Probe Performance tab lists up to the 5 most impacted departments, regions and locations with their Synthetic Probe Score. [zdx.dashboards:app-details]

## Users Overview and user details

By default, no information is displayed in the Users Overview's ZDX Score User Distribution and Users list until an application is selected. The list displays up to 100 users in the Poor, Okay or Good category for the selected period under tabs and loads on the Poor tab, can be downloaded in CSV format for each category, shows names that come from the identity providers configured in Internet & SaaS, and its filters apply only to this page, not to the Probes configuration page. [zdx.dashboards:users-overview-list] The Users Overview's ZDX Score column shows the user's ZDX Score from 1 to 100, with 1 the lowest and 100 the highest, and says it is an average score for the selected time period. The Device Count column lists the total number of devices associated with the user. [zdx.dashboards:users-overview-score] The score article defines a user's score differently, [zdx.score.calculation:user-score-vs-overview](kb:zdx.score.calculation:user-score-vs-overview), and the console gives the scale as 1 to 100 where the score article gives 0 to 100, [zdx.score:scale-range](kb:zdx.score:scale-range).

**User details.** On the user details page, if there is more than one device the first device is automatically selected and stays selected through time range and other filter changes, and the application with the lowest ZDX Score is selected by default. The ZDX Score Over Time graph is not populated if a device is not selected, and the time limit to zoom in is 5 minutes. [zdx.dashboards:user-details-selection] The user details page can display incident data from the Incidents Dashboard in the ZDX Score Over Time graph, based on the timeline of a particular incident, with icons for the incident types Wi-Fi, Last Mile ISP, Zscaler and Application. [zdx.dashboards:user-details-incidents]

## VIP users

A user can be tagged as a Very Important Person (VIP) user to categorize them as a high-impact user, and up to 100 users or 100 user groups can be tagged. On the dashboards and overviews filters menu, VIP can be enabled to filter data for VIP users only, including on the Performance Dashboard, Devices Overview, Incidents Dashboard and Users Overview, and a VIP user is tagged with a star icon in user details. The subscription table lists VIP users as not supported on Standard, Microsoft 365 and Advanced. [zdx.dashboards:vip-tagging] In the subscription table, Very Important Person (VIP) Users are not supported on Standard, Microsoft 365 and Advanced, and supported on Advanced Plus at 100 VIP users and 100 VIP user groups. [zdx.dashboards:vip-tier] Whether User Priority is the VIP tag is open [gap:zdx.dashboards:vip-vs-user-priority].

## ZIA Private Service Edge Health

Internet & SaaS (ZIA) Private Service Edge Health provides a statistical overview of Zscaler Private Service Edges deployed in the organization's data centers, with details of overall traffic, internal latency and transactions within the previous 24 hours to 14 days, as allowed per the ZDX subscription level. It needs an admin role with view permission and is reached under Private Access > Dashboards > Health; the subscription table lists Internet & SaaS PSE Health as supported on Standard, Microsoft 365, Advanced and Advanced Plus. [zdx.dashboards:pse-health] The Private Service Edge Health tiles give metrics from the previous 24-hour period to the current time: the total number of data centers with Private Service Edges, the average amount of data (in bits) processed in the data centers, and the aggregate number of transactions processed per second. [zdx.dashboards:pse-tiles] The Overall Traffic chart displays traffic in 5-minute intervals when 24 Hours or 48 Hours is selected and in 1-hour intervals for 7 Days or 14 Days, and Export CSV captures total throughput for the data centers by date and time. The data center table gives the number of Private Service Edge proxy instances, the average data processed per second, the aggregate transactions per second and the average latency in milliseconds. [zdx.dashboards:pse-traffic] A data center's page carries over the time range selected for Overall Traffic, downloads each chart's metrics in CSV format and offers Report Issue to submit a help ticket. It gives the VIP address, the cluster names for the VIP address, and PSE Internal Latency, the P95 latency in milliseconds calculated for the data point within the selected range. [zdx.dashboards:pse-datacenter] A Private Service Edge instance is identified by a node name extension (for example pchi1-1a1-sme) and belongs to a node cluster grouped by a physical node name (for example 1a). Its row gives the node name of the physical server, the service IP address, the asset tag (a physical ID label) and the Zscaler server model number, and each instance charts its processed traffic, its average P95 latency in milliseconds and its memory usage in gigabytes. [zdx.dashboards:pse-instance]

## Self Service

Self Service can help users identify the root cause of issues related to CPU usage and Wi-Fi access, so they can investigate potential solutions without contacting customer support, and each notification contains a brief diagnosis and recommendation. It needs the required Client Connector and ZDX Module versions, a subscription level that supports Self Service and an admin role configured for it; the subscription table lists Self Service as not supported on Standard, Microsoft 365 and Advanced and supported on Advanced Plus. [zdx.dashboards:self-service-overview] The Self Service Dashboard's time range runs from the previous 2 hours to 14 days and its notification types are Wi-Fi, CPU or Other. User Priority is described as the priority level assigned to users. It counts total notifications sent and notifications by type, users notified, users who found notifications helpful, active users with Self Service enabled and users who disabled their notifications, each with a percentage increase or decrease where stated. [zdx.dashboards:self-service-dashboard] A notification's table entry gives the user's device that launched the notification and Found It Helpful?, which is Yes or No if the user provided feedback and Not available if not. [zdx.dashboards:self-service-table] The user notifications are High CPU (the CPU running at a value for a sustained period of time, listing the top processes affecting performance, with advice to close applications contributing to high CPU usage), Degraded Wi-Fi (caused by a suboptimal band or an access point far from the user's location), Suboptimal Wi-Fi Access Point (advice to connect to a better access point in the SSID network) and Suboptimal Wi-Fi Band (physical mode 802.11n, with advice to connect to the SSID on the 5 GHz band). [zdx.dashboards:self-service-notifications] The values that raise each notification are open [gap:zdx.dashboards:self-service-triggers].

**Self Service settings.** Configuring Self Service settings needs full permission level for Self Service on ZDX and the Zscaler Client Connector Notification Framework enabled on the Notifications page in the Zscaler Admin Console. [zdx.dashboards:self-service-settings-prerequisites] The General Settings of Self Service include and exclude specific users, user groups, locations, location groups, departments and devices, deleted or unknown users cannot be selected for the include and exclude criteria, and the Notification Settings send push notifications via Zscaler Client Connector or enable users to configure notifications. [zdx.dashboards:self-service-settings] How its include and exclude criteria combine is open [gap:zdx.dashboards:self-service-criteria-logic]. Changing Self Service settings needs Organization scope: [Only admins with scope over the entire organization can configure organization-wide alert rules, inventory settings and information; configuring Inventory or Self Service settings, changing the Integration settings (Data Collection, SaaS App Tenants and Webhooks) and changing Authentication settings each need Organization scope.](kb:shared.admin-rbac.zdx:organization-scope-only).

**Other views.** Incidents are in [ZDX incidents: the incident area types, their epicenters, key metrics and detection minimums](kb:zdx.dashboards.incidents), Network Intelligence in [Network Intelligence: ISP and ASN latency baselines, anomalies and severity](kb:zdx.dashboards.network-intelligence), the Wi-Fi Dashboard in [The Wi-Fi Dashboard: access points, SSIDs and their metrics](kb:zdx.dashboards.wifi), the Devices Overview in [The Device Health Score and the hardware and usage profiles of the Devices Overview](kb:zdx.devices.health-score) and the Meetings dashboard in [ZDX call-quality metrics per device and meeting](kb:zdx.devices.call-quality).
