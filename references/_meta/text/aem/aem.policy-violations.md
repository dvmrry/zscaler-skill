# AEM policy violations: the Policy Violations page, views, charts, columns and drawer (aem.policy-violations)
Also called: aem policy violation; policy violations page; coverage policy violations

Each AEM policy violation represents a deviation from established security policies detected on an asset, aggregated with context such as severity, violation origins and affected asset types. [aem.policy-violations:definition]

**Views and filters.** The Policy Violations page's system-saved views with predefined filter selections include Active Violations, Active Critical and High Violations, CMDB Hygiene Policy Violations (generated by the CMDB Hygiene policy) and Coverage Policy Violations (generated by the Tool Coverage policy). [aem.policy-violations:system-views] Policy violations can be filtered by Severity, Policy Name, State or Asset Type, and grouped by fields such as Severity, Asset Type, Policy Name or Assignee. [aem.policy-violations:filters-group]

**Charts and columns.** The Policy Violations Overview charts show violations by severity, by policy name and by asset type, and adjust to the selected view and filters. [aem.policy-violations:charts] With the default Active Violations view, a policy violation shows a Severity Score assigned in the configured policy, First Seen (the date the violation was generated) and Last Seen (the most recent date it was still detected). [aem.policy-violations:columns]

**Actions and drawer.** Bulk actions on selected policy violations are Update (for example resolution SLA or assignment) and Create 3rd party Issue, and the list can be exported as a CSV file. [aem.policy-violations:bulk-actions] The actions available in the policy violation drawer depend on the user role in the AEM app, and the drawer shows detailed information including the affected asset, policy name and severity score. [aem.policy-violations:drawer] The drawer tab contents are open [gap:aem.policy-violations:drawer-tabs]. Policies that generate violations are in [AEM asset compliance policies: permissions, creation, preview, save and run, management actions](kb:aem.policies); violations are aggregated into tickets in [AEM violation tickets: the Violation Tickets page, drawer, split and merge, comments and activity log](kb:aem.violation-tickets).
