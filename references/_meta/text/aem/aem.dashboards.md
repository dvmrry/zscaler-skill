# AEM dashboards: OS End of Life, Violation Tickets, and Tool Coverage and Gap (aem.dashboards)
Also called: os eol dashboard; violation tickets dashboard; tool coverage and gap dashboard; coverage and gap; template gallery

**OS End of Life.** The OS End of Life dashboard is created from a template at Explore > Dashboards > Template Gallery > OS End of Life, with summary tiles, charts and tables on EOL and EOS status. [aem.dashboards:os-eol-access] The OS End of Life dashboard Top Assets tiles are: Total Active Assets in EOL, Assets Reaching EOL in the Next 6 Months, Total Active Assets in EOS and Assets Reaching EOS in the Next 6 Months. [aem.dashboards:os-eol-tiles] The Assets in EOL Overtime and Assets in EOS Overtime charts default to the last three months, and clicking a date and View Items lists the active OS assets at EOL or EOS for that date. [aem.dashboards:os-eol-trend] The OS EOL and OS EOS breakdown-by-OS charts list the top 10 operating systems, and the breakdown-by-owner charts the top 5 owners. [aem.dashboards:os-eol-top-n] The OS EOL and OS EOS breakdown-by-asset-type widgets show OS assets currently at EOL/EOS and nearing the date within the next 6 months by asset type (e.g. Windows workstation, Linux). [aem.dashboards:os-eol-asset-type]

**Violation Tickets.** The Violation Tickets Dashboard is created from a template at Explore > Dashboards > Template Gallery > Violation Tickets Dashboard, and breaks tickets down by severity, time and assignee. [aem.dashboards:vt-template] The Open Violation Tickets By Severity pie chart shows open tickets by severity: Critical (maroon), High (red), Medium (orange) and Low (yellow), with a legend giving each category name, count and percentage of total ticket volume. [aem.dashboards:vt-severity-pie] The Open Violation Tickets Over Time line chart shows the trend of open tickets and Open Violation Tickets Progress Over Time tracks open tickets by severity; clicking a date and View Items lists the open tickets for that date (and severity for the progress chart). [aem.dashboards:vt-trend-charts] The progress chart's severity count is disputed [divergence:aem.dashboards:vt-severity-count]. The Remediation Percentage by Assignee table gives a month-over-month comparison listing the individuals or teams responsible, their open versus closed violation tickets and the rate of ticket closure. [aem.dashboards:vt-remediation-assignee]

**Tool Coverage and Gap.** The Tool Coverage and Gap dashboard is reached in the Agentic Security Operations Platform at Assets > Coverage And Gap and shows asset discovery across integrated asset sources, to identify coverage gaps and overlaps. [aem.dashboards:tc-access-purpose] The Find Coverage Gap widget lists assets not discovered by a selected asset source in a specified time period, taking an Asset source and a Time range entered as a number of days. [aem.dashboards:tc-find-gap] The Explore Coverage Overlaps widget compares asset discovery across up to three asset sources. [aem.dashboards:tc-overlaps] The Asset Coverage table gives for each integrated source its Total Assets, split into a Unique bar (assets discovered only by that source) and an Overlap bar (assets discovered by more than one source). [aem.dashboards:tc-asset-coverage] The full list of asset sources is open [gap:aem.dashboards:asset-source-list].
