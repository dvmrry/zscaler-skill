# Asset Exposure Management (AEM): the asset inventory, coverage gaps and policies, and what can be automated (aem.overview)
Also called: aem; asset exposure management; caasm; cyber asset attack surface management; golden record; asset inventory zscaler; cmdb hygiene; aem api

AEM collects and manages asset data from various sources to track asset inventory and coverage, so that organizations can understand their attack surface, create policies, and track and remediate policy violations. [aem.overview:what-it-is] Zscaler's AEM product page says it gives a high-fidelity "golden record" of all assets, a complete, accurate and context-rich asset inventory, and lists multi-source entity deduplication and golden-record creation by correlating all data. [aem.overview:golden-record]

**What it finds.** The product page lists coverage gap identification, with misconfigurations such as assets lacking EDR, inactive asset recognition, and CMDB health features: identifying assets not registered in the CMDB, and discovering assets not previously known but seen in network traffic. [aem.overview:coverage-and-cmdb] Zscaler's AEM product page says that on average Zscaler reveals 20-30% of assets missing from inventory. [aem.overview:marketing-missing-assets] The product page lists CMDB enrichment with missing assets or elements, triggering built-in or custom access policies for risky assets, automated remediation workflows via ticketing systems, and assigning policy violations to the right owner. [aem.overview:remediation-actions]

**Where its data goes.** The product page's use cases pair accurate vulnerability analysis and risk-based prioritization, from comprehensive asset visibility and context, with a link to UVM, and more accurate attack-surface risk quantification, from a complete understanding of all assets, with a link to Risk360. [aem.overview:informs-risk360-uvm] External attack surface findings merge into it through the platform's EASM integration: [The platform natively integrates External Attack Surface Management, whose scanned assets and findings are merged into AEM and UVM, and integrates with Zscaler Deception and Breach Predictor for Preemptive Detection and Response.](kb:portfolio.secops-platform:easm-and-predr); EASM's own API is in [The EASM (ZEASM) API: organization scoping, clients, paging and the Postman collection](kb:easm.api).

**EASM.** Zscaler integrates EASM natively into the AEM and UVM solutions on the SecOps platform, which the page says gives security teams a single "golden record" of all internet-facing assets. [aem.overview:easm-native] On the SecOps platform at Configure > EASM Settings, access to EASM capabilities requires AEM or UVM or both; EASM functionality varies between them, AEM focusing on asset inventory and policy coverage and UVM on managing the associated vulnerabilities. [aem.overview:easm-aem-vs-uvm] The Enhanced CMDB feature uses the AEM policy framework to identify and close security gaps with discovered assets, including adding missing assets to the CMDB. [aem.overview:easm-cmdb] EASM asset data goes through the Zscaler data fabric's processing, such as harmonizing, deduplication and unification. [aem.overview:easm-data-fabric] The EASM use cases the SecOps platform page lists are Security Posture Management, M&A Diligence, Proactive Brand Protection (lookalike domains and phishing sites) and Continuous Compliance Monitoring. [aem.overview:easm-use-cases] Discovery profiles are in [How EASM discovery profiles work: seeds and their formats, limits, scan cadence, permissions and reports](kb:uvm.easm-discovery); the separately provisioned EASM tenant's own pages are in [Zscaler EASM: what it is, how it is set up, and where each part is documented](kb:easm.overview), whose use cases are [The use cases the page lists for EASM are security posture management, M&A diligence and continuous compliance monitoring.](kb:easm.overview:use-cases).

## Automation

No vendored client covers AEM: the Go and Python SDKs, the Terraform providers, the Ansible collections, the MCP server, the OneAPI Postman collection and the captured Automate contract have no AEM, Asset Exposure Management or CAASM service. [aem.overview:no-client-surface] Report export is the only programmatic path a source shows, through [A saved report can be exported programmatically through the Reporting API, a GraphQL-based interface that exports the data to an AWS S3 bucket or returns a downloadable link; the client authenticates for an access token, submits a request to start the report and polls for its status.](kb:portfolio.secops-platform:reporting-api), and its contract is open: [The SecOps Reporting API's endpoint, token request, GraphQL schema and response shape](kb:portfolio.secops-platform:reporting-api-contract).
