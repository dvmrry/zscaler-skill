# zscaler-skill v0.20.3

A maintenance release. It ships the knowledge-graph refresh of 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-16 (#354): 43 subjects and
2 log types across 13 products follow the help pages Zscaler changed, and 5 new help pages are captured. The
quote-repair job (`scripts/kb-reanchor.mjs`, run by maintainers on refresh pull requests) now holds back the
repairs that were most often wrong in that refresh, and it reads every source the way the citation check does.

## The refresh

The daily refreshes from 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-16 and the weekly pass of 2026-11-16 refetched the help captures
whose pages changed: 341 updated captures in `vendor/zscaler-help/`. The automatic quote repairs were then reviewed
by hand against the refreshed pages.

- **Every citation checks.** The 59 citation errors the automatic repairs left are fixed.
- **Wrong repairs corrected.** Of the 123 automatic repairs, 30 were wrong and are corrected: quotes taken from
  another OS's table or fused across list items, and restated claims the new quotes did not support.
- **Changed text read against its claims.** 86 evidence entries whose page text changed were read against their
  claims, and 4 claims were fixed. All 90 changed Tier A claims were audited; those flagged as overreach were
  narrowed, and two borderline flags remain.
- **ZDX supported versions.** Zscaler rebuilt the page as one table per OS. The claims follow that layout, with
  restated values and new iOS and Android rows.
- **ZIA outbound email policy.** Stop Rule Evaluation is no longer on the page, so it is now an open gap. A new
  divergence covers the exception rule's name.
- **New captures.** Five help pages are captured, and nothing cites them yet: AI Asset Management's single GCP
  project onboarding, inspection of unauthenticated traffic for Secure AI Users, the ZDX metrics page, adding
  Outlook to ZIA data security, and ZPA entitlement orchestration.

No product, subject, log type, workflow or command was added or removed, so this is a patch.

## For tools built on kb-lib

- `scripts/kb-lib.mjs` exports `anchorTextOf(text, ev)`, the text an evidence entry's quotes anchor in. For a page
  capture that is its `captureText`; for any other file, its text as it is; a pointer resolves against the raw
  JSON. Nothing else in kb-lib changed.

## The quote-repair job

These changes are for maintainers. They don't change what the skill answers or how it is loaded.

- **The whole evidence entry.** An entry is repaired whole or not at all. If one of its quotes, or its context
  anchor, still fails after the model's decisions, none of them is applied, and they stay in the report as
  suggestions. On refresh PR #354, the model had replaced a working section anchor with a data row while calling
  the entry's own quote unsupported, and the repair was accepted.
- **Anchors that still work stay.** A context anchor that still works may not be moved, because it is what picks
  the entry's part of the page (an OS's or a model's section). The one exception is a quote that now comes before
  it.
- **One line.** On a page capture, a repaired quote stays on one line (a heading, a list item or a table row)
  when the old quote did. A lead-in may still run into its own list.
- **Restated statements are held for review.** When the model restates a claim, or chooses to for any of its
  quotes, nothing is written to that claim. The draft and its quotes go to the report and the run's `--json`, and
  a reviewer applies them with `--apply`, which skips what the run already wrote.
- **Pinning to an old version is all or nothing.** `old_version` pins an evidence entry only when it covers every
  broken quote in that entry.
- **More sources.** Captures that are not pages, such as the OneAPI Postman collection and Markdown files, are now
  repaired too. Before, the job skipped them without saying so.

Replaying #354's recorded decisions through the new checks (without the model's retry): 14 of the 18 wrong quote
repairs and 12 of the 20 wrong restatements are no longer applied, against 6 of the 79 right repairs. Restatements
are held in any case.
